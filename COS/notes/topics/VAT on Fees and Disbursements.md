# VAT on Fees and Disbursements

**The rule that recurs in every calculation question**: VAT is levied at **14%** (the rate used throughout this archive — note the current statutory rate is higher, but reproduce whatever rate the question specifies) on **fees**, but **not** on **disbursements**.

## Where VAT is added in an allocatur

In every worked [[Allocatur Completion]], VAT is calculated **three times, separately**, once each on:

1. Fees allowed,
2. The drawing fee allowed,
3. The attendance fee allowed.

It is never added to the disbursements line — disbursements pass through the allocatur untaxed for VAT purposes.

Example split, from [[COS 2010-03-06]] Q2:

| VAT component | Amount |
|---|---|
| VAT on fees allowed | R17 640.00 |
| VAT on drawing fee | R1 664.43 |
| VAT on attendance fee | R752.52 |

## Where VAT is added in a statement of account

In a client statement of account (see [[Statement of Account and Bill Preparation]]), VAT is added once, on the **subtotal of fees only**, before disbursements/travel costs (which are added after VAT, VAT-free) and before deducting any deposit already paid. Worked example, from [[COS 2010-03-18 Supp]] Q2:

| Item | Amount |
|---|---|
| Consultation 2½ hrs @ R600/hr | R1 500.00 |
| Waiting 30 min | R300.00 |
| Attending trial 4 hrs | R2 400.00 |
| Travelling time 40 min | R400.00 |
| **14% VAT on fees** | **R644.00** |
| Travelling costs 40km @ R2/km | R80.00 |
| Less: deposit received | (R1 800.00) |
| **Total due** | **R3 524.00** |

Note travelling **costs** (the R2/km disbursement) are added *after* VAT and are not themselves VATed, while travelling **time** billed as a fee item *is* subject to VAT.

## Collection commission and VAT

[[Costs Terminology|Collection commission]] (the fee for collecting a debt in instalments) is expressly stated to be **subject to VAT**.
