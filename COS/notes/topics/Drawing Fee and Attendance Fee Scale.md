# Drawing Fee and Attendance Fee Scale

The percentages that drive every [[Allocatur Completion]] calculation. Two fees, applied at different points in the build-up:

- **Drawing fee** — the fee an attorney is entitled to for *drawing* the bill of costs prior to taxation. Calculated as a percentage of the **fees allowed only** (not disbursements).
- **Attendance fee** — the fee for *attending* the taxation itself. Calculated on **"the bill"** (fees allowed + drawing fee + disbursements, i.e. the running subtotal at that point), in **tiers**: one rate on the first R10 000, a lower rate on the second R10 000, a lower rate again on the balance.

## The rates actually used differ by sitting

This is important — do not assume one universal percentage. The archive shows three different scales in use across the years:

| Sitting | Court | Drawing fee | Attendance fee: 1st R10k / 2nd R10k / balance |
|---|---|---|---|
| [[COS 2010-03-06]] Q2 | High Court | 8.88% | 8.88% / 4.26% / 2.1% |
| [[COS 2010-03-18 Supp]] Q1 | High Court | 8.88% | 8.88% / 4.26% / 2.1% |
| [[COS 2010-09-07 Supp]] Q1 | High Court | 8.88% | 8.88% / 4.26% / 2.1% |
| [[COS 2012-08-30 Supp]] Q1 | High Court | 10.6% | 10.6% / 5.10% / 2.12% |
| [[COS 2012 Special Supp]] Q5 | Magistrate's Court | 5% (flat, not tiered) | 5% (flat, not tiered) |

**Read the question carefully** — if a percentage isn't given in the fact pattern, use the rate implied by the court named (High Court vs Magistrate's Court) and the sitting's own convention; the exam supplies the applicable tariff as an attachment in practice, so a real sitting will not require you to memorise a single "correct" number — use whatever the question/attachment gives you.

## The textbook definitional rate — a discrepancy worth knowing

Two separate marking memos ([[COS 2010-09-07 Supp]] Q4.4 and [[COS 2012-08-30 Supp]] Q4.4) define the drawing fee **conceptually** as:

> "In the Magistrate's Court he is entitled to 5% of the total fees allowed and in the High Court he is entitled to 6.25% of the total fees allowed."

Note this **6.25%** textbook figure does not match the **8.88%** or **10.6%** actually used in the worked High Court allocaturs in those very same papers. This most likely reflects a tariff increase between when the definitional model answer was written and when the tariff attached to the calculation question was last revised. **Lesson: for a "define drawing fee" question, give the 5%/6.25% textbook definition; for a "complete the allocatur" calculation, use whatever percentage the tariff/question attachment actually supplies (or the rate shown in the table above if the paper matches one of these sittings).**

## Worked example (COS — 7 September 2010, Q1, Durban High Court, 8.88% scale)

| Line | Amount |
|---|---|
| Subtotal fees / disbursements | R60 000.00 / R40 000.00 (after taxing off R2 500 / R17 650 from R62 500 / R57 650) |
| Add 8.88% drawing fee | R5 328.00 |
| Subtotal | R65 328.00 |
| Add disbursements | R40 000.00 |
| Subtotal ("the bill") | R105 328.00 |
| Attendance fee 8.88% on 1st R10 000 | R888.00 |
| Attendance fee 4.26% on 2nd R10 000 | R426.00 |
| Attendance fee 2.10% on balance | R1 791.89 |
| Subtotal | R108 433.88 |
| Add 14% VAT on fees allowed | R8 400.00 |
| Add 14% VAT on attendance allowed | R434.82 |
| Add 14% VAT on drawing fee | R745.92 |
| **TOTAL — taxed and allowed** | **R118 014.62** |
