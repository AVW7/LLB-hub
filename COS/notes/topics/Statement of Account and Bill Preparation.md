# Statement of Account and Bill Preparation

Drafting a client statement of account (time-based billing) or a party-and-party bill of costs (tariff-based billing) from a fact pattern — a question type that appears in every sitting, in one of two flavours.

## Flavour 1 — time-based statement of account (own-client billing)

Used for criminal defence matters and other own-client mandates billed hourly. Standard build order:

1. Itemise **every attendance** at the agreed rate — telephone calls, letters, consultation time, waiting time, docket perusal, court attendance, trial days — each item dated/timed and costed individually.
2. **Subtotal** the fees.
3. **Add 14% VAT** on the fees subtotal only. See [[VAT on Fees and Disbursements]].
4. **Add disbursements/travelling costs after VAT** — these are not VATed.
5. **Less: deposit already paid**.
6. **Total due**.

### Worked example (COS — Module 2.1 Practice, Q1, Regional Court, Mr Cosby, R1 500/hour)

| Item | Amount |
|---|---|
| Taking instructions (1 hr) | R1 500.00 |
| Consulting Regional Court Prosecutor (30 min) | R750.00 |
| Perusing docket (4 hrs) | R6 000.00 |
| Attending court, postponed for trial (30 min) | R750.00 |
| Consulting client, preparing for trial (3 hrs) | R4 500.00 |
| Attend court day 1 (7 hrs) | R10 500.00 |
| Attend court day 2 (2 hrs 30 min) | R3 750.00 |
| **Subtotal** | **R27 750.00** |
| Add 14% VAT | R3 885.00 |
| Subtotal | R31 635.00 |
| Less deposit paid | (R3 000.00) |
| **Total due** | **R28 635.00** |

### Recurring fact pattern — "Jay Zee bigamy"

The identical Mr Jay Zee / bigamy / District Court Durban fact pattern (consult 1hr, prosecutor 30min, docket 2hrs, postponement appearance 30min, prep 2hrs, trial day 1 = 7hrs, trial day 2 = 2hrs30, R1 500/hr, R5 000 deposit) appears **twice, near verbatim**, in [[COS 2010-09-07 Supp]] Q3 and [[COS 2012-08-30 Supp]] Q3 — both resolve to Subtotal R23 250.00 → VAT R3 255.00 → R26 505.00 → less deposit R5 000.00 → **Total due R21 505.00**.

## Flavour 2 — tariff-based party and party bill of costs

Used when a tariff is attached to the paper (e.g. the High Court tariff). Draft the bill itemising **only** the entries the facts support — do not manufacture extra entries. Watch for tariff limitation rules, e.g. **only one copy of a document is allowed** even if multiple copies were made.

### Worked example (COS — 6 March 2010, Q4, Mrs Smith divorce)

| Item | Fees |
|---|---|
| Telephone call from client (appointment) | R177.50 |
| Consultation, 1 hour | R710.00 |
| Completing Family Advocate documentation (3 pages) | R213.00 |
| Completing statistics form (1 page) | R71.00 |
| Drafting Summons (2 pages) | R142.00 |
| Drafting Particulars of Claim (4 pages) | R710.00 |
| Copy of Summons (only one copy allowed) | R18.00 |
| Telephone call terminating mandate | R177.50 |

## Flavour 3 — statement with a separate VAT-then-disbursement split (shoplifting/minor's matter)

### Worked example (COS — 2012 Special Supp, Q1, R1 000/hour, VAT-registered)

| Item | Amount |
|---|---|
| Telephone calls (4 @ R60), letters (2 @ R50), consultation (2.5 hrs), preparation (1.5 hrs), court (2 hrs) | Subtotal fees R6 340.00 |
| VAT @ 14% | R887.60 |
| Subtotal | R7 227.60 |
| Travel costs (24km @ R4/km) — added **after** VAT | R96.00 |
| Subtotal | R7 323.60 |
| Less paid on account (deposit) | (R4 000.00) |
| **Total due** | **R3 323.60** |

Confirms the [[VAT on Fees and Disbursements]] rule: VAT is calculated on fees only, and disbursements (travel costs) are added after VAT, unVATed.

## Flavour 4 — non-litigious lump-sum ("all-inclusive fee") statement

A single all-inclusive fee plus 14% VAT, e.g. R25 000 + R3 500 VAT = R28 500 for a lease agreement, or R15 500 + R2 170 VAT = R17 670 for a contract of sale. These lump-sum statements are the ones typically **disputed** by the client and resolved through the Law Society assessment procedure — see [[Litigious vs Non-Litigious Matters]].
