# Provisional Sentence Procedure

A fast-track procedure available only where the claim is founded on a **liquid document**.

## Liquid document — definition

A document evidencing an **unconditional liability for payment of a fixed or easily ascertainable amount of money**, signed by the debtor or his agent (e.g. a cheque, an acknowledgement of debt).

- An **acknowledgement of debt** qualifies as a liquid document — tested in [[HCP 2019-03-07 Assignment]] Q2.2.
- Without a liquid document, provisional sentence is **not competent** — must proceed by ordinary summons instead.

## Procedure

- The **provisional sentence summons** is issued by the Registrar (no separate application needed).
- Sets out the claim and calls on the defendant to appear personally or through a representative to **admit or deny liability**.
- If the defendant wishes to resist, he must, not later than noon on the stipulated day, file an **opposing affidavit** with the registrar (and serve a copy on the plaintiff's attorney) setting out the grounds of the defence.
- Where the defendant files an opposing affidavit, the plaintiff **may** file a replying affidavit.

### Drafting checklist — provisional sentence summons (structure, from the memos)

> INFORM [Defendant] ... that they are hereby called upon immediately to pay [Plaintiff] an amount of R[x] together with interest at [rate]% per annum as from [date], claimed for [a cheque drawn by defendant in favour of plaintiff which was returned marked "refer to drawer" / other liquid document], a copy of which is annexed hereto;
>
> that failing such payment they are hereby called upon to appear before this Court personally or represented by an advocate or an attorney with right of appearance, at [date/time], to admit or deny liability;
>
> that if they deny liability, they shall not later than noon on [date] file an affidavit with the registrar and serve a copy on plaintiff's attorney, setting forth the grounds of their defence and stating whether they admit or deny the claim.
>
> AND INFORM the defendant further that, failing payment and failing to file such an affidavit and appear, provisional sentence may be granted against them with costs and interest.

## Onus of proof

- If the defendant denies the **liquidity** of the document itself (e.g. disputes the authenticity of the signature, or the agent's authority) — onus rests on the **plaintiff**.
- If the defendant relies on an **underlying defence** (a defence on the merits) — onus rests on the **defendant**.

## Effect of the order and what follows

- If provisional sentence is granted and the defendant does not, within the stated period, enter the principal case, the provisional sentence **becomes a final judgment** (commonly cited as within **two months** of the order).
- A **counterclaim** may be raised, but only if it arises out of the **same cause of action** on which the cheque/liquid document claim is founded.
- If provisional sentence is refused (or the defendant satisfies the court), the court may order the defendant to file a plea within a stated time, whereafter ordinary trial-action rules apply *mutatis mutandis*.

## Documents for counsel's brief (opposed provisional sentence)

- Copy of the summons and the documents on which the claim rests.
- Copy of the return of service.
- The original liquid document.
- Copy of the defendant's answering affidavit.
- Copy of the plaintiff's (replying) affidavit.
- Copy of the notice of set down.

## Alternative summonses available for a liquid document

A holder of a dishonoured cheque is not restricted to provisional sentence — a **simple summons** or **combined summons** may also be used.
