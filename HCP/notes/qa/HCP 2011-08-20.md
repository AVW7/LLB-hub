## Q1 — Rule 18(10) and irregular step (15 marks)

> [!question] Question
> 1.1 List Rule 18's specific requirements for a plaintiff suing for bodily injury damages. 1.2 Effect of non-compliance? 1.3 You act for a defendant served with a non-compliant summons — list your steps and time limits. 1.4 Draft the last step in the process (no supporting affidavit needed; own facts).

> [!success]- Answer
> 1.1 (any five): set out so the defendant can reasonably assess quantum; date of birth; nature and extent of injuries; nature/effects/duration of disability; claim separately for medical/hospital/similar expenses; pain and suffering (temporary/permanent); disability re earning of income (earnings lost to date and how made up); enjoyment of amenities of life; disfigurement (full description, temporary/permanent).
> 1.2 A summons not complying with Rule 18 is deemed an irregular step under Rule 30.
> 1.3 Give notice to all parties specifying the irregularity — only if no further step has been taken — within 10 days of becoming aware of the step, affording the opponent an opportunity to remove the cause of complaint.
> 1.4 "BE PLEASED TO TAKE NOTICE that application will be made ... for an order in the following terms: 1. Striking out the Plaintiff's Summons as an irregular proceeding in terms of Rule 30. 2. Costs of this application. 3. Further and/or alternative relief. KINDLY ENROL the matter accordingly."

Related: [[Particulars of Claim and Rule 18 Requirements]], [[Exception, Irregular Proceedings and Striking Out]]

## Q2 — Ex parte application to found jurisdiction by attachment (13 marks)

> [!question] Question
> Client is owed R500,000 by Helmutt Schröder, a peregrinus resident in Berlin. Our courts lack jurisdiction. Schröder is owed R120,000 by ABC (Pty) Ltd, a Pretoria company. You advise proceeding by application. 2.1 What is this proceeding called? 2.2 Long or short form notice of motion? 2.3 On whom is it served? 2.4 Who signs it? 2.5 Draft the founding affidavit (incl. heading and relief).

> [!success]- Answer
> 2.1 An ex parte application to found jurisdiction.
> 2.2 The short form.
> 2.3 It is not served (ex parte).
> 2.4 An attorney.
> 2.5 Must cover: full names/status/address of applicant; full names/status/address of defendant; that defendant is a peregrinus of the RSA; that applicant has a prima facie cause of action against defendant (attach pro forma particulars of claim); that defendant is owed a debt within the court's jurisdiction, due, payable and attachable; that applicant intends instituting action; prayer for an order calling on ABC (Pty) Ltd to show cause why amounts owing to the defendant should not be attached to found jurisdiction, plus alternative relief and costs.

Related: [[Jurisdiction of the High Court]], [[Application Procedure and Notice of Motion]]

## Q3 — Trial preparation: experts, medical exam, photographs (8 marks)

> [!question] Question
> You act for a shopkeeper sued for a slip-and-fall injury. 3.1 What document must plaintiff's attorney file to lead quantum evidence, and when? 3.2 You suspect collusion between the plaintiff and his medical practitioner — what can you do? 3.3 You have photographs of the (since altered) floor — what steps to use them at trial?

> [!success]- Answer
> 3.1 Give notice not later than 15 days before the hearing of intent to call a medical practitioner/expert, and not later than 10 days before the hearing supply a summary of the proposed evidence (the two notices may be combined).
> 3.2 Give notice requiring the plaintiff to submit to a medical examination by a practitioner nominated by the defendant.
> 3.3 Give notice not less than 10 days before the hearing of intention to use the photographs.

Related: [[Discovery]]

## Q4 — Prayer drafting: action for an accounting (4 marks)

> [!question] Question
> Mr Jan de Wet's company engaged a debt collector (Joe Crocks) who collected money but failed to account or pay over. Draft the prayers only.

> [!success]- Answer
> An order that the defendant render a full account for the period [x] to [y], supported by documentary proof; debatement of the account; payment of the amount the court finds due; interest at 15,5% per annum a tempore morae; costs of suit.

Related: [[Particulars of Claim and Rule 18 Requirements]]

## Q5 — Prayer drafting: joint delictual damages (4 marks)

> [!question] Question
> Client sues two defendants, jointly liable for R400,000 delictual damages. Draft the preamble and prayers only.

> [!success]- Answer
> "Plaintiff claims against First and Second Defendant, jointly and severally, the one to pay the other to be absolved, payment of: R400,000; interest at 15,5% per annum a tempore morae; costs; further and alternative relief."

Related: [[Particulars of Claim and Rule 18 Requirements]]

## Q6 — Correct next procedural step, five scenarios (5 marks)

> [!question] Question
> 6.1 Simple summons served, no NID, dies expired. 6.2 Plaintiff failed to plead to your counterclaim, dies expired. 6.3 Client wishes to sue for divorce, spouse resides in England. 6.4 NID entered; instructions are that the claim has prescribed. 6.5 Opposing party disputes your mandate.

> [!success]- Answer
> 6.1 Apply for default judgment. 6.2 Serve a notice of bar. 6.3 Apply for an order authorising edictal citation. 6.4 Serve and file a special plea. 6.5 Lodge a power of attorney.
