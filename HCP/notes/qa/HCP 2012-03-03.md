## Q1 — Days, service and jurisdiction (10 marks)

> [!success]- Answer (no question wording available)
> 1.1 Calendar days. 1.2 Where a defendant resides outside the court's area and more than 150km from the seat of the court. 1.3 Yes, provided plaintiff has not yet obtained default judgment. 1.4 Special plea: "Defendant denies that this Honourable Court has jurisdiction to hear plaintiff's claim for damages as (1) the delict complained of allegedly occurred at [place], outside the area of jurisdiction of the court, and (2) the defendant resides at [address], outside the jurisdiction of the court. WHEREFORE defendant prays that plaintiff's claim be dismissed with costs."

## Q2 — Interpleader (15 marks)

> [!success]- Answer (no question wording available)
> 2.1 Draft an interpleader.
> 2.2 "INTERPLEADER NOTICE — Please take notice that [Applicant] has today paid into court in terms of Rule 58 an amount of R1 million to which First and Second Claimants are making adverse claims. The applicant calls upon the First and Second Claimants to deliver particulars of their claims within 15 days of service of these papers. The applicant will apply to court for a decision as to liability/validity of the claims on a date fixed by the registrar, not being less than 15 days from the date for delivery of claims."
> 2.3 The supporting affidavit must aver: the introductory paragraph; correct citation of applicant, 1st and 2nd claimant; that the court has jurisdiction; a logical exposition of the facts; attachments marked. Required allegations: claimant has no interest in the dispute (bar charges/costs); does not collude with any claimant; is willing to act as the court directs; has paid the money to the registrar.

Related: [[Joinder, Third Party Procedure and Interpleader]]

## Q3 — State organs notice and Rule 18(10) damages (15 marks)

> [!success]- Answer (no question wording available — closely matches [[HCP 2013-09-06 Supp]] Q1)
> 3.1 No legal proceedings may be instituted against an organ of state unless prior notice was given or the organ consented in writing (s3(1)–(2), Act 40 of 2002); notice within 6 months of the debt arising; notice must set out particulars of the debt and the facts giving rise to it.
> 3.2 Must allege the police officer acted within the course and scope of employment.
> 3.3 20 court days.
> 3.4 Rule 18(10): date of birth; nature and extent of injuries; nature/effects/duration of disability; separately: medical/hospital costs; pain and suffering (temporary/permanent, injuries causing it); disability re earning of income (lost earnings to date, future loss, future work capacity); enjoyment of amenities of life (temporary/permanent); disfigurement (full description, temporary/permanent).
> 3.5 Jointly and severally, the one to pay the other to be absolved.

Related: [[Particulars of Claim and Rule 18 Requirements]], [[Service of Process and Edictal Citation]]

## Q4 — Interdict: action vs application, interim/final requirements (10 marks)

> [!success]- Answer (no question wording available — closely matches [[HCP 2013-09-06 Supp]] Q5)
> 4.1 Where a real dispute of fact cannot be foreseen and the true dispute is legal rather than factual, the application procedure may be used — quicker and cheaper.
> 4.2 Interim interdict: prima facie right; irreparable harm; balance of convenience favours applicant; no alternative remedy. Final interdict: a clear right; actual injury or threatened violation of rights; no alternative remedy.
> 4.3 If the dispute is simple, the court may refer the matter for oral evidence; if complex, refer to trial with directions on the exchange of pleadings.
> 4.4 The court may dismiss the application (where the dispute of fact should have been foreseen).
