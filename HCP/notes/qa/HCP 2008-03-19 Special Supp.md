## Q1 — Provisional sentence on a cheque (10 marks)

> [!success]- Answer (no question wording available)
> 1.1 Yes — if ex facie the document there is an acknowledgement of indebtedness in a fixed or ascertainable amount of money, payment of which is due.
> 1.2 Yes, only oral evidence as to the authenticity of the defendant's signature on the cheque.
> 1.3 The court may order the defendant to file a plea within a stated time, and the ordinary rules on pleading and further conduct of trial actions then apply *mutatis mutandis*.
> 1.4 Within two months of the granting of provisional sentence.
> 1.5 The provisional sentence becomes a final judgment.
> 1.6 Yes, a counterclaim may be raised, but it must arise out of the same cause of action as the cheque claim.

## Q3 — Jurisdiction, citation, third party joinder (30 marks)

> [!success]- Answer
> a) Yes — Rule 14. b) No — Rule 17.
>
> 3.2 Citation of a minor defendant: "The Defendant is Martin Son, an adult male rugby player, who resides at [address], sued in his capacity as father and natural guardian of a minor male Martin Son junior" — or, alternatively, cite the minor directly, "duly assisted by his father and guardian."
>
> 3.3 Competent fora (any two): Durban Magistrate's Court (claim under R100,000, cause of action arose there); Polokwane Magistrate's Court (claim under R100,000, defendant resides there); Pretoria High Court (defendant resides there).
>
> 3.4 Combined summons — the claim is based on delict, not a liquidated claim.
>
> 3.5 A power of attorney is required where the defendant's attorney's authority to act is disputed, or where an appeal is prosecuted.
>
> 3.6 The defendant's attorney must file a plea.
>
> 3.7 The plaintiff may serve and file a notice of bar on the defendant.
>
> 3.8 Join Lisa Cooker as a third party in terms of Rule 13.
>
> 3.9 A third party notice must be delivered before the close of pleadings, unless leave of the court is obtained.
>
> 3.10 Full third party notice: plaintiff sued defendant for R96,000 for a motor collision on 16 December 2005 in Durban (summons/particulars annexed "A"); defendant pleaded, denying sole cause (plea annexed "B"); the collision was caused by the sole negligence of the third party, who failed to keep a proper lookout and failed to adhere to a red traffic light, entering the intersection when it was inherently dangerous to do so. WHEREFORE defendant claims against the third party an order that, should plaintiff succeed against defendant, the third party indemnify defendant for the amount defendant is ordered to pay, plus costs of defending the action.

Related: [[Jurisdiction of the High Court]], [[Parties, Citation and Legal Capacity to Litigate]], [[Joinder, Third Party Procedure and Interpleader]]

## Q4 — Particulars for a contract claim (5 marks)

> [!success]- Answer
> Information/allegations needed: full details of both companies (registered address, nature/principal place of business); where and when the agreement was concluded; who represented each party; whether oral or written (attach copy if written, or full terms if oral); when and where goods were delivered; copies of delivery notes.
