Source question paper: [[HCP2012(1)Q.doc]]

## Q1 — Correct next procedural step, five scenarios (5 marks)

> [!question] Question
> 1.1 Simple summons served, no NID, dies expired. 1.2 Plaintiff failed to plead to your counterclaim, dies expired. 1.3 Client wishes to sue for divorce, spouse resides in England. 1.4 NID entered; instructions are that the claim has prescribed. 1.5 Opposing party disputes your mandate.

> [!success]- Answer
> 1.1 Apply for default judgment. 1.2 Serve a notice of bar. 1.3 Apply for an order authorising edictal citation. 1.4 Serve and file a special plea. 1.5 Lodge a power of attorney.

Related: [[Default Judgment and Rescission of Judgment]], [[Service of Process and Edictal Citation]]

## Q2 — Prayer drafting: joint delictual damages (4 marks)

> [!question] Question
> Client sues two defendants, jointly liable for R400,000. Draft the preamble and prayers.

> [!success]- Answer
> "Plaintiff claims against First and Second Defendant, jointly and severally, the one to pay the other to be absolved, payment of: R400,000; interest at 15,5% per annum a tempore morae; costs; further and alternative relief."

Related: [[Particulars of Claim and Rule 18 Requirements]]

## Q3 — Prayer drafting: action for an accounting (4 marks)

> [!question] Question
> XYZ (Pty) Ltd's debt collector Bob Shady collected debts but failed to account or pay over. Draft the prayers only.

> [!success]- Answer
> An order that the defendant render a full account for the period [x] to [y], supported by documentary proof; debatement of the account; payment of the amount the court finds due; interest at 15,5% per annum a tempore morae; costs of suit.

Related: [[Particulars of Claim and Rule 18 Requirements]]

## Q4 — Trial preparation: experts, medical exam, photographs (8 marks)

> [!question] Question
> Shopkeeper sued for a slip-and-fall injury. 4.1 What document must plaintiff's attorney file for quantum evidence, and when? 4.2 Suspected collusion with the medical practitioner — what can you do? 4.3 Photographs of the (since altered) floor — steps to use them at trial?

> [!success]- Answer
> 4.1 Notice not later than 15 days before the hearing of intent to call a medical expert; not later than 10 days before the hearing, a summary of the proposed evidence.
> 4.2 Notice requiring the plaintiff to submit to a medical examination by a practitioner nominated by the defendant.
> 4.3 Notice not less than 10 days before the hearing of intention to use the photographs.

Related: [[Discovery]]

## Q5 — Ex parte application to found jurisdiction by attachment (13 marks)

> [!question] Question
> Client Joe Saop is owed R500,000 by Helmutt Schröder, a peregrinus resident in Berlin. Our courts lack jurisdiction. Schröder is owed R120,000 by CAB (Pty) Ltd, a Pretoria company. 5.1 What is this proceeding called? 5.2 Long or short form? 5.3 On whom is it served? 5.4 Who signs it? 5.5 Draft the founding affidavit including the prayers.

> [!success]- Answer
> 5.1 An ex parte application to found jurisdiction. 5.2 The short form. 5.3 It is not served. 5.4 An attorney. 5.5 Must cover: full names/status/address of applicant and defendant; that defendant is a peregrinus of the RSA; a prima facie cause of action (attach pro forma particulars of claim); that the local debtor's debt to the defendant is due, payable and attachable; that applicant intends instituting action; prayer for an order calling on CAB (Pty) Ltd to show cause why amounts owing to the defendant should not be attached to found jurisdiction, plus alternative relief and costs.

Related: [[Jurisdiction of the High Court]], [[Application Procedure and Notice of Motion]]

## Q6 — Rule 18(10) and irregular step (15 marks)

> [!question] Question
> 6.1 List Rule 18's specific requirements for a plaintiff suing for bodily injury damages. 6.2 Effect of non-compliance? 6.3 Steps and time limits to protect a defendant's rights. 6.4 Draft the last step in the process (own facts, may omit headings).

> [!success]- Answer
> 6.1 (any five): quantum-assessable detail; date of birth; nature/extent of injuries; nature/effects/duration of disability; separately claimed medical/hospital expenses; pain and suffering (temporary/permanent); disability re earning of income; enjoyment of amenities of life; disfigurement (temporary/permanent, full description).
> 6.2 A summons not complying with Rule 18 is deemed an irregular step under Rule 30.
> 6.3 Notice to all parties specifying the irregularity, given (before any further step) within 10 days of becoming aware of it, affording the opponent an opportunity to remove the cause of complaint.
> 6.4 "BE PLEASED TO TAKE NOTICE that application will be made ... for an order in the following terms: 1. Striking out the Plaintiff's Summons as an irregular proceeding in terms of Rule 30. 2. Costs of this application. 3. Further and/or alternative relief. KINDLY ENROL the matter accordingly."

Related: [[Particulars of Claim and Rule 18 Requirements]], [[Exception, Irregular Proceedings and Striking Out]]
