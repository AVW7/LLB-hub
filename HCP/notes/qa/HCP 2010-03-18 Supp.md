## Q1 — Spot 8 mistakes in a drafted declaration (8 marks)

> [!question] Question
> A declaration (South Gauteng High Court, motor collision, employer/employee 1st & 2nd defendant) is attached, containing numerous mistakes. Identify eight.

> [!success]- Answer
> 1. South Gauteng High Court, Johannesburg has no jurisdiction (should be North Gauteng, where the collision occurred and the 1st Defendant resides).
> 2. Plaintiff's full names should be stated.
> 3. South Gauteng and North Gauteng High Courts do not have concurrent jurisdiction.
> 4. No negligence alleged — no cause of action.
> 5. Ownership of the Mercedes is irrelevant.
> 6. Insufficient particulars to found vicarious liability.
> 7. No allegation that the plaintiff suffered damages.
> 8. Insufficient particulars of damages under Rule 18.
> 9. Costs to reinstate to pre-accident condition not alleged.
> 10. Wrong preamble to the prayers — should be "jointly and severally, the one paying the other to be absolved".
> 11. Wrong interest rate — should be 15,5% p.a.
> 12. Date from which interest runs can hardly be the date of the collision.
> 13. Attorney-and-client costs not applicable.
> 14. Wrong form of summons used — should be particulars of claim (combined summons), not a declaration (which follows a simple summons).

## Q2 — Full debt-collection drafting sequence (42 marks)

> [!question] Question
> Bell Bottoms CC (manufactures "Bliss Clothing") is owed R350,000 by The Metro Man (a company), unpaid since October 2009. 2.1 Why demand payment first? 2.2 Draft the demand (body only). 2.3 Why is provisional sentence not competent? 2.4–2.5 Draft citation of plaintiff and defendant. 2.6 Draft the cause of action and prayer for a simple summons. 2.7 Draft the summary judgment supporting affidavit. 2.8 Defendant offers security instead of disclosing a defence; registrar decides the form — can you do anything if unhappy? 2.9 What must a declaration set out? 2.10 Six weeks after the plea, served with a claim in reconvention — what should defendant's attorneys have done first? 2.11 Pleadings closed — two most important next steps? 2.12 Rule 35(3) notice — what does it oblige the defendant to do? 2.13 Notice of intention to amend — minimum contents? 2.14 No objection to the amendment — how is it effected?

> [!success]- Answer
> 2.1 The date the debt fell due is unclear — a demand places the debtor in mora before summons.
> 2.2 "We act for Bell Bottoms CC ... Our instructions are that you are indebted to our client in R350,000 for goods sold and delivered during/about October 2009, which remains unpaid despite being long overdue. We demand immediate payment of R350,000. Unless paid by close of business on [date], summons will be issued without further notice."
> 2.3 No liquid document is held — provisional sentence requires one.
> 2.4 Plaintiff: "Bell Bottoms CC, a close corporation duly registered under the Close Corporations Act, carrying on business as manufacturers of men's shirts under the label 'Bliss Clothing' at [address]."
> 2.5 Defendant: "The Metro Man (Pty) Ltd, a company duly registered under SA company law, carrying on business as The Metro Man Shop, a men's clothing boutique, with its principal place of business within this Honourable Court's jurisdiction at [address]."
> 2.6 "...hereby institutes action ... claims: (a) payment of R350,000, being the purchase price due, owing and payable for goods sold and delivered during/about October 2009 in terms of an agreement concluded within this Court's jurisdiction during/about October 2009, which remains unpaid despite demand; (b) interest at 15,5% per annum a tempore morae, alternatively interest according to law; (c) costs of suit; (d) further, other or alternative relief."
> 2.7 Deponent (accounting officer) confirms personal knowledge, authority to depose, that defendant is indebted on the grounds/amount set out in the summons, verifies the cause of action and amount, and states the defendant has no bona fide defence and delivered NID solely to delay.
> 2.8 Nothing — the registrar's decision on the form/amount of security is final and binding.
> 2.9 A declaration must set out: the nature of the claim; the conclusions of law the plaintiff is entitled to deduce; and a prayer for the relief claimed.
> 2.10 The claim in reconvention is out of time (should accompany the plea). Defendant's attorneys should have asked plaintiff's attorneys to consent to late delivery; if refused, apply to court for leave.
> 2.11 Immediately: (1) call on the defendant to discover under Rule 35(1); (2) place the matter on the awaiting-trial list (or local equivalent).
> 2.12 A Rule 35(3) notice obliges the defendant to make the stated documents available for inspection under Rule 35(6), or state on oath within 10 days that they are not in its possession (and their whereabouts, if known).
> 2.13 A Rule 28 notice must: set out particulars of the proposed amendment; state that unless written objection is delivered within 10 days, the amendment will be effected.
> 2.14 By delivering each relevant page of the declaration in its amended form.
