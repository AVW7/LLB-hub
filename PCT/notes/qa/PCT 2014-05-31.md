## Q1 — Sources of the rules of professional conduct (3)

> [!question] Question
> Name three main reference sources of the rules of Professional Conduct.

> [!success]- Answer
> Any three of: the Attorneys Act 53 of 1979 as amended; the regulations promulgated thereunder; the rules of the various Law Societies; rulings of the Councils of the various Law Societies; court decisions; the common law; South African textbooks; foreign influences; the public interest.

Related: [[Sources of the Rules of Professional Conduct]]

## Q2 — Attorneys Fidelity Fund income (5)

> [!question] Question
> 2.1 What are the two main sources of income of the Attorney's Fidelity Fund? (2)
> 2.2 How can a practitioner contribute to the income of the Fund? (1)
> 2.3 What discretionary payments does the Fund make that benefit all practitioners directly? (2)

> [!success]- Answer
> 2.1 Interest on trust money invested, and income from own investments.
> 2.2 By paying the interest accumulated on trust funds invested (i.r.o. section 78(2)(a)) to the Fund.
> 2.3 Refund of bank charges and audit fees with respect to practitioners' trust banking accounts, and payment of the premium for Professional Indemnity Insurance cover to all practitioners in South Africa.

Related: [[Trust Account Theft and the Attorneys Fidelity Fund]]

## Q3 — Purpose of the Attorneys Development Fund (3)

> [!question] Question
> What is the purpose of the Attorney's Development Fund?

> [!success]- Answer
> To promote the sustainability and growth of newly established and deserving attorneys' firms, and to provide skills development and skills transfer programmes so as to create a body of qualified and competent practitioners.

Related: [[Trust Account Theft and the Attorneys Fidelity Fund]]

## Q4 — Accepting a mandate outside your expertise (1)

> [!question] Question
> If you lack knowledge and expertise with regard to a specific matter, should you accept a mandate on that matter? For example, you are a practising commercial attorney and one of your clients approaches you and asks you to represent him/her in a criminal matter. Should you accept the mandate?

> [!success]- Answer
> No.

Related: [[Declining Work Outside Your Competence]]

## Q5 — Definition of privilege (2)

> [!question] Question
> Give the definition of privilege.

> [!success]- Answer
> Privilege is the protection from evidential use of certain confidential matter.

Related: [[Confidentiality, Privilege and Protected Communications]]

## Q6 — Does one privileged document immunise the whole file? (1)

> [!question] Question
> If one of the documents in your file is a document with information that is privileged, does the file become immune to protection because of privilege?

> [!success]- Answer
> No.

Related: [[Confidentiality, Privilege and Protected Communications]]

## Q7 — "Without prejudice" (2)

> [!question] Question
> What does a statement being "without prejudice" mean?

> [!success]- Answer
> The right of a party to make statements which cannot be proved/used against them.

Related: [[Confidentiality, Privilege and Protected Communications]]

## Q8 — Conflicting interest (1)

> [!question] Question
> What is a conflicting interest?

> [!success]- Answer
> A conflicting interest is one which would be likely to affect adversely the judgement of the lawyer on behalf of or his loyalty to a client or prospective client.

Related: [[Conflict of Interest]]

## Q9 — Acting as attorney and sworn translator for the same client (1)

> [!question] Question
> Is it desirable that you, as an attorney and sworn translator of the court, translate documents to be used on behalf of your client in litigation?

> [!success]- Answer
> No.

Related: [[Conflict of Interest]]

## Q10 — Fee deposits held in trust (5)

> [!question] Question
> You request a client to make a deposit in respect of costs in advance. Once the deposit is made:
> 10.1 Does the deposit become the property of the practitioner? (1)
> 10.2 In which bank account of the firm must it be deposited? (1)
> 10.3 When can the deposit be utilized as fees? (1)
> 10.4 Can you during the course of the matter make an estimate of fees and transfer the estimated amount from your trust account to your business account? (1)
> 10.5 If you may not transfer an estimated amount, what will the consequences be if you do make a transfer? (1)

> [!success]- Answer
> 10.1 No. It remains the property of the client.
> 10.2 Trust account.
> 10.3 When the matter is completed and you have rendered a proper statement of account to the client. Unless you have agreed in advance on interim fees.
> 10.4 No.
> 10.5 It will constitute unprofessional conduct and renders you liable to be charged with theft of trust monies.

Related: [[Fees, Trust Deposits and Billing Practice]]
