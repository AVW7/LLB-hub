## Q1 — Eligibility to sit the Attorneys Admission Examination (4)

> [!question] Question
> Who may enter for the Attorneys Admission Examination?

> [!success]- Answer
> A candidate who: has completed 6 months of articles and is still serving articles; has completed articles; has satisfactorily attended a full-time course approved by the Law Society; or has been exempted from serving articles.

Related: [[Requirements for Admission as an Attorney]]

## Q2 — Admission requirements with a South African LLB (5)

> [!question] Question
> You have a South African LLB and wish to be admitted as an attorney. On what matters will you have to satisfy the High Court?

> [!success]- Answer
> You have completed an appropriate LLB degree; you have attended a practical course approved by the Law Society; you are a SA citizen or have permanent residence; you have passed the prescribed admission examination; you are a "fit and proper" person.

Related: [[Requirements for Admission as an Attorney]], [[Fit and Proper Person and Striking Off the Roll]]

## Q3 — Opening your own practice (3)

> [!question] Question
> Having been admitted you wish to open your own practice. What do you need from the Law Society and what are their requirements?

> [!success]- Answer
> You need a Fidelity Fund Certificate, which the Law Society will issue on receipt of the name and address of your practice and where your trust banking account will be kept.

Related: [[Requirements for Admission as an Attorney]]

## Q4 — Ongoing requirements after opening (3)

> [!question] Question
> After opening your own practice, what further requirements will you have to meet in due course in order to continue to practice?

> [!success]- Answer
> You must submit an opening certificate by a public accountant confirming you are operating a bookkeeping system enabling compliance with the Act; you must complete a Practice Management Course approved by the Law Society; you must renew your Fidelity Fund Certificate.

Related: [[Requirements for Admission as an Attorney]]

## Q5 — Reserved work under s83(8) (4)

> [!question] Question
> What work may by law only be done by an attorney in the expectation of fee, gain or reward?

> [!success]- Answer
> Any four of: agreements relating to immovable property or rights therein; wills or testamentary writings; memorandum/articles of association/prospectus of a company; agreements creating/dissolving a partnership; documents for use in civil court litigation. (Section 83(8) of the Attorneys Act.)

Related: [[Requirements for Admission as an Attorney]]

## Q6 — Withdrawing from a mandate (6)

> [!question] Question
> When may an attorney ethically withdraw from a mandate? How and when must it then be done?

> [!success]- Answer
> Any four grounds: a conflict of interest arises; the client requires an illegal or improper action; the client fails to give instructions; the client fails to pay an agreed deposit; a personality clash/breakdown of trust occurs; the attorney cannot perform due to illness. (4)
>
> Procedure: where appropriate, the client must first be placed on terms; the withdrawal must take place timeously/at an opportune time; the client must still have time to obtain other representation. (2)

Related: [[Accepting, Refusing and Withdrawing from a Mandate]]
