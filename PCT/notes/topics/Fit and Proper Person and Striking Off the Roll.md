# Fit and Proper Person and Striking Off the Roll

## Core standard

A practitioner is a **fit and proper person** if his/her personal qualities reflect **integrity and reliability**.

## Negligence vs misconduct

Negligence will only constitute professional misconduct if it involves an element of **impropriety** — negligence alone, without impropriety, is not misconduct.
