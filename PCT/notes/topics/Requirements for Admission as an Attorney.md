# Requirements for Admission as an Attorney

Tested as one comprehensive paper (2012-06-01) rather than scattered across sittings, but worth knowing cold since it can carry a large share of the marks in the sitting that tests it.

## Eligibility to sit the Attorneys Admission Examination

A candidate must:
- have completed 6 months of articles and still be serving articles; **or**
- have completed articles; **or**
- have satisfactorily attended a full-time course approved by the Law Society; **or**
- have been exempted from serving articles.

## Requirements for admission with a South African LLB

Must satisfy the High Court that:
- an appropriate LLB degree has been completed;
- a practical course approved by the Law Society has been attended;
- the candidate is a SA citizen or has permanent residence;
- the prescribed admission examination has been passed;
- the candidate is a **"fit and proper" person** (see [[Fit and Proper Person and Striking Off the Roll]]).

## Opening your own practice

Need a **Fidelity Fund Certificate** from the Law Society, issued on receipt of the practice's name and address and the details of the trust banking account to be kept.

## Ongoing requirements once practising

- An **opening certificate from a public accountant**, confirming the bookkeeping system enables compliance with the Attorneys Act.
- Completion of a **Practice Management Course** approved by the Law Society.
- **Annual renewal** of the Fidelity Fund Certificate.

## Reserved work (s83(8) of the Attorneys Act)

For fee, gain or reward, the following work may **only** be done by an attorney (any four):
- agreements relating to immovable property or rights therein;
- wills or testamentary writings;
- memorandum/articles of association/prospectus of a company;
- agreements creating or dissolving a partnership;
- documents for use in civil court litigation.
