# Sources of the Rules of Professional Conduct

> [!important] Statutory Update (Legal Practice Act 28 of 2014)
> - Effective **1 November 2018**, the **Legal Practice Act 28 of 2014 (LPA)** repealed the Attorneys Act 53 of 1979, abolished the provincial statutory Law Societies, and established the national **Legal Practice Council (LPC)** (with Provincial Councils), the **LPC Rules**, and the **Code of Conduct for All Legal Practitioners, Candidate Legal Practitioners and Juristic Entities**. In current exams, cite the **Legal Practice Act 28 of 2014**, the **LPC Rules**, and the **LPC Code of Conduct** alongside common law, court decisions, textbooks, foreign influences, and the public interest.

## The nine recognised sources (name any three)

1. The **Attorneys Act 53 of 1979**, as amended.
2. Regulations promulgated under the Act.
3. The rules of the various Law Societies.
4. Rulings of the Councils of the various Law Societies.
5. Court decisions.
6. The common law.
7. South African textbooks.
8. Foreign influences.
9. The public interest.
