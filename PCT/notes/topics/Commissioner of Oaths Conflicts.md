# Commissioner of Oaths Conflicts

## Core rule

A partner (or any other member of the same firm) **may not** act as commissioner of oaths for an affidavit in a matter that the firm itself is handling. All clients of the firm are deemed to be represented by **everyone** in the firm, so every partner/associate is deemed to have an interest in the matter — and legislation bars anyone with an interest in a matter from commissioning an oath in it. This applies **even where the affidavit is urgent** and no other commissioner is conveniently available.

## Exceptions

- **Conveyancing transactions.**
- **Formal affidavits for government records.**
