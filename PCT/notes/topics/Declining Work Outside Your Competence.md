# Declining Work Outside Your Competence

## Core rule

Where a client asks an attorney to handle a matter in an area the attorney knows nothing about (e.g. a commercial attorney asked to appear in a criminal matter, or asked to arrange a patent), the attorney should **decline to act** and **refer the client to another attorney** who can properly assist — rather than accept work outside their competence.
