# Conflict of Interest

## Definition

A **conflicting interest** is one which would be likely to **adversely affect the judgment** of the lawyer on behalf of, or the lawyer's **loyalty** to, a client or prospective client.

(Tested near-verbatim in two separate sittings — the definition is worth memorising exactly.)

## Where it shows up across the papers

- As one of the standard **grounds to refuse a new mandate** or **withdraw from an existing one** — see [[Accepting, Refusing and Withdrawing from a Mandate]].
- **Borrowing money from, or investing with, a client** — see [[Borrowing Money From or Investing With a Client]].
- **Commissioner of oaths within the same firm** as the acting attorney — see [[Commissioner of Oaths Conflicts]].
- An attorney acting as **both attorney and sworn translator** for the same client's litigation documents is undesirable — a dual-role conflict of the same underlying kind (attorney's judgment on the translation could be coloured by the client relationship).
- An attorney who may be called as a **witness** in the client's own matter.
