# Contingency Fee Agreements

## Core framework

- An attorney **is permitted** to enter into a contingency fee agreement with a client — including a "no win, no fee" style arrangement for a money claim.
- **Formalities:** the agreement must be **in writing**, in the form prescribed by the **Contingency Fees Act**.
- **Fee cap:** the practitioner may charge **up to double the normal fee**, but **not exceeding 25% of the amount of the award/judgment** — **whichever of the two is the lesser**.

## Regional variation (from the marking memo)

The Law Society of the Northern Provinces has accepted that members have a choice between:
1. entering into a contingency fee agreement under the Contingency Fees Act, or
2. a common-law contingency fee agreement.

Markers are instructed to apply the rules applicable to the candidate's region and give full credit for either correct answer.
