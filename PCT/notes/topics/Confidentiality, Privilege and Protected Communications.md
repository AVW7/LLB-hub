# Confidentiality, Privilege and Protected Communications

## Confidentiality vs privilege

- **Attorney-and-client privilege** arises from the **rules of evidence**, designed to encourage clients to be frank with their attorneys.
- **Confidentiality** arises from the underlying **contract** between attorney and client.
- **Privilege**, defined: the protection from evidential use of certain confidential matter.

## A privileged document does not immunise the whole file

If one document in a file is privileged, the **file as a whole does not** thereby become immune from disclosure/production.

## "Without prejudice"

A statement made "without prejudice" means the party has the right to make that statement (typically in settlement negotiations) **without it being proved or used against them** later.

## Exceptions — where confidentiality does NOT apply (name any three)

- Where **legislation** requires disclosure (e.g. FICA).
- Where the client informs the attorney that they intend to **commit a crime**.
- Disclosure of the **client's identity/name**.
- Where the attorney has knowledge of an **asset in a deceased client's estate** of which the executor is unaware.
