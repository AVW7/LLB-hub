# Plea Procedure

Drafting a plea is the highest-value skill in CCP — nearly every paper includes a full statement to draft, worth 10–17 marks.

## The three plea instruments

### s112(2) — plea of guilty
Used in the Magistrate's Court where the accused pleads guilty. The statement must:
- confirm the accused is in sound and sober senses and understands the charge;
- confirm the plea is voluntary, without undue influence;
- narrate facts that satisfy **every element** of the offence charged (unlawfulness, intent/negligence, causation, the specific conduct);
- be signed and dated.

### s115 — plea of not guilty
Used where the accused denies guilt (in whole or part). The statement should:
- plead not guilty to the specific charge;
- set out the basis of the defence (e.g. private/self-defence, sudden emergency, denial of an element);
- record **formal admissions under s220** for facts not in dispute (dates, identity, cause of death per post-mortem, chain-of-custody reports, photo albums) — this narrows the trial to the genuine dispute and speeds up proceedings.

### s113 — correcting a guilty plea
Where an accused (often unrepresented) has pleaded guilty to a charge they don't actually have all the elements of (classic fact pattern: pleaded guilty to **housebreaking and theft**, but entry was via an **open door** — no housebreaking element).
Procedure:
1. Advise the client that they have a defence to part of the charge as pleaded.
2. Apply under **s113** to correct the plea.
3. The accused gives evidence under oath; the prosecutor may cross-examine; the court may also question the accused; witnesses may be called in support.
4. Defence addresses the court on the merits, then the State responds.
5. Magistrate rules on the application.
6. **If successful** — proceedings continue as if a not-guilty plea had been tendered from the outset. The State/Defence may agree the admitted facts and close their cases; a conviction of the lesser offence (theft only) becomes available; proceed to sentence.
7. **If unsuccessful** — proceed immediately to sentence on the original guilty plea.

## Drafting checklist (any statement)

- Correct court heading (Magistrate's Court / Regional Court / High Court) and case number
- "In the matter between: The State and [Accused]"
- Correct statutory heading ("Statement in terms of Section 112(2)/115 of the Criminal Procedure Act 51 of 1977")
- First-person declaration ("I, the undersigned, [Name], state as follows:")
- Numbered paragraphs
- Signature block, place and date
