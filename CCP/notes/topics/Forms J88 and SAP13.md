# Forms J88 and SAP13

Two standard forms that recur across CCP fact patterns (post-mortem/injury evidence, exhibit handling) — worth knowing by name and purpose even where a paper doesn't ask about them directly.

## Form J88

Medical report used where an injury was sustained (including rape cases). Records:
- the nature of the injury;
- a medical opinion on how it could have been sustained;
- crucial complainant details (age, sex, etc.).

## Form SAP13

Exhibit register. Items pertaining to a particular case are entered on it and later produced at trial. Contains crucial chain-of-custody information for physical evidence.

## Where these show up implicitly

Several fact-pattern questions reference "the fingerprint report," "the photo album," "the post-mortem report" as items in the police docket admitted by s220 — these are the kind of items an SAP13 register would track and a J88 would document where injury/cause of death is in issue.
