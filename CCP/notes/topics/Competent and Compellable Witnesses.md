# Competent and Compellable Witnesses

Tested via a short scenario cluster on spousal witnesses (s196 CPA).

## Core scenario (from [[CCP 2012-01-28]] Q5)

- Client's **estranged wife** as a *defence* witness — **competent and compellable** (s196).
- Client's wife called by a **co-accused** and she refuses — **not compellable**.
- Client charged with assaulting **the child born of the marriage** — wife is **both competent and compellable** to give evidence against the accused in that scenario (offences against a child of the marriage are an exception to ordinary spousal non-compellability).

## Quick-reference answer pattern

| Scenario | Competent? | Compellable? |
|---|---|---|
| Estranged spouse as defence witness | Yes | Yes |
| Spouse called by a co-accused (against accused) | — | No, if she refuses |
| Spouse as witness re: offence against their own child | Yes | Yes |
