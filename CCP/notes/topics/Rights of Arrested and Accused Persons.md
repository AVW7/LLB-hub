# Rights of Arrested and Accused Persons

## During police interrogation / custody

- **Right to remain silent** — not obliged to answer questions relating to the merits of the case (e.g. "did you steal the vehicle?" → **no**, not obliged to answer).
- **Right to a legal representative present** when making any statement — client can require this before speaking to police at all.
- **Obligation that remains**: must furnish full **names and address** without delay when asked — this is not covered by the right to silence.

## Competent and compellable witnesses (cross-reference)

See [[Competent and Compellable Witnesses]] for spousal competence/compellability, which sits alongside these rights.

## Answer template for "advise your client" questions

1. Right to remain silent on the merits.
2. Right to request a legal representative be present during any statement.
3. Must still furnish name and address without delay (this is the one thing they cannot refuse).
