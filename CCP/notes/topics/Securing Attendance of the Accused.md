# Securing Attendance of the Accused in Court

## Four methods

1. **Arrest / custody**
2. **Summons**
3. **Written notice**
4. **Warning** (e.g. warned to appear when released, or warned over from a previous appearance)

## Related: the four ways a suspect can be brought before court

The 2019 assignment phrases the same idea from the suspect's side — "four ways a suspect can be brought before a court" maps onto this same list.
