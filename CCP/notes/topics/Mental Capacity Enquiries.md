# Mental Capacity Enquiries (s77–s79 CPA)

Two distinct enquiries, both conducted under the s79 procedure — a common source of confusion, so keep them clearly separated.

## Section 77 — capacity to understand the proceedings (fitness to stand trial)

If it appears, at any stage of the proceedings, that the accused is by reason of mental illness or mental defect **not capable of understanding the proceedings** so as to make a proper defence, the court directs that the matter be enquired into and reported on under s79. The legal representative (if any) must bring this to the court's attention as early as possible.

**Timing test:** capacity **at the time of trial**.

## Section 78 — criminal responsibility at the time of the offence

Asks whether the accused, **at the time of the offence**, suffered from a mental illness or defect which made them incapable of:
- appreciating the wrongfulness of the act, or
- acting in accordance with that appreciation.

**Timing test:** capacity **at the time the offence was committed**.

## Memory hook

- s77 = "**can he understand the trial now?**"
- s78 = "**could he understand what he was doing then?**"
