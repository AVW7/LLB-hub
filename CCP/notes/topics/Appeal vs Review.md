# Appeal vs Review

The single most-repeated table question in CCP — appears almost verbatim in nearly every paper. Memorise the table cold.

## The table

| Appeal | Review |
|---|---|
| Against a judgment / conviction or sentence | Against **procedure** |
| Limited to the contents of the **record** of the proceedings | Evidence may be tendered by way of **affidavit** |
| Limited to the **ground of appeal** noted | **Any** ground of review |
| Within a **specific time limit** (14 days, Magistrate's Court) | Within a **reasonable time** |

## Three kinds of review

1. **Automatic review**
2. **Normal review**
3. **Argued review**

## Exam pattern

Usually asked as either:
- "Tabulate the differences between appeal and review" (4 marks) — reproduce the table above; or
- "What is the difference between appeal and review?" (4 marks) — same content, prose or table both accepted per the memos.
