# Discharge and Close of State's Case (s174)

## Options available to the accused at close of the State's case

Consistently tested as a list question (3–4 marks) and as a scenario (advise the client):
1. **Change of plea**
2. **Application for discharge under s174**
3. **Close the defence case** without calling the accused or witnesses
4. **Open the defence case** and call the accused and/or witnesses

## Section 174 discharge

> If, at the close of the prosecution's case, the court believes there is no evidence that the accused committed the offence, it may return a verdict of not guilty.

Trigger for advising a s174 application: the State has failed to establish even a **prima facie case** against the accused on the evidence led.

## Related procedure: s113 correction of plea

Where the close-of-State's-case scenario is combined with a wrongly-entered guilty plea (housebreaking via an open door being the recurring fact pattern), the discharge options interact with the **s113** correction-of-plea procedure — see [[Plea Procedure]] for the full sequence.
