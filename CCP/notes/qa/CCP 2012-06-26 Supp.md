## Q1 — s115 statement, private defence with disarmed weapon (10)

> [!question] Question
> Accused charged with assault GBH, says he acted in private defence after disarming the complainant of a knife. Draw a Section 115 statement, including headings.

> [!success]- Answer
> Not-guilty s115 statement: admits the complainant and date of the incident; denies unlawfully/intentionally stabbing; states the complainant produced the knife and tried to stab him; states he disarmed the complainant and stabbed him once while held by the collar (private defence narrative).

Related: [[Plea Procedure]]

## Q2 — Options at close of State's case (4)

> [!question] Question
> Name four options the accused has at the close of the State's case.

> [!success]- Answer
> Change of plea; s174 application; closing the defence case; opening the defence case.

Related: [[Discharge and Close of State's Case]]

## Q3 — Withdrawing a case after a not-guilty plea (3)

> [!question] Question
> May the prosecutor withdraw a case after the accused has pleaded not guilty? Motivate.

> [!success]- Answer
> Withdrawal amounts to a **stopping of the prosecution**, which is the prerogative of the **Director of Public Prosecutions**. Without DPP authorisation, the prosecutor cannot withdraw and must seek authority.

## Q4 — Conflicted co-accused (3)

> [!question] Question
> 4.1 Two accused with conflicting statements ask one attorney to represent them — proceed? 4.2 Can the attorney elect to represent just one?

> [!success]- Answer
> 4.1 No. 4.2 No — once briefed and then withdrawn, counsel may not cross-examine that accused on behalf of the co-accused.

Related: [[Legal Representation of Multiple Accused]]

## Q5 — Rights under police interrogation (6)

> [!question] Question
> 5.1 Client under police custody says police are forcing a statement — advise on rights and reply to police (4). 5.2 What must the client furnish under the CPA? (2)

> [!success]- Answer
> 5.1 Right to remain silent on the merits; entitled to request a legal representative be present. 5.2 Obliged to furnish full names and address without delay.

Related: [[Rights of Arrested and Accused Persons]]

## Q6 — Mitigation, theft/job loss (10)

> [!question] Question
> Accused is 50, married, four children; lost employment due to a theft charge but now earns R3000/month in a new job. List mitigating factors in full.

> [!success]- Answer
> Personal circumstances (age, marriage, four minor children aged 15/12/8/5, unemployed wife, income); remorse (guilty plea, cooperation); facts (loss of previous employment, civil claim instituted, amount repaid, first offender).

Related: [[Sentencing and Mitigation]]

## Q7 — s60(11B) disclosures (4)

> [!question] Question
> 7.1 Two facts the accused must disclose under s60(11B). 7.2 Consequences of wilful non-disclosure?

> [!success]- Answer
> 7.1 Previous convictions; pending charges/current bail status. 7.2 Offence, fine or imprisonment up to 2 years.

## Q9 — Objecting to a charge, s85 procedure (6)

> [!question] Question
> Discuss briefly the procedure to object to a charge under s85 CPA.

> [!success]- Answer
> Reasonable notice to the prosecution stating the ground of objection, which may be waived by the prosecution; court may dispense with notice on good cause or adjourn to allow it; if the objection is well-founded, the court orders amendment/further particulars; failure to comply with such an order may result in the charge being quashed.

Related: [[Objection to a Charge]]
