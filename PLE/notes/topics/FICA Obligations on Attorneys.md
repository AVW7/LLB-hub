# FICA Obligations on Attorneys

Attorneys are "accountable institutions" under the Financial Intelligence Centre Act 38 of 2001 (FICA). Tested consistently across three sittings with the same two-part structure: the aim of the Act, then the client-identification steps required before taking on work.

## Aim of FICA

> "To combat organised crime. To deter money laundering." — [[PLE 2015-06-11 Supp]] A1.1, restated at [[PLE 2016-06-15 Supp]] A3

## Steps before establishing a business relationship or single transaction

All practitioners are accountable institutions under FICA and must, before establishing a business relationship or concluding even a single transaction with a client:

(a) Establish and verify the **identity of the client**.
(b) If the client is acting on behalf of another person, establish and verify:
   (i) the identity of that other person, and
   (ii) the client's authority to act for that other person in the relationship/transaction.
(c) If another person is acting on behalf of the client, establish and verify:
   (i) the identity of that other person, and
   (ii) that other person's authority to act on behalf of the client.

Sources: [[PLE 2015-06-11 Supp]] A1.2, [[PLE 2015-11-26 Supp]] A3 (identical steps, asked without the "aim" sub-question).
