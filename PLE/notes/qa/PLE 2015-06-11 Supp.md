## Q1 — FICA aim and identification steps (7)

> [!question] Question
> 1.1 What is the aim of the Financial Intelligence Centre Act (2001)? (2)
> 1.2 All practitioners are accountable institutions under FICA. Name the prescribed steps you must take before establishing a business relationship or concluding even a single transaction with a client. (5)

> [!success]- Answer
> 1.1 To combat organised crime; to deter money laundering.
> 1.2 (a) Establish and verify the identity of the client. (b) If the client acts on behalf of another, establish and verify (i) that other person's identity and (ii) the client's authority to act for them. (c) If another person acts on behalf of the client, establish and verify (i) that other person's identity and (ii) their authority to act for the client.

Related: [[FICA Obligations on Attorneys]]

## Q2 — Requirements for admission (4)

> [!question] Question
> You have passed the attorneys admission examination and intend to apply for admission as an attorney of the High Court. On which matters will you have to satisfy the Court?

> [!success]- Answer
> Any 4 of: fit and proper person; SA citizen or permanent resident; SA LLB qualification or a qualification from a designated country; served the required period of articles or exempted therefrom; passed the attorneys admission examination.

Related: [[Admission as an Attorney and Fitness to Practise]]

## Q3 — Fidelity Fund Certificate requirements (2)

> [!question] Question
> After admission you intend to practise for your own account, for which you need a Fidelity Fund Certificate. What information will your Law Society require before issuing a certificate?

> [!success]- Answer
> Any 4 (half mark each): details of trust bank account; FICA registration; address and contact details; membership fees; names of all professionals in your practice; details of appointed auditors.

Related: [[Admission as an Attorney and Fitness to Practise]]

## Q4 — Contributing to the Fidelity Fund (1)

> [!question] Question
> How can a single practitioner or a firm of practitioners contribute to the income of the Attorneys Fidelity Fund?

> [!success]- Answer
> By investing trust funds to earn interest for the benefit of the Fund.

## Q5 — Assessing a fair and reasonable fee (5)

> [!question] Question
> State briefly what factors an assessor appointed by a Law Society will take into account in assessing whether a fee charged was fair and reasonable.

> [!success]- Answer
> Any 5: amount and importance of the work done; complexity/difficulty/novelty of the work; skill, labour, specialised knowledge and responsibility involved; number and importance of documents prepared or perused; place and circumstances of the services; time expended; amount or value of money/property involved; importance of the matter to the client; quality of the work done; experience or seniority of the practitioner.

Related: [[Fee Assessment, Overreaching and Fee Sharing]]

## Q6 — Grounds to refuse a mandate (3)

> [!question] Question
> Name three situations in which an attorney would be obliged to refuse a mandate to act on behalf of a client.

> [!success]- Answer
> Any three: conflict of interest exists; client wants the attorney to break the law or commit fraud; attorney lacks expertise for the matter; the matter develops in a way requiring expertise/skill the practitioner does not possess.

Related: [[Mandate Acceptance, Refusal and Withdrawal]]

## Q7 — Duty to disclose adverse authority (3)

> [!question] Question
> At the end of the evidence in a civil trial, you are preparing to address the Court in summation. You discover SCA judgments relevant to your client's case. What are your duties in relation to: 8.1 the court (2); 8.2 your colleague (1)?

> [!success]- Answer
> 8.1 An attorney's duty is first and foremost to the court; the obligation is to assist the court in dispensing justice — you must bring to the court's attention cases both supportive of and adverse to your case.
> 8.2 No similar obligation exists in respect of your colleague.
