# Pre-Trial Procedure and Expedited Judgment

## Expedited (summary) judgment

- **Magistrates' Court Rule 14(1)** permits an expedited judgment application only for: a liquid document; a liquidated amount in money; delivery of specified movable property; or ejectment. An unliquidated damages claim cannot support this remedy even where joined with a liquidated claim in the same action — assess each claim separately.
- Cancelling an agreement before suing is prudent, but it is trite that service of a summons claiming cancellation itself constitutes proper notice of cancellation, provided the necessary entitlement-to-cancel allegations are pleaded.
- **Rule 6(6)** requires a written contract relied on to be attached to the particulars of claim; failure to do so is an irregular step under Rule 6(13), remediable under Rule 60A — it is an irregularity, not automatically a failure to disclose a cause of action.
- The Magistrates' Courts have **no general power of condonation** (Rule 60A(3)'s wide remedial power operates only once an irregular-step application is before the court, not as a freestanding condonation power — *Hip Hop Clothing Manufacturing CC v Wagener NO*). The High Court, by contrast, has a wide condonation power under Rule 27 — identical non-compliance may be condonable in the High Court but not the Magistrates' Court.
- **Rule 14(2)** requires the supporting affidavit to allege personal knowledge of the facts, to swear *positively* to facts verifying the cause of action and the amount claimed (a bare "confirmation" of indebtedness is insufficient), and to state there is no bona fide defence and that the notice of intention to defend was filed solely to delay.

## Pre-trial and interlocutory procedure

- **Notice of Bar:** the remedy where the defendant fails to file a plea within the prescribed period despite correspondence.
- **Application to compel:** the remedy where the defendant fails to supply requested further particulars.
- **Exception:** the remedy where the defendant's plea is vague and embarrassing (or discloses no defence).
- **Rule 37 pre-trial conference:** purpose is to reach agreement on formal issues, eliminate unnecessary witnesses/evidence, shorten the trial, conserve costs, and agree procedural matters (onus, duty to begin, bundles, separation of issues) so the trial runs smoothly. Each party (usually through their attorney, not the party personally) must attend; the minutes must be signed by or on behalf of every party.
- **Rule 28 notice to amend:** sets out particulars of the proposed amendment and states that unless written objection is delivered within 10 days of the notice, the amendment will be effected.
- **Rule 36(9)(b) expert notice:** must set out the expert's personal particulars, experience and qualifications, the instructions given, the address/subject examined, the findings, and the estimated cost.
