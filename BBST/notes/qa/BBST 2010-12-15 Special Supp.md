## Q1 — Matrimonial matters: Hassim divorce + settlement agreement

> [!question] Question
> Mohammed and Naazira Hassim, married in community of property since 1988, two children (20 and 15). Family survived a car accident that killed their youngest child and Mohammed's father. Mohammed wants to remarry; both want to finalise the divorce. They've informally agreed on maintenance (medical expenses + R1 000/month for Naazira; R5 000/month + medical/scholastic expenses for the 15-year-old), asset division and a new car for Naazira. Draft the particulars of claim and settlement agreement.

## Q2 — Insolvency practice: David Max Brown / Andree Scott

> [!question] Question
> Your client, a commercial printer, obtained default judgment of R250 000 plus costs against a debtor (Andree Scott) and issued a writ of execution. Investigation reveals: (a) a Range Rover worth ~R300 000, already attached by another creditor (Standard Bank) owed R300 000; (b) three further default judgments (R50 000, R20 000, R40 000) by miscellaneous creditors; (c) a mortgaged holiday house worth ~R450 000 (bond R150 000) that could be sold. The sheriff has advertised a sale in execution for one week's time. What is the appropriate remedy? Draft the founding affidavit in support of the application.

> [!note]- Self-check pointers (not an official memo)
> See [[Compulsory Sequestration and Acts of Insolvency]] — sequestration is the appropriate remedy to arrest the piecemeal attachment by a single creditor and bring all of the debtor's assets (including the unencumbered equity in the holiday house) into a collective process for the benefit of all creditors. Draft the founding affidavit addressing jurisdiction, the liquidated claim, an act of insolvency/factual insolvency, and advantage to creditors.

## Q3 — Personal injury: Daisy RAF claim

> [!question] Question
> Daisy, 18, injured as a passenger when her father (the driver) collided with a robot; serious permanent disfigurement. 1. Possible claim(s) and against whom? 2. What can she claim — tariffs, limited/unlimited, special/general damages, which forms? 3. Last date for lodgment and service of summons? 4. Where should the claim be lodged? 5. Would the answer differ if the accident occurred on 14 April 2006? 6. Draft the particulars of claim (High Court) assuming the RAF made no offer. 7. If the RAF's offer was accepted before summons was served, can legal costs still be recovered?

> [!note]- Self-check pointers (not an official memo)
> See [[Road Accident Fund Claims]] — this question spans the pre- and post-2008 RAF Act amendment regime (Q5 tests whether the 2006 accident date changes the answer, i.e. before the amendment introducing the serious-injury threshold for general damages). Cross-reference [[BBST 2012-06-09]] Q5, which tests the identical amendment-date distinction.
