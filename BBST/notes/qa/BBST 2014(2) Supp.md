## Statement of Facts (identical to 2013(1))

John and Sally Biko, married in community of property; John is sole member of J&S Motors CC, which trades in used vehicles and is in financial difficulty (SARS, salary and rental arrears, a default judgment) after selling stock-in-trade to the members themselves at undervalue. Quantum Bank wants to liquidate the CC and hold the Bikos personally liable. The Bikos plan an expedited, uncontested divorce (assets to Mrs Biko) without alerting the Bank, and hold a joint will leaving the estate to the survivor (or to their son Pieter on simultaneous death).

## Questions (identical to 2013(1) — see that note for full text and real memo answers)

- **Q1 (20)** — Matrimonial property basics: liability for marital debts after divorce; the three matrimonial property systems. See [[Matrimonial Property Systems]].
- **Q2 (45)** — Personal liability of Mr Biko for the CC's debts (Close Corporations Act ss63–65, *Airport Cold Storage v Ebrahim*); drafting the liquidation/sequestration notice and founding affidavit; workers' rights on liquidation (Insolvency Act s38); effect of the intervening divorce on the sequestration. See [[Personal Liability of CC Members]], [[Compulsory Sequestration and Acts of Insolvency]].
- **Q3 (10)** — Rescission of the sequestration order post-divorce (Rule 42, Rule 31(2)(b), s149(2) Insolvency Act; *Abdurahman v Estate Abdurahman*). See [[Rescission of Judgment]].
- **Q4 (25)** — Letter of demand to SAPS for vicarious liability after the fatal collision with Sergeant Dube's vehicle; effect of the joint will and the s2B Wills Act three-month deeming provision given the sequence of the Bikos' deaths. See [[Road Accident Fund Claims]], [[Wills and Disqualified Beneficiaries]].
