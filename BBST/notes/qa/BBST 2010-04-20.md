## Q1 — Effect of sequestration on pending litigation (marks not stated)

> [!question] Question
> What is the effect on a pending action if one of the parties thereto is sequestrated?

> [!note]- Self-check pointers (not an official memo)
> See [[Compulsory Sequestration and Acts of Insolvency]] — a pending action against an insolvent is generally stayed pending the appointment of a trustee, who may be substituted as a party or elect whether to continue defending/prosecuting the claim.

## Q2 — Naidoo: sequestration, founding affidavit, order of preference

> [!question] Question
> Mr and Mrs Naidoo are married out of community of property, accrual excluded. Detailed facts given on Mr Naidoo's plumbing business assets/debts (house, truck, tools, laptop, TV) and Mrs Naidoo's beauty salon, inherited house, vehicle and furniture. Mr Naidoo owes Special Bank (overdraft), nine months' unpaid worker salaries, SARS VAT, Telkom, ABC Manufacturing (materials), and DEF Commercial Properties (rental arrears).
> 2.1 ABC Manufacturing's managing director instructs you to apply for Mr Naidoo's sequestration (company holds no security). Draft the Founding Affidavit.
> 2.2 List the creditors mentioned in descending order of preference, with the relevant Insolvency Act sections and the preferent portion of each claim.

> [!note]- Self-check pointers (not an official memo)
> See [[Compulsory Sequestration and Acts of Insolvency]] for the founding affidavit structure (jurisdiction, notice to Master, liquidated claim, act of insolvency/factual insolvency, advantage to creditors). 2.2 requires ranking preferent claims (e.g. secured bond creditor, SARS, employees' wages under the Insolvency Act's order of preference) ahead of concurrent creditors — work through Naidoo's asset values against each debt using the Act's preference sections.

## Q3 — Malumbete RAF claim (family of deceased passenger)

> [!question] Question
> Collision at a stop-street controlled intersection; Mr Malumbete, a passenger, killed instantly. Mrs Malumbete (married in community of property, three minor children) consults you.
> 3.1 Does she have a claim against the RAF, and what is the maximum claimable? 3.2 What would the claim be for? 3.3 Any other party she may have to sue? 3.4 Last date for lodgment against the RAF? 3.5 Where must the claim be lodged? 3.6 Last date for service of summons? 3.7 Which court? 3.8 In what capacity must she act?

> [!note]- Self-check pointers (not an official memo)
> See [[Road Accident Fund Claims]] — loss of support claim by a widow acting in her personal capacity and as guardian of the minor children; lodgment/prescription periods under the RAF Act.

## Q4 — Zulu: RAF particulars of claim (drunk driver, wrong side of road)

> [!question] Question
> Mrs Zulu's husband killed by a drunk driver travelling on the incorrect side of the road; two minor children; deceased earned R35 000/month; Mrs Zulu never worked during the 16-year marriage. Claim lodged, RAF neither offered nor repudiated after the statutory period. Draft the particulars of claim (High Court).

> [!note]- Self-check pointers (not an official memo)
> See [[Road Accident Fund Claims]] — loss of support quantification for a non-earning surviving spouse and minor children; citation of the RAF as defendant; jurisdiction; prayers.

## Q5 — Peterson: consultation questions for an RAF "third party claim"

> [!question] Question
> 5.1 List at least 10 questions to establish the possible merits of the claim. 5.2 List at least 10 questions to establish the possible quantum.

> [!note]- Self-check pointers (not an official memo)
> See [[Road Accident Fund Claims]] — merits questions cover how/where the collision occurred, negligence, driver/vehicle details, police reports; quantum questions cover income before/after, medical expenses, degree of injury, future loss of earnings.

## Q6 — Kekana: divorce particulars of claim + settlement agreement

> [!question] Question
> Peter and Lily Kekana, married in community of property since 1984, two children (20 and 15). They have drifted apart, both want to divorce, have informally agreed on maintenance, asset division (each keeps current residence and possessions), and a new car for Lily. Draft the particulars of claim and a settlement agreement giving effect to their wishes.

> [!note]- Self-check pointers (not an official memo)
> See [[Divorce Actions and Settlement Agreements]] — citation, jurisdiction under the Divorce Act, particulars of the marriage/children, best-interests-of-the-child averments, incorporation of the settlement agreement as an order of court, prayers.
