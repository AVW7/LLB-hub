## Statement of Facts

You are a candidate attorney handling three files for existing clients Yvonne and Steve Mbeki (married in community of property, two children), whose joint estate has fallen from R1,45 million to R615 000 net after medical costs and failed high-risk investments; their home is under debt review.

**File 1 — Lease/eviction:** Sure Rentals CC (Steve's close corporation) sues Linda Katz t/a SA Motor Parts for R100 000 arrears rental (December 2016 – July 2017) and eviction from commercial premises Katz used for vehicle repairs, causing floor damage. Full combined summons text is included in the facts.

**File 2 — CC insolvency and personal liability:** S & Y Coins CC (Yvonne's employer, dealing in rare coins) borrowed R500 000 from Safety Bank against a six-month repayment term, secured by sureties from members Kruger and Bernato. The CC sold coins below market value to raise cash, kept no formal accounting records or accounting officer, paid VAT-inclusive income into members' personal accounts, and posted a notice that it was "closed until further notice due to financial difficulties." Safety Bank intends to sue the CC and/or hold Kruger and Bernato personally liable. Kruger has since divorced (decree made an order of court by agreement) — timing again raising a divorce-of-convenience question echoing the Biko fact pattern in [[BBST 2013(1)]].

**File 3 — RAF claim:** The Mbekis' son Pieter was severely injured (traumatic head injury, pelvic/femur fractures, bladder rupture) when Sergeant John Biko of SAPS, driving an official vehicle while on his phone, rear-ended the stationary vehicle in which Pieter was a passenger, driven by Joan Harris (an au pair employed by the Mbekis under a verbal agreement). Past medical expenses R815 000, future medical expenses R275 000. The Mbekis' own vehicle (leased, R790 987,23 still owing) was written off, towed and sold for scrap after their insurer (SkyNet) settled the claim in full.
