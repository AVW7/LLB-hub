# BBST — 2013(1) Supplementary

Source question paper: [[BBST2013(1)SuppQ.doc]]
Source combined memo: [[BBST2013(1)SuppQ&A.doc]]

Facts and questions combined in a single document. Three unrelated waiting-room clients (4h30, 100 marks).

## Client 1 — Tembi Ndlebe (rehabilitation)

Sequestrated 1 year ago (retrenchment); no prior sequestration, no insolvency-related conviction. Claims proved: ABC Bank R300 000 (bonded), SARS R20 000, Markhams R7 000. House sold for R350 000, other assets R20 000.

### Q1 — Rehabilitation options (30)

> [!question] Question
> 1.1 Advise on when she might apply for rehabilitation and whether it happens automatically (10). Further information: assume she was sequestrated five years ago, now employed earning R20 000/month gross, assets: savings R10 000, Toyota Corolla R20 000, furniture R15 000, no liabilities. 1.2 Draft the affidavit in support of an application for rehabilitation (20).

> [!success]- Answer
> 1.1 Rehabilitation timelines: automatic after 10 years; a possible route at 12 months from the account being confirmed; 3 years if previously sequestrated; 5 years if convicted of an insolvency-related offence; 6 months if there are no outstanding claims, no prior sequestration and no fraud.
> 1.2 Memo checks for the affidavit: correct heading for an ex parte High Court application; made personally by the applicant; full particulars; confirmation the facts are true, correct and within personal knowledge; reference to the sequestration order of her own estate; confirmation no prior sequestration and no insolvency-related conviction; background of the insolvency per the facts, with the final liquidation and distribution account attached; allegation of complete surrender of the estate; current employment/income; current assets/new estate; account of current income and expenses; current liabilities; allegation that security was lodged (s125); allegation of adequate notice to the Master and to the trustee (with proof attached); allegation of Government Gazette publication (with proof); prayer that the court rehabilitate her under s124(4); deponent's signature and Commissioner of Oaths certificate.

Related: [[Rehabilitation of an Insolvent Estate]]

## Client 2 — Cecile Coetzee (divorce + maintenance)

Teacher, married in community of property to Koos (plumber), one child Jaco (aged ~6, living costs R3 000/month). Koos is serving a life sentence for rape and murder; still has a going concern earning ~R30 000/year. Cecile earns R15 000/month.

### Q2 — Particulars of claim + maintenance determination (35)

> [!question] Question
> 2.1 Draw up the particulars of claim (25). 2.2 How will the court determine what maintenance Koos must pay (10)?

> [!success]- Answer
> 2.1 Memo checks: particulars of claim for a divorce action, clearly drafted and in correct order; correct citation of Plaintiff/Defendant; jurisdiction under s2(1)(a) of Act 70 of 1979; particulars of the marriage and child; best-interests averments (both retain guardianship; Plaintiff retains care and primary residence; Defendant's contact rights are limited, with reasons given — given Koos's convictions); the Regulation 2 affidavit under the Mediation in Certain Divorce Matters Act attached as Annexure "A"; correctly pleaded reasons for the marriage breakdown; division of the estate; correct prayers.
> 2.2 The court weighs: the child's previous lifestyle; the child's needs; both parents' income (both parents are liable for maintenance in proportion to means); the child's best interests and needs come first.

Related: [[Divorce Actions and Settlement Agreements]], [[Maintenance Claims]]

## Client 3 — Victoria Kware (RAF claim + trial procedure)

Nurse, married in community of property to Samuel (Unilever sales consultant), one 15-year-old child, Lerato (wants to study accounting). Samuel killed on 20 July 2010 crossing the road; driver (John Biko) ran a red light while on his phone. Samuel's income was R300 000/year; past medical expenses R400 000; funeral expenses R20 000.

### Q3 — RAF particulars of claim + default judgment documents (23)

> [!question] Question
> 3.1 Draft the particulars of claim against the RAF (no offer made within the prescribed time; summons in the High Court) (20). 3.2 What documents are required to apply for default judgment if the Defendant fails to file a notice of intention to defend (3)?

> [!success]- Answer
> 3.1 Memo checks: correct citation of the plaintiff personally and in her representative capacity; correct citation of the RAF; date/time/place and description of the collision; driver and vehicle detail; jurisdiction; description of negligence; result of the collision; damages for the plaintiff personally and representatively, properly calculated taking into account the capped amounts, with the calculation method and relevant factors stated; allegation of compliance with the RAF Act; full and correct prayers; clear, unambiguous drafting.
> 3.2 Original summons; return of service; statements proving the plaintiff's quantum.

### Q4 — Trial procedure once defended (12)

> [!question] Question
> Further information: the Defendant enters a Notice of Intention to Defend. 4.1 Remedy if the Defendant fails to file a plea within the prescribed period despite correspondence (1)? 4.2 Remedy if the Defendant fails to supply requested further particulars (1)? 4.3 Remedy where the Defendant's plea is vague and embarrassing (1)? 4.4 What is the fundamental purpose of the Rule 37 pre-trial conference (3)? 4.5 Who must attend (1)? 4.6 Who must sign the minutes (1)? 4.7 Draft the notice to amend the particulars of claim to correct the date of the collision (4).

> [!success]- Answer
> 4.1 File a Notice of Bar. 4.2 Bring an application to compel. 4.3 Deliver a notice of exception. 4.4 Rule 37's purpose is to reach agreement on formal issues to eliminate unnecessary witnesses/evidence, shorten the trial, conserve costs, and agree procedural matters (onus, duty to begin, bundles, separation of issues) so the trial runs smoothly. 4.5 Each party or their attorney (usually attorneys attend, not the parties). 4.6 Signed by or on behalf of every party, usually the attorneys. 4.7 Rule 28 notice: set out particulars of the proposed amendment and state that unless written objection is delivered within 10 days of the notice, the amendment will be effected.

Related: [[Road Accident Fund Claims]], [[Pre-Trial Procedure and Expedited Judgment]]
