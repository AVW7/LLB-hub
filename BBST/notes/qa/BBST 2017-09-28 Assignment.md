## Statement of Facts

You are a practising attorney consulted by five separate clients/matters:

1. **Mr and Mrs Down** — married out of community of property 10 years, several jointly-registered properties. Mr Down's import/export company is insolvent (sued by two suppliers, bank demanding payment on two overdrafts he personally guaranteed) despite cost-cutting. Mrs Down runs a separate, successful clothing-import sole proprietorship.
2. **Mr and Mrs Smal** — married out of community of property with accrual, two minor children (Benjamin 6, Sue-Ann 4). Mrs Smal wants a divorce (alleges disrespect, alcohol abuse, and a suspected affair); Mr Smal is moving out.
3. **The Smal Family Trust** (Mr and Mrs Smal as trustees, children as beneficiaries) owns a rental house leased to Mr and Mrs Stone (married in community of property) from 1 October 2015, holding over on expiry with no new agreement. The Stones fell into arrears from December 2016, then vanished, leaving the property damaged (missing cupboard, torn carpet, dead garden, broken pool pump) and a Mrs Blue occupying it, claiming she doesn't know their whereabouts and can't pay rent herself. Repair quotes total specific itemised amounts; municipal arrears and a tracer's fee are also owed.
4. **Maya Burke** (Mrs Smal's sister) is in an abusive cohabiting relationship with John Johnson, who is financially/verbally/emotionally abusive, has sold her assets and damaged property in anger, and has threatened to burn down the house and kill her; he owns a firearm.
5. **Mr Andrews** learns a newspaper, The Weekly, is about to publish allegedly false, defamatory information about him going to press the next day, and wants to stop publication.

## Q1 — Down: company insolvency options (20)

> [!question] Question

## Q3 — Recovering against the Stones; evicting Mrs Blue (35)

> [!question] Question
> 3.1 What type of summons recovers the arrears owed by the Stones, and why (2)? 3.2 Would the same summons type apply if the items in the locked room had commercial value (2)? 3.3 Which court has jurisdiction (2)? 3.4 Draw the particulars of claim and prayers, including all recoverable amounts (15). 3.5 Name and motivate the appropriate costs order (2). 3.6 If Mrs Blue refuses to leave, what remedy is available and under which Act (2)? 3.7 Draw the Notice of Motion to have Mrs Blue vacate (10).

## Q4 — Maya Burke: domestic abuse remedy (10)

> [!question] Question
> 4.1 Advise Ms Burke on the legal remedy/remedies available, naming the relevant Act(s), and describe the procedure and relief fully (9). 4.2 Which court has jurisdiction (1)?

> [!note]- Self-check pointers (not an official memo)
> See [[Domestic Violence Protection Orders]] — a protection order under the Domestic Violence Act 116 of 1998 (cohabitation qualifies as a "domestic relationship") is the primary remedy, heard in the Magistrates' Court, with an interim order available ex parte where there is a risk of harm; compare the near-identical fact pattern (an MMA-fighter ex-partner making threats) in [[BBST 2015(1) Statement of Facts Only]] Legal Issue 1.

## Q5 — Andrews: interdicting publication (15)

> [!question] Question
> 5.1 Draft the application to court, omitting headings and the notice's ending, including all necessary relief (5). 5.2 Draft Mr Andrews's founding affidavit in support (10).

> [!note]- Self-check pointers (not an official memo)
> See [[Urgent Applications and Interdicts]] and [[Defamation]] — a prior-restraint interdict against publication is a serious remedy that courts grant sparingly (freedom of expression considerations); the affidavit must establish urgency, a clear right, the falsity of the statements, that damages after the fact would be an inadequate remedy, and balance of convenience.
