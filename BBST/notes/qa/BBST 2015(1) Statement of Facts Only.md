## Statement of Facts

You are a candidate attorney in Bloemfontein at MM Marciano Incorporated, handling four legal issues across existing client files.

**Legal Issue 1 (20 marks in the mark scheme, though the questions themselves are lost) — domestic violence:** Pieter Meyer (42, manager at Birds and Bees Garden Services) has left his wife Rita and moved in with Joan Scheepers, a divorced nurse. Joan's ex-husband, Steve Scheepers — a professional MMA fighter who owns The MMA Gym — has twice broken into their home and assaulted both Pieter and Joan (most recently the day before the consultation), threatening to "come back and kill them both," and follows them to their workplaces.

**Legal Issue 2 (25 marks) — divorce, accrual claim:** Pieter instituted divorce action against Rita in the High Court (June 2014). Rita, formerly a business analyst, resigned by agreement to raise their first child and is now unemployed and financially destitute. In her claim in reconvention she pleads the marriage is subject to the accrual system and seeks 50% of the amount by which Pieter's accrual exceeds hers.

**Legal Issue 3 (35 marks) — attorney's fees, default judgment, sequestration:** MM Marciano, Pieter's attorneys of 13 years, obtained default judgment against him (R175 277,57 professional fees plus interest and taxed costs of R4 389,39) after he stopped paying his account. A warrant of execution led to an informal payment arrangement (R6 000/month) which Pieter breached after paying only R24 000; a re-issued warrant could not be served as Pieter had vacated his address. MM Marciano launched — and obtained — a provisional sequestration order via substituted service. Pieter opposes final sequestration, explaining in his answering affidavit that he moved to protect his girlfriend (Joan) from her abusive ex-husband, not to evade the firm, and that his own property was separately sold in execution by Nedbank (leaving a R300 000 shortfall he is still paying down).

**Legal Issue 4 (20 marks) — lease arrears and eviction:** A separate file shows Sure Rentals (Pty) Ltd suing B&B CC for arrear rental and eviction from commercial premises (written lease from 12 April 2013, R25 000/month, arrears from June 2014 to March 2015 = R250 000) plus unquantified damage from the tenant's unauthorised mechanical repair work on the premises. The file includes the full combined summons text, a Notice of Intention to Defend, and notices to amend/oppose amendment of the particulars of claim.

## Self-check pointers (no memo exists)

- Legal Issue 1 → [[Domestic Violence Protection Orders]] — evaluate a Protection from Harassment Act / Domestic Violence Act application against Steve Scheepers.
- Legal Issue 2 → [[Divorce Actions and Settlement Agreements]] — accrual-system claim mechanics; Rita's destitution is relevant to any interim maintenance pendente lite argument.
- Legal Issue 3 → [[Attorneys Fees and Fee Agreements]], [[Compulsory Sequestration and Acts of Insolvency]] — the absence of a written fee agreement barred attorney-and-client costs; evaluate whether Pieter's answering affidavit rebuts the presumption that his relocations were to evade creditors (an act of insolvency under s8 turns on *intent* to evade payment).
- Legal Issue 4 → [[Lease Agreements and Eviction]] — the combined summons in the facts is itself a useful precedent to study directly.
