1.1. Yes. Both Mr and Mrs Williams are entitled to compensation from the RAF for their personal injuries as these were caused by / arose out of the negligent driving of a motor vehicle and, in Mr William's case, the other driver was solely to blame for the relevant collision. (3)
1.2. Yes, there is a claim for compensation in respect of the son's injuries.

- A foetus in utero is regarded as a "person" for the purposes of S17(1)(a) and (b) of the Road Accident Fund Act. Our courts have held that insofar as the Act offered protection to the mother of the unborn child, such protection would extend to the child itself. [RAF v M obo M {2005} 3 A11 SA 340 (SCA); Mtati v RAF 2005(6) SA 215 (SCA)].
Either Mr or Mrs Williams may claim, in their representative capacity as parent and natural guardian, on behalf of their son, for his ante-natal injuries. (3)
