# QUESTION 15 [4]

Client should be advised to proceed by way of provisional sentence.

The court has to decide on the papers before it whether on a balance of probabilities the defendant would win the case. If not provisional sentence would be entered.

On the other hand if Summons is issued followed on an Appearance to Defend then an Application for Summary Judgment would be filed, the onus is far easier for the Defendant as he merely has to show that he has a bona fide defence to the action. (The answer may differ if the candidate refers to the Twee Jonge Gezellen (Pty) Ltd. versus Land and Agricultural Development Bank of South Africa 2011).
