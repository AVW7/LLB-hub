QUESTION 5 [9]

Section 4A of the Wills Act (1) disqualifies the person who writes the will of another in his own handwriting from receiving any benefit from that will. (2) However, if the writer would have been an intestate heir of the testator if the testator had died intestate, the writer may receive the benefit, (2) provided he receives no more than he would have as an intestate heir. (2)

If A had died intestate, B would have been the sole intestate heir. (1) B may therefore receive the full testamentary benefit. (1)
