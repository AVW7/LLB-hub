## For future agent & verification models

Index and verification manifest for the **16 Candidate Model Memoranda (2022–2023)** in `Attorneys Exam/Candidate Model Answers 2022-2023/`. Because no complete, freely downloadable official LSSA LEAD PDF memorandum was available in public media archives for the 2022–2023 sittings as of 2026-10-19, these 16 full 100-mark candidate model memoranda were drafted for multi-agent and multi-model verification, grounded in:
1. **50 verified official LSSA LEAD memoranda (2015–2021)** in `Attorneys Exam/Official Memoranda 2015-2021/` (many 2022–2023 questions are direct repeats or adaptations of 2015–2021 questions);
2. **Public YouTube walkthrough transcripts** by Simplified Academe for 16 March 2023 Paper 4 (`j9-V2vqmIVI`), 23 August 2023 Paper 2 (`LzLJMlF1WO4`), and 24 August 2023 Paper 4 (`9Xz3wTl1hbs`); and
3. **Indexed snippets from paywalled third-party listings** on Scribd and Studocu.

> [!warning] Provenance & Verification Boundary
> These files carry `official: false` and `verification-status: pending-multi-agent-review`. They do **not** replace official LSSA LEAD PDFs and are kept separate from `Attorneys Exam/Official Memoranda 2015-2021/`.
