  - **2.4** A registered **auditor / independent registered accountant** appointed by the firm (1).
  - **2.5** Under the Legal Practice Act 28 of 2014, audit fees for the annual trust audit are paid by the **firm itself** (historically recoverable from the **Legal Practitioners' Fidelity Fund (LPFF)** — credit either answer per examiner's discretion) (1).

    1. **Immediately rectify the deficit** by transferring the shortfall amount from the **Business banking account to the Trust banking account** (1½); and
    2. **Report the shortfall/deficit in writing to the Legal Practice Council (LPC)** (and reverse/correct the erroneous entry via a transfer/rectification journal) (1½).
  - **2.7** **No** — a referral legal practitioner (advocate taking briefs only from attorneys under s34(2)(a)(i) LPA) does not receive or hold trust money and is **exempt** from holding a Fidelity Fund Certificate (s84(2) LPA) (1).
