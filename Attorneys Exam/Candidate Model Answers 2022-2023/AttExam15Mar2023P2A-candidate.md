## QUESTION 4 [11]
- **Statutory source**: Section 1(1)(c), Section 1(4)(a), Section 1(4)(e), Section 1(6), and Section 1(7) of the Intestate Succession Act 81 of 1987, and the common-law *bloedige hand* maxim.
- **Model Answer**:
  - **1. Division of Joint Estate in Community of Property (2 marks)**:
    - The joint estate is **R1 650 500,00**.
    - Surviving spouse **Pearl** first takes her **50% half-share** by virtue of the marriage in community of property = `R1 650 500,00 ÷ 2` = **R825 250,00** (1).
    - The net intestate estate of Sizwe Shinga to be distributed is **R825 250,00** (1).
  - **2. Calculation of Child's Share & Spouse's Intestate Entitlement (s1(1)(c)(i)) (2 marks)**:
    - Sizwe has **three (3) stirpes of descendants** (Linda's stirps, Cynthia's stirps, and predeceased adopted daughter Gladys's stirps) plus his surviving spouse **Pearl** = **4 shares**.
    - A child's share = `R825 250,00 ÷ 4 = R206 312,50` (1).
    - Under Section 1(1)(c)(i), **Pearl** inherits a child's share (`R206 312,50`) or **R250 000,00**, whichever is the greater -> **Pearl inherits R250 000,00** from the intestate estate (1).
    - The remaining balance for the 3 descendant stirpes is `R825 250,00 - R250 000,00` = **R575 250,00** (`R575 250,00 ÷ 3 = R191 750,00` per stirps) (1).
  - **3. Distribution of the 3 Stirpes (`R191 750,00` each) & Explanations (6 marks)**:
    - **Stirps 1 — Linda & Sthembiso (2 marks)**:
      - **Linda (major son)** **does not inherit** because he murdered his father Sizwe and is disqualified as unworthy under the common-law maxim *de bloedige hand neemt geen erfenis* (1).
      - Under **Section 1(7)** of the Intestate Succession Act, the descendants of a disqualified heir inherit by representation as if the disqualified heir had predeceased the deceased; therefore, Linda's son **Sthembiso inherits R191 750,00** (1).
    - **Stirps 2 — Cynthia & Fano (2 marks)**:
      - **Cynthia (illegitimate major daughter)**: Birth out of wedlock does not affect capacity to inherit (s1(2)), but because Cynthia is a major and **renounced (repudiated) her inheritance** while the deceased is survived by a spouse (**Pearl**), **Section 1(6)** of the Intestate Succession Act provides that her **R191 750,00 share accrues to the surviving spouse, Pearl** (1), and Cynthia's son **Fano does not inherit** (1).
    - **Stirps 3 — Predeceased Adopted Daughter Gladys & Adopted Son Harry (2 marks)**:
      - **Gladys** predeceased her father and cannot inherit personally (½).
      - Under **Section 1(4)(a) and 1(4)(e)**, an adopted child is deemed for all purposes of intestate succession to be the biological descendant of his adoptive parent; therefore, Gladys's adopted son **Harry inherits R191 750,00 by representation** (1½).
  - **Summary of Final Totals**:
    - **Pearl (surviving wife)**: `R825 250,00 (50% COP) + R250 000,00 (s1(1)(c)(i)) + R191 750,00 (s1(6) accrual from Cynthia)` = **R1 267 000,00**.
    - **Sthembiso**: **R191 750,00**.
    - **Harry**: **R191 750,00**.
    - *(Total = `1 267 000 + 191 750 + 191 750 = R1 650 500,00`).*
