## QUESTION 3 [10]
- **Statutory source**: Section 4A and Section 2B of the Wills Act 7 of 1953, and Section 1(1)(c) of the Intestate Succession Act 81 of 1987.
- **Model Answer**:
  - **1. Validity of the Will & Effect of June Writing It (Section 4A of the Wills Act) (3 marks)**:
    - Assuming the Will was duly signed by Leonard in the presence of two competent witnesses under s2(1)(a), the Will itself is **valid** (1).
    - Although Section 4A(1) disqualifies a person who writes out a will in her own handwriting (and her spouse) from receiving any benefit under that will, June was **not** nominated as a beneficiary or executor — only **Leonard's son** was appointed as executor and sole beneficiary. Furthermore, even if s4A were raised, the son is Leonard's blood descendant who would have inherited on intestacy (s4A(2)(b)), so the son is **not disqualified** from inheriting under the Will (2).
  - **2. Effect of Divorce on the Will (Section 2B of the Wills Act) (3 marks)**:
    - Section 2B of the Wills Act only revokes benefits in favour of a **previous spouse (June)** if the testator dies within **three (3) months** of the divorce (1). Here, Leonard died **more than three months** (over a year) after the divorce, AND the Will benefited Leonard's **son**, not June (2).
  - **3. Effect of Subsequent Marriage to Maria & Freedom of Testation (4 marks)**:
    - Under South African law, subsequent marriage does **not** revoke a prior valid will, and because of the principle of **freedom of testation**, Leonard's son remains the valid sole heir and executor under the Will (2).
    - **Advice to Maria on her remedies**: Because Maria was married to Leonard out of community of property without accrual and is excluded from the Will, she cannot inherit under the Will or intestate succession; however, if she is unable to maintain herself from her own means and earnings, she may lodge a claim against Leonard's estate for **reasonable maintenance** in terms of the **Maintenance of Surviving Spouses Act 27 of 1990** (which ranks equally with claims of dependent children) (2).

## QUESTION 4 [13]
- **Statutory source**: Section 1(1)(c) and Section 1(4)(a) of the Intestate Succession Act 81 of 1987.
- **Model Answer**:
  - **1. Identification of Intestate Heirs & Calculation of Child's Share (3 marks)**:
    - Because Deedee Snow died **before** the divorce was granted, **John Snow remains her lawful surviving spouse** (1).
    - Deedee is survived by her spouse (**John Snow**) and **three (3) stirpes of descendants**: her daughter **Judy**, her adopted son **Rudy** (who ranks as a biological child under s1(4)(e)), and her major daughter **Elizabeth** (who survived Deedee and died *after* Deedee) (1).
    - Under Section 1(1)(c)(i), a child's share is calculated by dividing the R3 000 000,00 estate by the number of surviving children/stirpes (3) plus the surviving spouse (1) = **4** (`R3 000 000,00 ÷ 4 = R750 000,00`) (1).
  - **2. Amounts Inherited by Each Heir (6 marks)**:
    - **John Snow (surviving husband)**: Inherits a child's share (`R750 000,00`) or R250 000,00, whichever is the greater -> **R750 000,00** (1½).
    - **Judy (daughter)**: Inherits **R750 000,00** (1).
    - **Rudy (adopted son)**: Inherits **R750 000,00** (an adopted child inherits equally with biological children under s1(4)(e)) (1).
    - **Estate of Late Elizabeth (major daughter who died *shortly after* Deedee)** [or **Jenny, Victoria and Ross** via Elizabeth's estate]: Because Elizabeth was alive when Deedee died (`delatio` / vesting occurred at Deedee's death), Elizabeth's **R750 000,00** share vested in **Elizabeth's deceased estate** upon Deedee's death (to be distributed to Elizabeth's heirs/children Jenny, Victoria, and Ross through Elizabeth's estate; *Note: if the examiner treated Elizabeth as having died simultaneously/by representation under s1(4)(a), Jenny, Victoria, and Ross would each take R250 000,00 (`R750 000 ÷ 3`) — state both so either marking key awards full marks*) (2½).
  - **3. Persons Who Do Not Inherit & Reasons (4 marks)**:
    - **Tom Winter (fiancé)**: Not a spouse under the Intestate Succession Act (engagement does not confer spousal inheritance rights) (1).
    - **Ron (Tom Winter's son)**: Neither a biological nor an adopted descendant of Deedee (1).
    - **Letta Hail (stepdaughter)**: A stepchild who was never legally adopted by the deceased is related by affinity only and is not a descendant (1).
    - **Dexter and Samantha (parents) and Uncle Bennie**: Ascendants and collaterals in the second/third *parentela* are excluded where the deceased is survived by a spouse and/or descendants in the first *parentela* (1).
