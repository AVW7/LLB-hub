## QUESTION 4 [10]
- **Statutory source**: Financial Intelligence Centre Act 38 of 2001 (ss22–23, 29, 42); Legal Practice Act 28 of 2014 (ss86–87) & LPC Rules (Rule 54).
- **Model Answer**:
  - **4.1** **(e) (a) & (b)** *(Note: under FICA ss22–23 the statutory record-keeping period is **5 years** [whereas LPC Rule 54.9.1 is 7 years for accounting records] and FICA s42A/43 requires a Compliance Officer rather than an "office manager", making **(e)** strictly accurate; if the examiner equated FICA retention with 7 years and Compliance Officer with (d), **(f)** is the secondary alternative)* (2).
  - **4.2** **(d) (a) & (b)** *(or **(f) All of the above** if referring to the 5% LPFF share on s86(4) paid when called up — under LPC Rule 54.14.16.1 & 54.14.16.3, 100% of s86(2) and s86(3) interest is paid monthly to the LPFF, while 5% of s86(4) interest is paid to the LPFF when the investment is called up/paid)* (2).
  - **4.3** **(a) Be recorded in the financial records** *(Under LPC Rule 54.14.16.4, unknown trust money is paid over to the LPFF after the **second** closing of the accounting records [not the first closing as in (b)], and under s87(4)(b) LPA the owner is **never** deprived of his right to claim a refund from the LPFF)* (2).
  - **4.4** **(f) All the above** (LPC Rule 54.6 / s87(1) LPA requires proper accounting records for business account money, s86 trust money and interest, deceased estates, and insolvent estates) (2).
  - **4.5** **(f) (b) & (c)** (On earlier termination of any mandate and after completion of any mandate — see [[Attorneys Exam/Official Memoranda 2015-2021/AttExam5Nov2020P4A]] Q1.4) (2).
