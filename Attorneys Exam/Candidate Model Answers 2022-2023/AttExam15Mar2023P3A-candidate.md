## QUESTION 1 [35]
- **Precedent grounding**: [[Attorneys Exam/Official Memoranda 2015-2021/AttExam20Oct2021P3A]] Section A Q1.4, Q2, Q3; Contingency Fees Act 66 of 1997; Matrimonial Property Act 88 of 1984 s4(1)(b)(i).
- **Model Answer**:
  - **1.1 Contingency Fee Agreement (3 marks)**:
    - A **Contingency Fee Agreement** in terms of the **Contingency Fees Act 66 of 1997** (1).
    - It is a written agreement on a "no win, no fee" basis in terms of which the legal practitioner undertakes not to charge any fees unless the client is successful in the proceedings (1), and if successful, the practitioner may charge either his normal fee or a higher **success fee** (up to double the normal fee, capped at 25% of the capital amount awarded or recovered, whichever is the lesser) (1).
  - **1.2 Calculation of Fee Entitlement (3 marks)**:
    - Total capital award recovered by Mrs Gold = `R325 000,00 (special damages) + R250 000,00 (general damages)` = **R575 000,00** (½).
    - **Double the normal fee** = `2 × R65 000,00` = **R130 000,00** (1).
    - **25% cap on the award** = `25% × R575 000,00` = **R143 750,00** (½).
    - Because the practitioner is entitled to the **lesser** of double the normal fee or 25% of the award (Section 2(2) of the Contingency Fees Act), you are entitled to **R130 000,00** (1).
  - **1.3 Do the Proceeds Form Part of the Accrual? (4 marks)**:
    - **General damages (R250 000,00)**: **No** (1) — in terms of **Section 4(1)(b)(i) of the Matrimonial Property Act 88 of 1984**, any amount which accrued to a spouse by way of damages **other than damages for patrimonial loss** (i.e. non-patrimonial / general damages) is **excluded** from the accrual of her estate (1).
    - **Special damages (R325 000,00)**: **Yes** (1) — because special damages compensate for **patrimonial loss** (medical expenses and loss of earnings), the R325 000,00 is **included** in the accrual of Mrs Gold's estate (1).
  - **1.4 Recovery of Costs upon Premature Termination of Mandate by Client (2 marks)**:
    - **Yes** (1) — if the client prematurely terminates a contingency fee mandate without good cause before finalisation, the attorney is entitled to recover his **normal attorney-and-client fees and disbursements** incurred for the work actually performed up to the date of termination (normally provided for in the cancellation clause of the statutory contingency fee agreement) (1).
  - **1.5 Forum / Office for Relief Against Excessive Fees (1 mark)**:
    - The **Legal Practice Council (LPC)** (specifically a fee assessment panel/committee under Section 3(3) of the Contingency Fees Act / LPC Rules) or the **Taxing Master of the High Court / Magistrate's Court** (for taxation of the bill of costs) (1).
  - **1.6 Four Instances Where a Legal Practitioner May Refuse a Mandate (4 marks)** — Precedent [[Attorneys Exam/Official Memoranda 2015-2021/AttExam20Oct2021P3A]] Sec A Q1.4:
    1. Where accepting the mandate would create a **conflict of interest** (e.g. against an existing client) (1);
    2. Where the practitioner **lacks the necessary expertise, skill, experience, or time/capacity** to handle the matter competently (1);
    3. Where the client instructs the practitioner to **do or assist in something illegal, fraudulent, improper, or unethical** (or purely to harass/abuse court process) (1);
    4. Where the client **refuses or is unable to agree on fees or provide a reasonable deposit/cover** for fees and disbursements (1).
  - **1.7 Two Instances Where a Legal Practitioner May Be Obliged to Accept a Mandate (2 marks)**:
    1. Where the practitioner is **assigned by the Court (or the Legal Practice Council)** to represent an indigent or *pro deo* / *in forma pauperis* litigant or accused person (1);
    2. Where an **existing client faces an urgent situation** (or an existing matter requires urgent steps to prevent prescription or irreparable harm) and cannot timeously secure another practitioner (1).
  - **1.8 Document to Avoid Disputes Over Fees and Instructions (1 mark)**:
    - A written **Fee and Mandate Agreement (Letter of Engagement)** signed by the client (1).
  - **1.9 Draft Fee and Mandate Agreement (Costs and Default Recourse Clauses) (12 marks)**:
    > **FEE AND MANDATE AGREEMENT**
    > **1. SCOPE OF MANDATE AND AUTHORITY [2]**
    > The Client (**Mrs Gold**) hereby instructs and authorises **[Firm Name]** ("the Firm") to institute and prosecute a claim against the Road Accident Fund arising from the motor vehicle collision on 24 December [Year].
    >
    > **2. BASIS OF FEES AND DISBURSEMENTS [5]**
    > 2.1 The Client agrees to remunerate the Firm on an **attorney-and-own-client scale** at the following agreed hourly rates: [e.g. Partner/Director: R2 500,00 per hour; Associate: R1 500,00 per hour; Candidate Attorney: R800,00 per hour], calculated in 6-minute units for consultations, drafting, perusing, telephone calls, and court appearances, plus Value-Added Tax (VAT) at 15% (2).
    > 2.2 The Client acknowledges that the agreed attorney-and-own-client tariff **exceeds the prescribed party-and-party court tariff**, and that even if the Client succeeds and recovers taxed party-and-party costs from the opponent, the Client remains personally liable for the difference between the Firm's attorney-and-own-client bill and the party-and-party costs recovered (2).
    > 2.3 In addition to fees, the Client is liable for all **disbursements** incurred on her behalf (including Sheriff's charges, Counsel's fees, medico-legal expert reports, and actuarial fees), and undertakes to pay deposits into the Firm's trust account on request to cover anticipated fees and disbursements (1).
    >
    > **3. INTERIM BILLING, DEFAULT IN PAYMENT AND RIGHT OF RECOURSE [5]**
    > 3.1 The Firm shall render monthly/interim statements of account, which are payable within **30 (thirty) days** of invoice date, and the Client authorises the Firm to transfer any available trust funds held on her behalf towards settled statements of account (1).
    > 3.2 Should the Client fail to pay any statement of account or requested deposit on due date, the Firm shall be entitled:
    >    (a) to charge **interest** on overdue amounts at the prescribed legal rate (1);
    >    (b) to **withdraw as attorney of record** upon reasonable written notice to the Client (1);
    >    (c) to exercise a **right of retention (debtor-and-creditor lien)** over all documents drafted by the Firm or on which the Firm bestowed skill and labour until its fees and disbursements are paid or secured (1); and
    >    (d) to have its bill of costs **taxed** by the Taxing Master and institute legal proceedings against the Client to recover the taxed amount together with costs on the attorney-and-client scale (1).
  - **1.10 Clause Where Client is a Close Corporation (Suretyship / Personal Liability of Members) (3 marks)**:
    > **SURETYSHIP BY MEMBERS OF CLOSE CORPORATION**
    > By their signature hereto, the undersigned member(s) of the Client Close Corporation (**[Name] CC**) hereby interpose and bind themselves, **jointly and severally, as sureties for and co-principal debtors *in solidum* with** the Close Corporation (2) in favour of the Firm for the due and punctual payment of all fees, disbursements, and VAT owing by the Close Corporation under this Mandate Agreement, renouncing the benefits of excussion and division (1).

## QUESTION 2 [5]
- **Statutory source**: Section 30(3) and Section 84 of the Legal Practice Act 28 of 2014 & LPC Rules.
- **Model Answer**:
  - Failure by a legal practitioner to pay annual membership fees/subscriptions prescribed by the Legal Practice Council despite lawful demand constitutes **unprofessional conduct** and a ground for administrative/disciplinary action (1).
  - Under **Section 30(3)(e) of the Legal Practice Act 28 of 2014**, the LPC may cancel or **suspend Mr Sunshine's enrolment** (or apply to the High Court to suspend him from practice or strike his name from the Roll) if he fails to pay any prescribed annual fee or contribution within the stipulated time after demand (2).
  - Furthermore, while his annual fees remain unpaid, the LPC will **refuse to issue him a Fidelity Fund Certificate (FFC)** under Section 85 (1). Without a valid FFC, Mr Sunshine **may not practise for his own account, receive trust money, or recover any fees** in respect of work performed while uncertified (s84(1)–(3) LPA) (1).

### 3.1 Suspensive Condition Clause (Operation Dependent on Bond) [5]
> **SUSPENSIVE CONDITION (MORTGAGE BOND)**
> This agreement of sale is **subject to the suspensive condition** that the Purchaser (**Adam Sandler**) obtains approval in writing for a loan of **R950 000,00 (Nine Hundred and Fifty Thousand Rand)** from a registered bank or financial institution against the security of a first mortgage bond to be registered over the Property (**Erf 888, Neverland**) (2), within **30 (thirty) days** from the date of signature of this agreement (or such extended period as the parties may agree in writing) (1). Should the said loan not be approved within the stipulated period, this agreement shall be **null and void *ab initio*** (1) and neither party shall have any claim against the other arising herefrom, save that the Seller shall refund any deposit paid by the Purchaser (1).

### 3.2 Resolutive Condition Clause (Agreement Terminates Upon Bond Refusal) [5]
> **RESOLUTIVE CONDITION (MORTGAGE BOND)**
> This agreement shall come into full force and effect immediately upon signature hereof (1), provided that **should the Purchaser be advised in writing** by a bank or financial institution that his application for a mortgage loan of **R950 000,00** over **Erf 888, Neverland** has been **refused**, or should the loan not be granted within **30 (thirty) days** from date of signature hereof (2), then this agreement shall **immediately terminate and cease to have any further force or effect** (1), and each party shall be obliged to restore the other to the same position he would have been in had this agreement never been concluded (1).

## QUESTION 5 [4]
- **Statutory source**: Section 192(1) and (2) of the Labour Relations Act 66 of 1995.
- **Model Answer**:
  - **Employee's Onus (s192(1))**: In any proceedings concerning any dismissal, the **employee must establish the existence of the dismissal** on a balance of probabilities (2).
  - **Employer's Onus (s192(2))**: Once the existence of the dismissal is established, the **employer must prove that the dismissal is fair** — both **substantively fair** (for a fair reason relating to conduct, capacity, or operational requirements under s188(1)(a)) and **procedurally fair** (in accordance with a fair procedure under s188(1)(b)) (2).

## QUESTION 7 [14]
- **Precedent grounding**: LSSA Commercial Contract Drafting precedents (Deed of Suretyship clauses).

### 7.1 Clause on Procedure in Respect of Disputing a Claim (Pay Now, Argue Later / Certificate of Balance) [10]
> **DISPUTE OF CLAIM AND CERTIFICATE OF INDEBTEDNESS**
> 1. A certificate signed by any director, manager, or auditor of the Creditor stating the amount owing by the Principal Debtor and/or the Surety to the Creditor at any date, and the fact that such amount is due and payable, shall be *prima facie* proof of the contents thereof for all purposes, including summary judgment and provisional sentence (3).
> 2. Should the Surety dispute the existence or quantum of any claim by the Creditor under this Deed of Suretyship:
>    2.1 the Surety shall deliver written notice of such dispute, setting out full reasons and calculations, to the Creditor within **seven (7) days** of demand (2);
>    2.2 the Surety shall **not be entitled to withhold, delay, or set off payment** of the amount claimed pending resolution of the dispute, and shall first pay the full amount claimed by the Creditor in accordance with the certificate ("pay now, argue later") (3); and
>    2.3 only after making full payment to the Creditor may the Surety institute proceedings or refer the dispute to arbitration to reclaim any alleged overpayment (2).

### 7.2 Clause on Effect of Amendment, Indulgence, or Novation in a Deed of Suretyship [4]
> **AMENDMENT, INDULGENCE AND NOVATION**
> 1. This Deed of Suretyship shall remain in full force and effect as a **continuing covering security**, and the Surety's liability hereunder shall **not be released, discharged, or prejudiced** by any **amendment, variation, extension of time, indulgence, compromise, or novation** of the principal debt or principal agreement agreed between the Creditor and the Principal Debtor, with or without notice to or consent of the Surety (2).
> 2. No variation, amendment, cancellation, or consensual release of this Deed of Suretyship itself shall be of any force or effect unless reduced to **writing and signed by both the Creditor and the Surety** (in compliance with Section 6 of the General Law Amendment Act 50 of 1956) (2).

## QUESTION 8 [18]
- **Precedent grounding**: Standard conveyancing *voetstoots* / extent clause & Consumer Protection Act 68 of 2008 non-applicability warranty clause.

### 8.1 *Voetstoots* (Condition and Extent of Property) Clause [10]
> **VOETSTOOTS AND EXTENT OF PROPERTY**
> 1. The Property (**Kingdom Palace**) is sold **_voetstoots_** ("as it stands") and as it is described in the existing title deed thereof, subject to all conditions and servitudes mentioned or referred to in the title deed or imposed by law (2).
> 2. The Seller (**Mr Hogwart**) gives no warranty, express or implied, regarding the physical condition, state of repair, zoning, or fitness for any purpose of the Property or any buildings and improvements thereon, and shall not be liable for any **patent or latent defects** in the Property (3).
> 3. The Purchaser (**Mr Potter**) acknowledges that he has inspected the Property prior to signing this agreement and is fully acquainted with its nature, condition, beacons, and boundaries, and warrants that he was not induced to enter into this agreement by any representation made by or on behalf of the Seller (2).
> 4. The Seller shall not be liable for any **deficiency in the extent** of the Property which may be revealed upon any resurvey, and conversely the Seller renounces any claim to any **excess in the extent** of the Property (3).

### 8.2 Consumer Protection Act 68 of 2008 Non-Applicability Clause [8]
> **NON-APPLICABILITY OF THE CONSUMER PROTECTION ACT 68 OF 2008**
> 1. The Seller (**Mr Hogwart**) and the Purchaser (**Mr Potter**) hereby record, warrant, and confirm that:
>    1.1 the Seller does **not** sell immovable property in the **ordinary course of his business** (within the meaning of Section 5 of the Consumer Protection Act 68 of 2008 ("the CPA")), and this sale is a once-off private transaction (3); and/or
>    1.2 [if the Purchaser is a juristic person] the Purchaser is a juristic person whose asset value or annual turnover at the time of this transaction equals or exceeds the statutory threshold of R2 000 000,00 prescribed under Section 5(2)(b) of the CPA (2).
> 2. Consequently, the parties agree and acknowledge that the **Consumer Protection Act 68 of 2008 is not applicable** to this transaction, and that the Purchaser relies no right or implied warranty under Sections 55 and 56 of the CPA (3).
