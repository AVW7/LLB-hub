4.3 Mr Strong requests that Mr Cloudy and Mr Sunny, who are both directors of
Setting Sun (Pty) Ltd. will have to sign as sureties for the fulfillment of
the obligations as per the lease agreement. Draft the following clauses that
you would insert in the deed of surety: 


4.3.1  Draft only the paragraph in the deed of surety making provision for the
