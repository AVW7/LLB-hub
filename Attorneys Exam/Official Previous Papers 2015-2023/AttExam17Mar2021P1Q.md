QUESTION 1 [10]

known registration details, skipped a red robot and collided into his motor
vehicle. As a result hereof, Mr Dlamini sustained fatal injuries and died at
the scene of the collision. During his lifetime Mr Dlamini was a self-employed
businessman.

loss of support from the Road Accident Fund for herself and her 3 (three)

in establishing the merits or liability of the matter. (5)
