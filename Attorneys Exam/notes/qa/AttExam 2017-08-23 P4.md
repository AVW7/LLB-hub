## Q1 — Supplementary Business Cash Book & Bank Reconciliation Statement (15)

> [!question] Question
> At 28 February 2016, your Business Cash Book shows an unfavourable (overdraft) balance of R10 000, while the Bank Statement shows an overdraft of R28 455.
> - Items on Bank Statement not in Cash Book: direct deposit R3 000; deposit understatement error R7 000; RD cheque R26 000; interest on overdraft R3 000; bank charges R4 000; stop order R7 000.
> - Items not on Bank Statement / Bank errors: two erroneous bank debits of R10 000 and R12 000; outstanding deposit of R10 545.
> Prepare the Supplementary Business Cash Book (9) and the Business Bank Reconciliation Statement at 28 February 2016 (6).

> [!success]- Answer
> - **1.1 Supplementary Business Cash Book (28 Feb 2016)**:
>   - **Receipts (Dr)**: Direct deposit R3 000 + Deposit error R7 000 + **Closing Overdraft Balance R40 000** = **R50 000**.
>   - **Payments (Cr)**: Opening Overdraft Balance R10 000 + RD Cheque R26 000 + Interest paid R3 000 + Bank charges R4 000 + Stop order R7 000 = **R50 000** (9).
> - **1.2 Business Bank Reconciliation Statement (28 Feb 2016)**:
>   - Balance as per Cash Book (O/D): `(R40 000)`
>   - Less Bank error: `+R10 000` → `(R30 000)`
>   - Less Bank error: `+R12 000` → `(R18 000)`
>   - Less Outstanding deposit: `-R10 545`
>   - **Balance as per Bank Statement (O/D)**: **`(R28 455)`** (6).

## Q2 — Instructing Attorney Recording Correspondent Collection & 1/3 Allowance (30)

> [!question] Question
> You act for client Black and instruct a Correspondent to collect R18 000 from debtor White. Correspondent collects R18 000, charges gross fees & disbursements of R6 472 (including a 1/3 fee allowance to you of R1 600 + R224 VAT = R1 824), and remits a net cheque of R13 352 (`R18 000 - R6 472 + R1 824`). You bank the cheque, transfer your allowance, and pay Black the net R11 528 (`R18 000 - R6 472`). Record all Cash Books, Ledgers, and Journals.

> [!success]- Answer
> - **Trust Journal**:
>   1. `Dr Correspondent (T) R18 000 / Cr Black (T) R18 000` (capital collected ex White).
>   2. `Dr Black (T) R6 472 / Cr Correspondent (T) R6 472` (correspondent's gross fees & disbursements).
> - **Fees Journal (Business)**: `Dr Correspondent (B) R1 824 / Cr Fees R1 600 & Cr Output VAT R224` (⅓ allowance).
> - **Trust Cash Book**: Receipt from Correspondent **R13 352**; Payments: Transfer to Business **R1 824**, Cheque to Black **R11 528**.
> - **Transfer Journal**: `Dr Correspondent (T) R1 824 / Cr Correspondent (B) R1 824` (transfer ⅓ allowance).

## Q3 — Trust Deposit, Revenue Stamps, Fees & Accounting Statement: Client David (25)

> [!question] Question
> Client David deposits R50 000 into trust for the registration of a trust and preparation of an estate plan. You issue revenue stamps of R1 800 from business stock, charge R30 000 fees + R4 200 VAT (= R34 200), transfer your fees and disbursements from Trust to Business, and remit the R14 000 balance to David. Record the Cash Books, Ledgers, Journals, and Accounting Statement.

> [!success]- Answer
> - **Trust Cash Book & David (T)**: Receipt of R50 000 deposit; Payments: Transfer to Business **R36 000** (`R34 200 + R1 800`) and refund cheque to David **R14 000**.
> - **Journals**:
>   - *Fees Journal*: `Dr David (B) R34 200 / Cr Fees R30 000 & Cr Output VAT R4 200`.
>   - *Business Journal*: `Dr David (B) R1 800 / Cr Revenue Stamps R1 800`.
>   - *Transfer Journal*: `Dr David (T) R36 000 / Cr David (B) R36 000`.
> - **Accounting Statement**: Deposit Cr R50 000; Revenue stamps Dr R1 800, Fees Dr R30 000, VAT Dr R4 200, Cheque herewith Dr R14 000 (Total R50 000 / R50 000).

## Q4 — Section 78(2)(a) [s86(3)] vs Section 78(2A) [s86(4)] Investments & Trust Reconciliation (30)

> [!question] Question
> You receive trust deposits from Reddy (R400 000, invested on written mandate earning R22 000 interest), Van Der Merwe (R1 000 000), Ngubane (R685 000), Adams (R300 000), and Khoza (R500 000, paid out to Seller). You invest R1 500 000 of surplus general trust funds in Nedbank under s78(2)(a) [now s86(3)], which matures with R15 000 interest. Both investments are uplifted before month-end, Reddy is paid his R22 000 interest, and the R15 000 s78(2)(a) interest is paid to the Fidelity Fund.
> 4.1 Record the Trust Cash Book and Trust Ledgers. (25)
> 4.2 Extract the Trust Liabilities schedule and reconcile with Trust Funds Available. (5)

> [!success]- Answer
> - **4.1**:
>   - **s78(2)(a) [s86(3)] Nedbank Surplus Investment**: `Dr Cash R1 500 000` and `Dr Cash – Fidelity Fund R15 000` / `Cr Cash R1 515 000` (100% of interest on surplus general trust investment goes to the Fidelity Fund).
>   - **s78(2A) [s86(4)] Bank Investment (Reddy)**: `Dr Cash R400 000` and `Dr Reddy R22 000` / `Cr Cash R422 000` (interest credited to client Reddy and paid out to him; note under current s86(5) LPA 5% goes to LPFF).
> - **4.2 Trust Reconciliation**:
>   - **Trust Liabilities (Creditors)**: Reddy R400 000 + Van Der Merwe R1 000 000 + Ngubane R685 000 + Adams R300 000 (Khoza = R0) = **R2 385 000**.
>   - **Trust Funds Available**: Trust Cash Book closing balance = **R2 385 000**.
