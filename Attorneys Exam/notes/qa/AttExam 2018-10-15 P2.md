## Q1 — First and Final Liquidation and Distribution Account: Estate Late Anthony Naidoo (63)

> [!question] Question
> Anthony Naidoo (ID 650425 0099 088) died intestate on 9 August 2018, married out of community of property with the accrual system to his sole surviving heir Vanessa Naidoo (no descendants). Master's reference: 1854/2018.
> Assets: Farm 188 Avos, Pretoria (1600 ha, T1346/1999) valued at R1 500 000; furniture R213 000; 9mm Browning pistol R4 050; EBS Ltd shares valued at R146 000 (plus R13 950 post-death dividend); Toyota RunX sold for R120 000; Liberty Life policy payable to estate R200 000; shares in ARL (Pty) Ltd sold for R142 000 (auditor valuation R147 000); ornaments & jewellery sold for R165 000; VB-Bank investment R800 000 + R9 950 interest at death (plus R6 050 post-death interest). Deemed property also includes Old Mutual policy payable to Thillay (R780 000; beneficiary paid premiums + 6% interest of R80 000). Vanessa has an accrual claim against the estate of R105 000.
> Draft the complete First and Final Liquidation and Distribution Account, including the Estate Duty Addendum and Executor's Certificate.

> [!success]- Answer
> - **Liquidation Account — Assets**:
>   - Immovable: Farm 188 Avos (**R1 500 000.00**).
>   - Movables awarded to Vanessa Naidoo under s1(1)(a): Furniture (**R213 000.00**), 9mm Parabellum (**R4 050.00**), EBS Ltd shares (**R146 000.00**) = R363 050.00.
>   - Cash & Assets Reduced to Cash: Toyota RunX (**R120 000.00**), Liberty Life policy (**R200 000.00**), ARL (Pty) Ltd shares (**R142 000.00**), Jewellery (**R165 000.00**), VB-Bank capital (**R800 000.00**) + pre-death interest (**R9 950.00**) = **R1 436 950.00**.
>   - **Total Assets**: **R3 300 000.00**.
> - **Liquidation Account — Liabilities**:
>   - Admin costs (s29 & s35 adverts R700, Master's fee R6 400, Executor's fee 3.5% on R3.3m = **R115 500.00**, valuations R7 600 + R9 500 + R7 300, conveyancer R15 340, bank charges R8 000 + R6 000, postages R260 = R176 600) + Funeral expenses (**R18 400**) + Absa overdraft (**R200 000**) + **Accrual claim against deceased's estate (R105 000.00)** = **Total Liabilities R500 000.00**.
>   - **Balance for Distribution**: **R2 800 000.00** awarded to Vanessa Naidoo as sole heir under s1(1)(a) of Act 81 of 1987 (Immovable R1 500 000 + Movable R363 050 + Cash surplus R936 950).
> - **Income & Expenditure Account**: Post-death interest (R6 050) + dividend (R13 950) = **R20 000.00**; less 6% Executor's fee (**R1 200.00**) = **R18 800.00** to Vanessa Naidoo.
> - **Estate Duty Addendum**:
>   - Assets per L&D (R3 300 000) less Liberty policy (R200 000) + ARL (Pty) Ltd s5 valuation difference (+R5 000) = **R3 105 000.00** s3(2) property.
>   - Add s3(3) Deemed Property: Liberty policy (R200 000) + Old Mutual policy to Thillay (R780 000 less R80 000 premiums & 6% interest = R700 000) = **R900 000.00**.
>   - **Gross Value**: **R4 005 000.00**.
>   - Less s4 Deductions: s4(a) funeral (R18 400) + s4(b) overdraft (R200 000) + s4(c) admin costs (R176 600) + **s4(lA) accrual claim (R105 000)** [= R500 000 total liabilities] + **s4(q) surviving spouse inheritance (R2 800 000)** = **R3 300 000.00**.
>   - **Net Value**: **R705 000.00** less s4A primary rebate (**R3 500 000.00**) = **R0.00 Dutiable Amount**.

## Q2 — Intestate Succession: Joint Estate, Nasciturus, Murderer Son (s1(7)) & Renunciation (s1(6)) (13)

> [!question] Question
> Bruce dies intestate, married in community of property to Rose, who is pregnant at his death. The net joint estate is R1 200 000. Bruce had four children: Judas (who murdered Bruce, leaving a son Simon), Daniel, Vivian (who renounces her inheritance), and Dolly. Bruce's parents Moses and Joseph also survive him. Calculate how the estate devolves.

> [!success]- Answer
> - **Rose's ½ share by marriage in community of property**: **R600 000.00** (1).
> - **Deceased's ½ share (R600 000.00)**:
>   - **Scenario A — If the unborn child is born alive (nasciturus rule)**:
>     - 6 shares in divisor (Rose + Judas's line + Daniel + Vivian + Dolly + unborn child) (1).
>     - Child's share = `R600 000 ÷ 6 = R100 000.00` (1).
>     - **Rose** takes the greater of R250 000 or a child's share under s1(1)(c)(i) = **R250 000.00** (2).
>     - Remaining balance for 5 descendant lines = `R600 000 - R250 000 = R350 000` (**R70 000.00** per line).
>     - **Judas** is disqualified (*de bloedige hand erf niet*); under **s1(7)** his son **Simon** inherits his share by representation = **R70 000.00** (2).
>     - **Vivian** renounced; under **s1(6)** her R70 000 share vests in the surviving spouse **Rose**, bringing Rose's intestate inheritance to **R320 000.00** (plus her R600 000 ½ share = R920 000 total) (2).
>     - **Daniel, Dolly, and the unborn child** each inherit **R70 000.00** (1).
>   - **Scenario B — If the unborn child is NOT born alive**:
>     - Rose takes R250 000 + Vivian's renounced ¼ of R350 000 (R87 500) = **R337 500.00**; **Simon, Daniel, and Dolly** each inherit **R87 500.00** (2).
>   - **Moses and Joseph (parents)**: Inherit **nothing** because descendants exclude ascendants (1).

## Q3 — Massing Clause & Adiation Certificate with Attorney's Certificate (17)

> [!question] Question
> 3.1 Draft the massing clause in the joint will of Allan and Judy Wilson (married out of community of property), massing their separate estates on the death of the first-dying in favour of their son Joseph, subject to a lifelong usufruct for the survivor. (5)
> 3.2 Allan dies first and Judy elects to adiate. Draft the Adiation Certificate and the accompanying Attorney's Certificate by Robert Davids. (12)

> [!success]- Answer
> - **3.1 Massing Clause**: "We hereby mass our separate estates upon the death of the first-dying of us and direct that the so massed estates shall, upon the death of the first-dying, devolve upon our son Joseph, subject to the lifelong usufruct of the survivor of us." (5)
> - **3.2 Adiation Certificate**:
>   - Declaration by Judy Wilson (surviving spouse of late Allan Wilson) confirming awareness of the conditions of the joint Will dated 3 April 2015 constituting a massing of their separate estates, awareness of her right to accept (adiate) or reject (repudiate) after explanation of the consequences, and her election to **adiate**, forfeiting her own estate in favour of son Joseph while receiving a lifelong usufruct over both estates. Signed before 2 witnesses (7).
>   - **Attorney's Certificate**: Certificate by Robert Davids (attorney) confirming he explained to Judy Wilson the consequences of accepting or repudiating the joint Will, that she understood those consequences, and that after due consideration she elected to accept the Will. Signed before 2 witnesses (5).

## Q4 — Letter to the Master Requesting Extension to Lodge L&D Account (7)

> [!question] Question
> Draft a formal letter to the Master of the High Court requesting a 4-month extension to lodge the Liquidation and Distribution Account in the Estate Late Joel Steyn (No. 967/18), where SARS has not yet issued the final income tax assessment.

> [!success]- Answer
> Formal letter addressed to the Master of the High Court citing `RE: ESTATE LATE JOEL STEYN NO. 967/18`, requesting a 4-month extension beyond the due date (30 November 2018) and motivating per s35(2A):
> 1. Awaiting final income tax assessment from SARS (deceased was employed at death).
> 2. All assets have been valued and all cash collected.
> 3. Current balances held in the estate current bank account (R9 000) and estate savings account (R55 000) reserved for the tax liability.
> 4. Confirmation that the estate is solvent. (7)
