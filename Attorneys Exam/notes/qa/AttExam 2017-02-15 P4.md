## Q1 — Journal Entries (Transfer, Error Correction, Fee Reduction, Counsel Fees, Bad Debts) (15)

> [!question] Question
> Draft the journal entries (with narrations, 3 marks each) for:
> 1.1 Transferring R4 500 owed by Client from Trust to Business.
> 1.2 Reversing R7 500 erroneously debited to Poor instead of Rich in the Business Ledger.
> 1.3 Reducing an overcharged fee of R7 000 to Client.
> 1.4 Recording Counsel's fee statement of R5 000 in Mrs Cross's divorce action.
> 1.5 Writing off an irrecoverable business balance of R2 500 owed by Client.

> [!success]- Answer
> - **1.1 Transfer Journal**: `Dr Client (T) R4 500 / Cr Client (B) R4 500` — *Being transfer from Trust to Business* (3).
> - **1.2 Business Journal**: `Dr Rich (B) R7 500 / Cr Poor (B) R7 500` — *Being erroneous debit corrected* (3).
> - **1.3 Business Journal (Fee Journal)**: `Dr Fees R7 000 / Cr Client (B) R7 000` — *Being correction of overcharge* (3).
> - **1.4 Business Journal**: `Dr Mrs Cross (B) R5 000 / Cr Counsel (B) R5 000` — *Being fees due in divorce action* (3).
> - **1.5 Business Journal**: `Dr Bad Debts R2 500 / Cr Client (B) R2 500` — *Being amount written off* (3).

## Q2 — Supplementary Cash Book & Bank Reconciliation Statement (15)

> [!question] Question
> Given a provisional Trust Cash Book balance of R943 210 on 30 June 2016 and a Bank Statement overdraft of R319 580:
> - Unrecorded Cash Book items: interest credited R17 730; cheque error overstated by R109 340; direct deposit R128 820; cheque understatement error R104 930; RD cheque R21 780; deposit overstatement error R188 820.
> - Unrecorded Bank Statement items: unpresented cheques R179 980; outstanding deposit R81 190; business cheque erroneously debited to trust bank account R1 342 300; business deposit erroneously credited to trust bank account R40 360.
> 2.1 Prepare the Supplementary Cash Book for June 2016. (8)
> 2.2 Prepare the Bank Reconciliation Statement at 30 June 2016. (5)
> 2.3 How do you rectify the R1 342 300 business cheque paid out of the trust bank account? (2)

> [!success]- Answer
> - **2.1 Supplementary Cash Book (June 2016)**:
>   - **Receipts (Dr)**: Opening Balance R943 210 + Interest R17 730 + Cheque Error R109 340 + Direct Deposit R128 820 = **R1 199 100**.
>   - **Payments (Cr)**: Cheque Error R104 930 + RD Cheque R21 780 + Deposit Error R188 820 + **Closing Balance R883 570** = **R1 199 100** (8).
> - **2.2 Bank Reconciliation Statement at 30 June 2016**:
>   - Overdraft as per Bank Statement: `(R319 580)`
>   - Add unpresented cheques: `(R179 980)` → Subtotal `(R499 560)`
>   - Less outstanding deposit: `+R81 190` → Subtotal `(R418 370)`
>   - Less bank error (business cheque debited to trust): `+R1 342 300` → Favourable balance `R923 930`
>   - Less bank error (business deposit credited to trust): `-R40 360` → **Cash Book Balance R883 570** (5).
> - **2.3**: Instruct the bank to reverse the erroneous debit immediately, or transfer R1 342 300 from the Business bank account to the Trust bank account (2).

## Q3 — Conveyancing Transaction: Purchaser Trust Investment, Correspondent Allowance & Statement (50)

> [!question] Question
> Purchaser buys property for R3 000 000 (R1 000 000 cash deposit invested in Phoenix Bank under s78(2A)/s86(4) earning R5 800 interest + R2 000 000 bank guarantee; R200 000 paid for transfer & bond costs). Correspondent conveyancer pays R150 000 transfer duty and charges R40 000 fee + R5 600 VAT (= R45 600), granting you a ¼ allowance (R10 000 fee + R1 400 VAT = R11 400). Registration takes place on 26 February.
> Record the Cash Books, Trust & Business Ledgers, Business Journal entries, and the final Accounting Statement (`Transfer of Property`).

> [!success]- Answer
> - **Trust Cash Book**: Receipts: Purchaser deposit R1 000 000, Purchaser costs R200 000, Phoenix Bank maturity R1 005 800 (= R2 205 800). Payments: Phoenix Bank s78(2A) R1 000 000, Correspondent (Transfer Duty) R150 000, Correspondent (deposit to seller) R1 000 000, Transfer to Business R45 600, Purchaser interest refund R5 800, Purchaser cost refund R4 400 (= R2 205 800).
> - **Business Cash Book**: Receipt: Transfer ex Trust R45 600. Payment: Correspondent net remittance R34 200 (`R45 600 - R11 400 allowance`).
> - **Business Journals**:
>   1. `Dr Purchaser (B) R45 600 / Cr Correspondent (B) R45 600` (fees due to correspondent).
>   2. `Dr Correspondent (B) R11 400 / Cr Fees R10 000 & Cr Output VAT R1 400` (allowance on fees).
> - **Transfer of Property Statement**:
>   - Credits (Received): Bank guarantee proceeds R2 000 000 + Deposit R1 000 000 + Costs deposit R200 000 + Investment interest R5 800 = **R3 205 800**.
>   - Debits (Paid/Charged): Transfer duty R150 000 + Transfer fees R40 000 + VAT R5 600 + Paid Seller R3 000 000 + Interest cheque to Purchaser R5 800 + Refund cheque to Purchaser R4 400 = **R3 205 800**.

## Q4 — Trust Creditors List, Retention Period & Surplus Trust Investment Rules (20)

> [!question] Question
> 4.1 How often must a practitioner extract a list of trust creditors, what must it contain, where is it recorded, and how long must accounting records be retained? (12)
> 4.2 How may a practitioner invest trust funds not immediately required? (8)

> [!success]- Answer
> - **4.1**:
>   - (a) **Quarterly** (every 3 months) (2).
>   - (b) Name of each trust creditor, the credit balance due to each creditor, and the total of the list — which must be compared with the total trust funds available in the general trust bank account and trust investment accounts (6).
>   - (c) Recorded in a trial balance or permanent trust ledger/reconciliation record (2).
>   - (d) Retained for **5 years** from the date of the last entry (2).
> - **4.2**: Either obtain a **written instruction/mandate from the client** to invest in a dedicated client investment account (s86(4) LPA / s78(2A) Attorneys Act), OR invest surplus general trust monies in a **s86(3) LPA / s78(2)(a)** account where all interest accrues to the Fidelity Fund (8).
