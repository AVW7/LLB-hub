## Q1 — Supplementary Business Cash Book & Bank Reconciliation Statement (15)

> [!question] Question
> At 30 June 2018, the Business Cash Book has an unfavourable balance of R300 000.
> - Unrecorded Cash Book items: direct deposit R150 000; deposit understatement error R180 000; incorrect deposit credited in cash book R108 000; bank charges R5 600; interest on overdraft R16 200.
> - Reconciling items: bank error of R105 000 (overstating overdraft on bank statement) and an outstanding EFT payment of R160 000.
> Prepare the Supplementary Business Cash Book and Bank Reconciliation Statement as at 30 June 2018.

> [!success]- Answer
> - **Supplementary Business Cash Book (June 2018)**:
>   - **Receipts (Dr)**: Deposit R150 000 + Deposit error R180 000 + **Closing Overdraft Balance R99 800** = **R429 800**.
>   - **Payments (Cr)**: Opening Overdraft R300 000 + Incorrect deposit reversed R108 000 + Bank charges R5 600 + Interest R16 200 = **R429 800**.
> - **Bank Reconciliation Statement at 30 June 2018**:
>   - Balance as per Cash Book: `R99 800 (O/D)`
>   - Add Bank error: `R105 000` → Subtotal `R204 800 (O/D)`
>   - Less Outstanding EFT: `R160 000`
>   - **Balance as per Bank Statement**: **`R44 800 (O/D)`**.

## Q2 — Section 86(3) & Section 86(4) Investments, Trust Creditors & Available Trust Funds (40)

> [!question] Question
> Record the following transactions in the Trust Cash Book and Trust Ledger, then extract the List of Trust Creditors and reconcile with Trust Funds Available:
> - Sibusiso deposits R1 000 000 (invested under s86(4) at Nedbank; R250 000 later uplifted and R150 000 paid to Counsel).
> - Gloria deposits R2 500 000 purchase price (R2 000 000 invested under s86(4) at Capitec) + R250 000 costs (R200 000 paid to SARS for transfer duty).
> - Various sundry clients deposit R3 500 000; R3 000 000 of surplus trust money is invested at Absa under s86(3), of which R2 000 000 capital + R24 000 interest is uplifted and the R24 000 interest is paid to the Fidelity Fund.
> - Martin deposits R500 000; R296 000 is paid to a stockbroker for shares and R348 000 is received back from the stockbroker.

> [!success]- Answer
> - **Trust Cash Book Closing Balance**: Total Receipts **R10 372 000** less Total Payments **R6 670 000** = **R3 702 000**.
> - **2.2 List of Trust Creditors**:
>   - Sibusiso: `R1 000 000 - R150 000 = R850 000`
>   - Gloria: `R2 500 000 + R250 000 - R200 000 = R2 550 000`
>   - Sundry clients: `R3 500 000`
>   - Martin: `R500 000 - R296 000 + R348 000 = R552 000`
>   - **Total Trust Liabilities**: **R7 452 000**.
> - **2.3 Trust Funds Available**:
>   - As per Trust Cash Book: **R3 702 000**
>   - s86(4) Sibusiso (Nedbank): **R750 000** (`R1 000 000 - R250 000`)
>   - s86(4) Gloria (Capitec): **R2 000 000**
>   - s86(3) Absa surplus investment: **R1 000 000** (`R3 000 000 - R2 000 000`)
>   - **Total Trust Funds Available**: **R7 452 000**.

## Q3 — Trust Deposit, Third-Party Disbursement, 15% VAT Fee Transfer & Statement: Patricia (30)

> [!question] Question
> Patricia deposits R25 000 into trust. You pay R3 450 from trust to a Financial Adviser on her behalf, charge a fee of R10 000 plus 15% VAT (R1 500), transfer your fee and VAT from Trust to Business, and pay Patricia the balance of R10 050. Draft all Cash Books, Ledgers, Journals, and the Accounting Statement.

> [!success]- Answer
> - **Trust Cash Book & Patricia (T)**: Receipt R25 000; Payments: Financial Adviser R3 450, Transfer to Business R11 500, Patricia refund R10 050.
> - **Fees Journal**: `Dr Patricia (B) R11 500 / Cr Fees R10 000 & Cr Output VAT R1 500` (5).
> - **Transfer Journal**: `Dr Patricia (T) R11 500 / Cr Patricia (B) R11 500` (4).
> - **Accounting Statement**: Deposit Cr R25 000; Paid Financial Adviser Dr R3 450, Fees Dr R10 000, VAT @ 15% Dr R1 500, Cheque herewith Dr R10 050 (Total R25 000 / R25 000) (8).

## Q4 — Legal Practice Act Sections 86 & 87 Accounting Obligations (15)

> [!question] Question
> 4.1 When must an attorney render an accounting statement to a client, may a client demand an interim statement, and what must it show? (5)
> 4.2 Outline the statutory accounting obligations under sections 86 and 87 of the Legal Practice Act 28 of 2014. (10)

> [!success]- Answer
> - **4.1**: At the conclusion of the matter (1), or earlier on demand by the client (1); it must reflect all monies received, all disbursements made, and all fees plus VAT charged (3).
> - **4.2**:
>   - **s86(2) LPA**: Open and keep a separate Trust Banking Account and record all trust receipts, disbursements, third-party payments, and fee transfers in a Trust Cash Book, Trust Journal, and Trust Ledger alongside a separate Business Banking Account (Cash Book, Journal, Ledger).
>   - **s87 LPA**: Accounting records must show particulars of (a) money received and paid on the firm's own account, (b) money received and paid on account of any person, (c) money invested under s86(3) or s86(4), and (d) any interest on such investments — covering trust monies, s86 investments, deceased/insolvent estates administered, and firm business affairs (10).
