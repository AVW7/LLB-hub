## Q1 — First and Final Liquidation and Distribution Account: Estate Late Sam Dube (60)

> [!question] Question
> Sam Dube (ID 520915 5638 08 3) died on 30 June 2018, married according to customary law out of community of property without the accrual system to Sarah Dube (sole heir under his valid will). Master's reference: 1369/2018.
> Assets include: Farm NAMBO 755, Brits (360 ha, T1306/1987) valued at R2 100 000; farm implements & livestock valued at R900 000; household furniture valued at R135 000; 100% shares in VIC (Pty) Ltd sold for R1 100 000 (auditor valuation at death R1 000 000); Absa Savings account R175 000 (plus R6 250 post-death interest); 2015 Toyota Corolla sold for R100 000; Sanlam Policy payable to estate R950 000; JSE quoted shares sold for R76 000. Post-death farm rental collected: R15 000. Deemed property policies: Old Mutual policy payable to Jane Dube (R500 000, marriage settlement) and Liberty Life policy (R1 100 000, beneficiary paid premiums + 6% interest of R100 000).
> Draft the complete First and Final Liquidation and Distribution Account, including the Recapitulation Statement, Distribution Account, Income & Expenditure Account, Fiduciary Assets Account, Estate Duty Addendum, and Executor's Certificate.

> [!success]- Answer
> - **Heading**: First and Final L&D Account in the estate of the late Sam Dube (ID 520915 5638 08 3), died 30 June 2018, married according to customary law out of community of property without accrual; Master's Ref 1369/2018 (4).
> - **Liquidation Account — Assets**:
>   1. Farm NAMBO 755 (immovable, awarded to Sarah Dube): **R2 100 000.00** (2)
>   2. Farm implements & livestock (movable, awarded to Sarah Dube): **R900 000.00** (1)
>   3. Household furniture & effects (movable, awarded to Sarah Dube): **R135 000.00** (2)
>   4. 100% shares in VIC (Pty) Ltd (sold): **R1 100 000.00** (1)
>   5. Absa Savings Account: **R175 000.00** (1)
>   6. 2015 Toyota Corolla (sold): **R100 000.00** (1)
>   7. Sanlam Policy No. 175345X9 (payable to estate): **R950 000.00** (1)
>   8. JSE quoted shares (sold): **R76 000.00** (1)
>   - **Total Assets**: **R5 536 000.00**
> - **Liquidation Account — Liabilities**:
>   - s29 & s35 adverts (R350.00 + R350.00), Master's fees (max **R7 000.00**), Executor's fee 3.5% on R5 536 000 (**R193 760.00**) + 15% VAT (**R29 064.00**), bank charges to date (R6 000) + provision (R4 500), postages & petties (R260), broker's fees (R7 600), valuation fees (R6 500), conveyancer's & bond cancellation costs (R20 000), funeral expenses (R27 616), Sanlam policy loan (R25 000), Land Bank bond (R208 000) = **Total Liabilities R536 000.00**.
>   - **Balance for Distribution**: **R5 000 000.00** (awarded in full to Sarah Dube: Immovable R2 100 000 + Movable R1 035 000 + Cash surplus R1 865 000).
> - **Recapitulation Statement**: Cash & assets reduced to cash (**R2 401 000.00**) less liabilities (**R536 000.00**) = **Cash Surplus R1 865 000.00**.
> - **Income & Expenditure Account**: Post-death Absa interest (R6 250) + farm rental (R15 000) = **R21 250.00**; less Executor's fee @ 6% (**R1 275.00**) + 15% VAT (**R191.25**) = **R19 783.75** balance awarded to Sarah Dube.
> - **Estate Duty Addendum**:
>   - Assets per L&D (R5 536 000) less Sanlam policy (R950 000) less private share valuation difference (R100 000) less 30% farming allowance on R2.1m (**R630 000**) = **R3 856 000.00** s3(2) property.
>   - Add s3(3) Deemed Property: Sanlam (R950 000) + Old Mutual (R500 000) + Liberty Life (R1 100 000 less R100 000 premiums & 6% compound interest = R1 000 000) = **R2 450 000.00**.
>   - **Gross Value**: **R6 306 000.00**.
>   - Less s4 Deductions: Liabilities & admin costs (**R536 000**) + s4(q) inheritance to Sarah Dube (**R5 000 000**) + s4(q)/marriage-settlement Old Mutual policy to Jane Dube (**R500 000**) = **R6 036 000.00**.
>   - **Net Estate**: **R270 000.00** less s4A primary rebate (**R3 500 000.00**) = **R0.00 Dutiable Amount** (Estate Duty = Nil).

## Q2 — Drafting a Joint Will with Massing, Amanuensis Signature & s2(1)(a)(v) Certificate (26)

> [!question] Question
> Cedric Jones and Mabel Jones (born Nel), married in community of property in Midrand, instruct you to draft their Joint Will:
> - On the death of the first-dying, their joint estate is massed and bequeathed to their daughter Julia Bam (born Jones), subject to a lifelong usufruct in favour of the survivor (with substitution to Julia's children *per stirpes*).
> - Survivor appointed executor with power of assumption (failing whom, Julia Bam), exempt from security.
> - Inheritance excluded from community of property and accrual.
> - Cedric cannot sign due to a hand injury and directs Paul Kumar to sign on his behalf in the presence of Commissioner of Oaths Joyce Pillay. Draft the complete Joint Will, attestation clause, and s2(1)(a)(v) certificate.

> [!success]- Answer
> - **Heading & Revocation**: Joint Will of Cedric Jones and Mabel Jones (born Nel), married in community of property; revoke all prior wills/codicils (2).
> - **Executor Clause**: Appoint survivor with power of assumption, failing whom daughter Julia Bam (born Jones); exempt from furnishing security to the Master (3).
> - **Massing & Usufruct Clause (s37)**: Direct that on the death of the first-dying, the joint community estate shall be massed and consolidated into a single estate, bequeathed to daughter Julia Bam subject to a lifelong usufruct in favour of the survivor; failing Julia, to her children *per stirpes* (5).
> - **Exclusion Clause**: Exclude both community of property and the accrual system (Matrimonial Property Act 88 of 1984) from any benefit (1).
> - **Page 1 & End Signatures**: Signed on every page by the Testatrix, by **Paul Kumar on behalf of and by the direction of the Testator**, and by the **Commissioner of Oaths (J Pillay)**, plus two witnesses on the last page all present at the same time (9).
> - **Section 2(1)(a)(v) Certificate**:
>   > I, Joyce Pillay of 150 Church Street, Midrand, in my capacity as Commissioner of Oaths, certify that I have satisfied myself as to the identity of the testator Cedric Jones, and that the accompanying joint Will is the Will of the testator. (Signed J Pillay, Commissioner of Oaths, date & place) (6).

## Q3 — Intestate Succession: Surviving Parent and Predeceased Parent's Descendants (8)

> [!question] Question
> Vukela dies intestate, unmarried and without descendants, leaving a net estate of R3 500 000. He is survived by his biological father David, his stepmother Louisa, his foster sister Agnes, his girlfriend Phindi, and his predeceased mother's descendants from a prior marriage: half-brother Ronald, and nephews Peter and Alex (sons of predeceased half-brother Joseph). Calculate how Vukela's estate devolves.

> [!success]- Answer
> - **David (surviving father)**: Inherits **½ (R1 750 000.00)** in terms of **s1(1)(d)(ii)** of the Intestate Succession Act 81 of 1987 (1).
> - **Louisa (stepmother)**: Inherits **nothing** — not a biological or adoptive parent of Vukela (1).
> - **Agnes (foster sister)**: Inherits **nothing** — not a blood relative or legally adopted sibling (1).
> - **Phindi (girlfriend)**: Per the 2019 memo, inherits **nothing** as not a surviving spouse (1). *(Statutory note: see `[[Intestate Succession and Representation]]` regarding the 2021 Constitutional Court decision in Bwanya v Master of the High Court for permanent life partners with reciprocal support duties.)*
> - **Predeceased mother's descendants (s1(1)(d)(ii))**: Share the other **½ (R1 750 000.00)** *per stirpes* (1):
>   - **Ronald (half-brother)**: **R875 000.00** (½ of R1 750 000) (1).
>   - **Peter (nephew, representing Joseph)**: **R437 500.00** (1).
>   - **Alex (nephew, representing Joseph)**: **R437 500.00** (1).

Related: [[Intestate Succession and Representation]]

## Q4 — Estate Duty: Section 4A Portable Spousal Rebate Rollover (6)

> [!question] Question
> Sam's predeceased first wife Betty died leaving her entire estate to Sam, so no estate duty was paid in Betty's estate. Assume Sam now dies leaving a net dutiable estate of R19 000 000. Calculate the estate duty payable in Sam's estate.

> [!success]- Answer
> - Net dutiable estate: **R19 000 000.00** (1)
> - Less: Primary Rebate (s4A(1)): **R3 500 000.00** (1)
> - Less: Rollover of unused Primary Rebate from Betty's estate (s4A(2)): **R3 500 000.00** (2)
> - **Dutiable Amount**: **R12 000 000.00** (1)
> - **Estate Duty @ 20%** on R12 000 000.00: **R2 400 000.00** (1)
