## Q1 — First and Final L&D Account in a Joint Estate (In Community of Property): Late Deon Nel (60)

> [!question] Question
> Deon Nel (ID 560213 5162 08 4) died intestate on 13 March 2016, married in community of property to Bion Nel (born Kent, ID 590207 5003 08 9), and survived by Bion and two children: Cassie Nel (minor) and Carla Nel (major). Master's Ref: 1234/2016.
> Assets total R8 500 000 (Farm Vula R4 000 000; Erf 570 Ballito R850 000; Tractors R800 000; Cattle realised R1 500 000; Ruger Firearm R24 000; Agriblock (Pty) Ltd shares realised R24 000 [auditor value R16 000]; Sasol shares realised R45 000; Furniture R241 000; Absa account R166 000; Old Mutual policy payable to estate R600 000; Momentum policy R250 000). Deemed property also includes a Sanlam policy of R1 000 000 payable directly to a third party. Liabilities total R2 410 600 (including Angel Funerals R36 000).
> Draft the complete First and Final Liquidation and Distribution Account, Distribution Account, and Estate Duty Addendum.

> [!success]- Answer
> - **Liquidation Account**: Total Assets **R8 500 000.00**; Total Liabilities **R2 410 600.00** (including Master's fee R600, Executor's fee 3.5% on R8.5m = R297 500, funeral expenses R36 000, Absa bond R1 800 000, SARS R176 000, transfer/bond cancellation R76 000, etc.); **Balance Available for Distribution = R6 089 400.00**.
> - **Recapitulation Statement**: Cash & assets reduced to cash (Items 4, 6, 7, 9, 10, 11 = **R2 585 000.00**) less liabilities (**R2 410 600.00**) = **Cash Surplus R174 400.00**.
> - **Distribution Account (Joint Estate Funeral Expense Rule)**:
>   - Balance available for distribution: **R6 089 400.00**
>   - **Add back Funeral Expenses**: **+R36 000.00** = **R6 125 400.00** (net joint estate before funeral costs) (1).
>   - Less **½ share awarded to surviving spouse Bion Nel** by virtue of marriage in community of property: **R3 062 700.00** (2).
>   - Deceased's half-share (R3 062 700.00) **less Funeral Expenses (R36 000.00)** = **R3 026 700.00** balance for distribution to intestate heirs (1).
>   - Divided equally (⅓ each, since child's share of R1 008 900 > R250 000) between:
>     - **Bion Nel** (surviving spouse, s1(1)(c)(i)): **R1 008 900.00** (1)
>     - **Cassie Nel** (minor child, s1(1)(c)(ii)): **R1 008 900.00** (1)
>     - **Carla Nel** (major child, s1(1)(c)(ii)): **R1 008 900.00** (1)
> - **Estate Duty Addendum**:
>   - Assets per L&D (R8 500 000) less 30% farm allowance on R4m (R1 200 000) less Agriblock share adjustment (R8 000) less Old Mutual policy (R600 000) = R6 692 000; less surviving spouse's ½ share (R3 346 000) = **R3 346 000.00** s3(2) property.
>   - Add s3(3) Deemed Property: Sanlam policy (R1 000 000) + Old Mutual policy (R600 000) = **R1 600 000.00** → **Gross Value R4 946 000.00**.
>   - Less s4 Deductions: 100% of funeral expenses (**R36 000**) + ½ of remaining joint liabilities/admin costs (`½ × R2 374 600 = R1 187 300`) + s4(q) surviving spouse inheritance (**R1 008 900**) = **R2 232 200.00**.
>   - **Net Value**: **R2 713 800.00** less s4A primary rebate (**R3 500 000.00**) = **NIL Dutiable Amount**.

## Q2 — Insolvency Forfeiture Clause & Section 2(1)(a)(v) Requirements (7)

> [!question] Question
> 2.1 Draft a clause in a will providing that any beneficiary who is insolvent at the testator's death or before vesting forfeits their inheritance in favour of their intestate heirs. (4)
> 2.2 State the requirements for a Commissioner of Oaths certificate under s2(1)(a)(v) of the Wills Act 7 of 1953. (3)

> [!success]- Answer
> - **2.1**: "Any beneficiary who is insolvent at the time of my death (1) or becomes insolvent by the time he/she is entitled to receive his/her inheritance (1) shall forfeit all rights of inheritance in terms of my Will (1), and such beneficiary's inheritance shall devolve upon such beneficiary's intestate heirs (1)."
> - **2.2**: Under s2(1)(a)(v) of the Wills Act 7 of 1953 (1), the Commissioner of Oaths must certify that he/she is satisfied as to the identity of the testator (1) and that the Will so signed is the Will of the testator (1), and must sign each page of the Will (excluding the page on which the certificate appears) anywhere on the page.

## Q3 — Intestate Succession in a Polygynous Customary Marriage (10)

> [!question] Question
> Lawrence dies intestate leaving a net estate of R4 500 000. He is survived by two customary wives, Grace and Precious, two children David and Linda, and a grandson Simon (son of predeceased son Peter). Unrelated/collateral relatives Max, Ernest, Lennox, and Pilani also claim. Calculate how the estate devolves.

> [!success]- Answer
> - Under s1(1)(c)(i) read with *Bhe* and the Reform of Customary Law of Succession Act 11 of 2009, each surviving spouse is entitled to a child's share or R250 000, whichever is greater (2).
> - **Divisor**: 3 child lines (David, Linda, predeceased Peter) + 2 surviving spouses (Grace, Precious) = **5** (2).
> - **Child's share**: `R4 500 000 ÷ 5 = R900 000.00` (which exceeds R250 000).
> - **Grace** and **Precious** each inherit **R900 000.00** (2).
> - **David**, **Linda**, and **Simon** (representing his predeceased father Peter *per stirpes*) each inherit **R900 000.00** under s1(1)(c)(ii) (3).
> - Max, Ernest, Lennox, and Pilani inherit **nothing** (2).

## Q4 — Drafting a Redistribution Agreement (12)

> [!question] Question
> Dunstan Cele died intestate on 13 October 2016 leaving his surviving spouse Xolo Cele and two major children Bongani Cele and Nombile Cele as equal intestate heirs to his estate (immovable property R3 000 000, movables R1 500 000, cash R1 000 000, liabilities R1 000 000). They agree that Xolo will take the movables (R1 500 000), Bongani and Nombile will take the immovable property in equal shares (R1 500 000 each), and the R1 000 000 cash will settle the liabilities. Draft the Redistribution Agreement.

> [!success]- Answer
> - **Heading & Parties**: Redistribution Agreement entered into between Xolo Cele (surviving spouse), Bongani Cele (major heir), and Nombile Cele (major heir) ("the parties") (1½).
> - **Preamble (`WHEREAS`)**: Recite that Dunstan Cele died intestate on 13 October 2016 and that the parties are equal intestate heirs to the whole of the deceased's assets (2).
> - **Operative Clauses**:
>   1. Xolo Cele is awarded the movable assets valued at R1 500 000.00 (1).
>   2. Bongani Cele and Nombile Cele inherit the immovable property valued at R3 000 000.00 in equal undivided shares (2).
>   3. The cash of R1 000 000.00 shall be used to pay the estate liabilities (2).
>   4. The parties acknowledge that this agreement is **subject to confirmation by the Master of the High Court** (2).
> - **Execution**: Dated and signed by all three parties in the presence of two witnesses (1½).

## Q5 — True/False: Wills Act & Estates Practice (11)

> [!question] Question
> State True or False and motivate:
> 5.1 A competent witness to a will must be 16 years or older. (2)
> 5.2 The notice period to creditors under s29 is not less than 30 days and not more than 3 months. (2)
> 5.3 An executor must open an estate bank account as soon as estate cash exceeds R1 000. (2)
> 5.4 An illegitimate child cannot inherit intestate from its biological father. (2)
> 5.5 For massing under s37 to bind the surviving spouse, the surviving spouse must repudiate the joint will. (3)

> [!success]- Answer
> - **5.1 False**: Under s1 of the Wills Act, a competent witness is a person aged **14 years or over** (2).
> - **5.2 True**: Under s29 of the Administration of Estates Act, the period is not less than 30 days and not more than 3 months (2).
> - **5.3 True**: Section 28 requires an estate bank account once funds exceed R1 000 (2).
> - **5.4 False**: Under s1(2) of the Intestate Succession Act, extra-marital birth does not affect the capacity of a blood relation to inherit from their biological father or his blood relations (2).
> - **5.5 False**: The surviving testator must **adiate** (accept the benefit under the joint will) for massing to take effect under s37 (3).
