# Company, Close Corporation and Partnership Law

A single, compact question in this sample, but a distinct examinable area — knowing entity types cold is a cheap five marks.

## Types of companies under the Companies Act 71 of 2008

- **Profit companies**, which may be:
  - a state-owned company;
  - a private company;
  - a personal liability company; or
  - a public company.
- **Non-profit companies** — incorporated for a public benefit or similar object (broadly equivalent to the old s21 company under the 1973 Act).

(See s8 and the definition of "non-profit company" in the Companies Act.)

## Essentialia of a valid partnership

For a valid partnership, the parties must agree that:

1. each partner will **contribute** (money, labour, or skill);
2. all partners will **share in profits and losses**;
3. the purpose is to **conduct a business**; and
4. the purpose is to **generate profit**.

## Practical relevance in this module
