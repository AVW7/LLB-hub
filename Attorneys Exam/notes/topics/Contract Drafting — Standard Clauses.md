# Contract Drafting — Standard Clauses

The signature skill of every Part 3 (Attorney's Practice) paper in this sample — both papers open with a substantial drafting question built entirely from boilerplate/standard clauses. Learn the clause skeletons, not just the legal principle behind each.

## Acknowledgement of debt

Debtor's admission of liability for the principal debt; interest rate and capitalisation terms; instalment schedule; **acceleration clause** on default; renunciation of the legal exceptions (revision of accounts, errore calculi, no value received); consent to Magistrate's Court jurisdiction (s45, Magistrates' Courts Act); acceleration on insolvency; costs on the attorney-and-client scale plus a collection commission; creditor's right to cede/pledge and to allocate payments; balance due on the debtor's death.

## Lease clauses

- **Option to purchase**: price, payment mechanism (guarantee within X days of exercise), and how the option is exercised (written notice to a chosen domicilium).
- **Prohibition on sub-letting**: no sub-letting, ceding, assigning, allowing occupation by others, or alienating shares/interest in the lessee, without the lessor's prior written consent (not unreasonably withheld).
- **Option to renew**: conditional on full compliance with lease terms, exercised by written notice a set period before expiry, for a stated further term on the same conditions (save the renewal right itself).

## General-purpose agreement clauses

| Clause | Core content | Statutory qualification to mention |
|---|---|---|
| **Whole agreement** | Document is the entire agreement; no rights from anything outside it | Sometimes unlawful/prohibited under consumer legislation — NCA s90, CPA s50(1)(g) |
| **Variation** | No variation/addition/cancellation except by further written agreement | NCA ss116–120, CPA s46 deal with variation of consumer agreements separately |
| **Jurisdiction of magistrates' courts** | Consent under s45, Magistrates' Courts Act 1944, to the **district** court's jurisdiction | Regional Courts have civil jurisdiction to R300 000 in some instances since 15 Oct 2010 — specify "district" for certainty; unnecessary for credit agreements (unlimited MC jurisdiction) |
| **Severability** | Each clause severable from the rest, unless severance is unlawful/unreasonable given the price/clause/agreement | — |
