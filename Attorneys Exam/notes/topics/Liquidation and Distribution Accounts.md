# Liquidation and Distribution Accounts

Tested as the anchor 55–68 mark drafting question in **every Part 2 (Estates)** sitting. You are required to draft a complete First and Final Liquidation and Distribution Account in accordance with s35 of the Administration of Estates Act 66 of 1965 and the regulations thereto.

> [!important] Statutory Update (Current Law vs Historical Memos)
> - **VAT on Executor's Remuneration**: Increased from **14% to 15%** effective 1 April 2018. In sittings before 2018 (e.g., 2015–2017 memos), VAT on executor's fees is calculated at 14%; for current exams, always use **15%** (if the executor is a registered VAT vendor).
> - **Master's Fees Tariff**: Effective 1 January 2018 (GN R1274 of 2017), the maximum Master's fee increased from **R600.00** (pre-2018 estates) to **R7 000.00** (for estates with gross asset value of R400 000 or more; R0 under R250 000; R600 between R250 000 and R400 000).
> - **Section 18(3) Small Estates Threshold**: Estates with gross value not exceeding **R250 000** qualify for simplified s18(3) Letters of Authority rather than full Letters of Executorship.

## 1. Account Heading (4–5 marks)

Include all statutory identifiers:
- Whether it is the **First and Final** Liquidation and Distribution Account.
- Whether it is a single estate, **joint estate** (married in community of property), or **massed estate** (s37 Wills Act).
- Full names and 13-digit Identity Number of the deceased (and of the surviving spouse if married in community of property or massed).
- Marital status at date of death (e.g., unmarried; married in community of property; married out of community of property with/without the accrual system; married according to customary law).
- Exact **date of death** and **Master's Reference Number**.

## 2. Liquidation Account — Assets

Group assets in three structured blocks, numbering items sequentially (`1, 2, 3...`) in the voucher column:
1. **Immovable Property**: Full deeds-office description (Erf/Farm number, Township/Registration Division, Province, extent in m² or hectares, Title Deed number `T.../...`), valuation or sale price, and a **divestment note** stating to whom the property is to be transferred (or whether sold).
2. **Movable Property**: Specific movables awarded in specie (vehicles with registration numbers, firearms with serial numbers, furniture, livestock/implements, listed shares not sold) at valuation, with a divestment note.
3. **Cash and Assets Reduced to Cash**: Proceeds of private/listed shares sold, bank accounts (capital plus interest accrued up to date of death), proceeds of life policies payable to the estate (or surrender value of policies owned by the deceased on another's life), and accrual claims in favour of the estate.

## 3. Liquidation Account — Liabilities

1. **Administration Costs**:
   - Section 29 advertisement (notice to debtors & creditors): Government Gazette + local newspaper.
   - Section 35 advertisement (account lying for inspection): Government Gazette + local newspaper.
   - **Master's fees** (tariff maximum R7 000.00 post-2018; R600.00 in pre-2018 memos).
   - **Executor's remuneration**: Tariff rate is **3.5%** on gross capital assets (unless varied by will), plus **15% VAT** if the executor is VAT-registered.
   - Valuation fees, conveyancing/transfer costs, bond cancellation costs, broker's fees, bank charges (to date + provision), and postages & petties.
2. **Claims Against the Estate**:
   - Funeral expenses (note: in a joint estate married in community of property, funeral expenses are deducted **only from the deceased's half-share** in the Distribution Account, or added back before splitting the joint estate in half!).
   - Mortgage bonds, vehicle finance, credit cards, policy loans, SARS final income tax assessment, and any accrual claim against the estate.
3. **Balance for Distribution**: `Total Assets - Total Liabilities - Estate Duty payable by estate`.

## 4. Recapitulation Statement

Tests liquidity (cash sufficiency):
- **Total Cash and Assets Reduced to Cash** (sum of all cash items in the Liquidation Account).
- **Less**: Total Liabilities, Cash Legacies, and Estate Duty payable by the estate.
- **Result**: Either a **Cash Surplus** (available for distribution to residuary heirs) or a **Cash Deficit/Shortfall** (indicating how the shortfall will be paid in by the heirs or met by realising assets).

## 5. Distribution Account

Shows who receives the `Balance for Distribution`:
- For a **joint estate (in community of property)**: Add back funeral expenses to the balance for distribution, award **½ share** to the surviving spouse by virtue of the marriage in community of property, deduct the funeral expenses from the deceased's ½ share, and distribute the remaining deceased's share to the testate or intestate heirs.
- For each beneficiary: State full name, capacity (major/minor with ID number and date of birth), legal basis (clause of Will, Intestate Succession Act section, or Redistribution Agreement), and break down the award into **Immovable Property + Movable Property + Cash** (less any cash shortfall paid in). Minor cash inheritances go to the **Guardian's Fund** unless the will directs otherwise.

## 6. Income & Expenditure Account, Fiduciary Assets Account & Certificate

- **Income & Expenditure Account**: Records post-death income (interest accrued *after* date of death, post-death rentals, dividends declared after death) less post-death expenditure and **Executor's remuneration at 6%** on post-death income (plus 15% VAT if applicable).
- **Fiduciary Assets Account**: Used when the deceased held property as a fiduciary under a prior will (e.g., `AttExam 2017-08-22 P2`, `AttExam 2020-02-19 P2`). Lists the fiduciary asset, fiduciary administration/transfer costs (including 3.5% executor's fee), and distributes the asset to the fideicommissary heir.
- **Executor's Certificate (s35(1B))**: Mandatory declaration signed by the executor certifying that the account is a true and proper account of the liquidation and distribution of the estate and that all assets and income collected subsequent to the death of the deceased to the date of the account have been disclosed.
