# Statements and Pleas in Criminal Trials

Every Part 1 paper in this module includes at least one drafting question requiring a s112, s115, or s220 statement — usually built on the same fact pattern as the bail question. Also tested in the CCP module (s113/s115 correction of plea) — this module's notes are self-contained.

## The three statement types

| Statement | When used | Content |
|---|---|---|
| **s112(2)** | Guilty plea | In writing; admits all elements of the offence; must satisfy the court the accused is guilty as charged |
| **s115** | Not guilty plea | Sets out the basis of the defence; the accused makes only the admissions they choose to make |
| **s220** | Either plea | Formal admissions of specific facts, freely and voluntarily made, relieving the State of proving those facts only — not a plea itself |

## Drafting checklist

- **s112 plea**: heading (court, case number, parties), accused's personal capacity, confirmation of understanding the charge, the material admissions establishing every element (act, causation, unlawfulness, fault/intention or negligence), and the formal plea of guilty.
- **s115 statement**: personal capacity, the factual narrative supporting the defence (e.g. private defence — disparity in size/strength, genuine belief in danger, proportionality of the response), an express denial of unlawfulness, and the plea of not guilty. Headings are sometimes required, sometimes explicitly excluded by the question — read the instruction carefully.
- **s220 statement**: identify the accused, confirm the admissions are made freely and voluntarily, list only the specific facts admitted (careful to **exclude** any fact the client disputes — e.g. ownership of money found on him), and consent that these be recorded as formal admissions.

## Recurring fact patterns

- **Private defence / defence of another** — shooting an aggressor to protect a friend under attack (see [[AttExam 2016-02-09]] Q7).
- **Cheque fraud with a denied intention to defraud** — insufficient funds at the time, but a genuine expectation of funds arriving before presentation (see [[AttExam 2016-08-16]] Q7).
- **Disputed ownership of recovered cash/property** — admits the conduct but disputes only one narrow fact (see [[AttExam 2017-02-14]] Q7).
