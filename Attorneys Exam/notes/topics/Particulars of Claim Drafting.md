# Particulars of Claim Drafting

The other recurring drafting skill in every Part 1 paper — always a full particulars-of-claim or a "spot the missing averment" question, usually a breach-of-contract, sale-of-goods, or delictual claim.

## Standard skeleton

1. **Parties** — full description (major/minor, occupation, residence/registered address; for a company/CC, the registering statute and registered office).
2. **Jurisdiction** — the cause of action arising, or the defendant carrying on business/residing, within the court's area — plead it expressly, don't assume it.
3. **The agreement or delict** — date, place, parties, and the material terms (price, payment schedule, place of payment) or the negligent conduct relied on.
4. **Breach/damage** — what went wrong (abandonment, non-payment, negligent driving) and how damages are quantified.
5. **Demand** — where required, plead that demand was made and refused/ignored, with the letter of demand annexed.
6. **Prayer** — the specific relief, interest (rate and the date it starts running), and costs.

## Common missing-averments traps

- **Consent to Regional Court jurisdiction** must be pleaded expressly where relied on — it is not automatic.
- **Delivery** of goods must be alleged in a sale-of-goods claim, not just the agreed price.
- **Ownership** of a damaged item, and its **pre-accident value exceeding the claim**, must be pleaded in a vehicle-damage claim.
- Where a party sues as **curator ad litem**, plead the court order appointing them (annexed) and, against an organ of state, compliance with s3 of the Institution of Legal Proceedings against certain Organs of State Act 40 of 2002.
- **Suretyship** — plead the surety's undertaking as co-principal debtor as a separate defendant citation, not folded into the main defendant's paragraph.
- **Cession** — plead the date of the written cession and the cedent's name, address and description as at the time of cession.
- A **third-party notice** must be served (via the Sheriff) before close of pleadings where the defendant blames someone else for the same damage.

## Correcting errors after issue

A wrong figure in the claim (e.g. two months' rent miscalculated) is corrected by a **notice to amend**; the other side may deliver a **notice to object** setting out grounds; if the objection fails, the amending party **serves and files the corrected pages**.
