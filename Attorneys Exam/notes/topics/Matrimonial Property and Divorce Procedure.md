# Matrimonial Property and Divorce Procedure

Two separate sittings each test one slice of matrimonial law — divorce jurisdiction and prayer-drafting in one paper, the accrual system in the other.

## Divorce jurisdiction (Divorce Act 70 of 1979)

Two independent jurisdictional bases can each found a valid divorce action, and both may apply simultaneously:

- **s2(1)(a)** — the court of the area where the **parties are domiciled**.
- **s2(1)(b)** — the court of the area where the **plaintiff is ordinarily resident** at the date of institution, **and** has been ordinarily resident in the Republic for at least **one year** immediately before that date.

Where both bases point to different Divisions, advise the client on the **more convenient and less costly** forum — usually wherever the plaintiff is currently located.

## Drafting the prayers

Typical prayers against the defendant spouse: a decree of divorce; maintenance (a monthly amount); a share (often 50%, or "such part as the court deems fit") of the defendant's **assets** transferred to the plaintiff; a share of the defendant's **pension interest** (as at date of the court order) vesting in the plaintiff **when the pension benefit accrues**, alternatively on date of divorce; costs; further/alternative relief. Where the pension fund itself is cited as a second defendant, add a prayer that it **endorse its records** accordingly and furnish proof of the endorsement to the Registrar within a set period.

**Accrual-system marriages** require a different pension/asset-sharing prayer: that a share of the **accrual** in the defendant's estate (calculated from date of marriage to date of the order) be transferred to the plaintiff, rather than a flat 50% of assets.

## What counts toward the accrual

| Item | Included? |
|---|---|
| Large income earned by one spouse while the other manages the household | Included |
| Money earned from part-time work (e.g. dressmaking) | Included |
| An inheritance | **Excluded** |
| General damages from a personal injury (e.g. after a collision) | **Excluded** |
| RAF payment for medical expenses | Included |

The pattern: **inheritances and non-patrimonial damages (general damages) are excluded** from the accrual; ordinary income and compensatory payments for patrimonial loss (like medical expenses) are included.
