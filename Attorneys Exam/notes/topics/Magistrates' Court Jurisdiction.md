# Magistrates' Court Jurisdiction

Tested as quick-fire "does this court have jurisdiction / from which court must summons issue" questions — easy marks if the checklist is memorised, easy to lose if guessed.

## What the District Magistrate's Court CAN do

- Liquidation of a Close Corporation.
- Substitution of an executor in place of a deceased plaintiff.

## What the District Magistrate's Court CANNOT do

- Hear an appeal against a magistrate's bail refusal (that goes to the High Court — see [[Bail Applications]]).
- Grant an unopposed divorce (High Court / Divorce Court jurisdiction).
- Sequestrate an individual's estate.
- Reinstate a wrongfully dismissed employee for unfair labour practice (that's the CCMA/Labour Court's territory).

## Which Magistrate's Court to sue in

- A **company/CC's "place of business"** for jurisdiction purposes is restricted to where its **central management** is, or its **registered address** — a mere **branch office does not found jurisdiction** there, even if the contract was concluded or delivery occurred at the branch. Rely instead on s28(1)(a) (defendant carries on business in the area) pointing to the principal place of business.
- Missing an express **consent-to-jurisdiction** allegation (for Regional Court jurisdiction specifically) is a classic missing-averment trap in particulars of claim — see [[Particulars of Claim Drafting]].
