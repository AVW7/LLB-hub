QUESTION 9 [5] 

WITHDRAWALS: 

A charge may be withdrawn only before an accused pleads to a charge (1). After the withdrawal, the accused shall not be entitled to a verdict of acquittal in respect of that charge. (1) 

STOPPING PROSECUTIONS: 

It can be done any time after an accused has pleaded but before conviction (1). 

The court must then acquit the accused in respect of that charge (1). 

DIFFERENCE BETWEEN THE TWO: 

After a matter had been withdrawn, one can be prosecuted of the same charge again. That is not the position when prosecution had been stopped (1).
