QUESTION 11

[8]

The introduction of FICA impinges upon the traditional role and independence of the attorneys' profession and upon the confidentiality of all communications between attorneys and their clients. Apart from having the duty to establish and verify the identity of clients and of their authority, they are obliged to keep detailed records of clients, business relationships and of transactions for a specified period; they are also under an obligation:

1. to make such records available to the Financial Intelligence Centre on the strength of a warrant;
2. to inform the centre on request of the existence of a current or past mandate;
3. to report cash transactions above a prescribed amount to the Centre; and
4. to report to the Centre the conveyancer in terms of a transaction with a client of cash above a prescribed amount to or through a specified account or institution.
