In B's estate the net value is R9 000 000.00. From this is deducted R7 000 000.00 in terms of section 4A.(2) of the Estate Duty Act. [2] Because no amount was deducted from the net value in the predeceased spouse's estate, the deduction of R7 000 000.00 is not reduced. [1]
