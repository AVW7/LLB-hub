## Q5 — Zero-rated transaction requirements (4)

> [!success]- Answer (no question wording available)
> The seller must be a VAT vendor; the purchaser must be a VAT vendor; the parties must agree in writing that the asset is sold as a going concern; the asset must indeed be an income-generating activity on date of transfer.

## Q6 — Definitions: mortgagor, mortgagee, section, unit, notarial bond, conduct rules, common property, simultaneous registration, consolidation, exclusive use area (10)

> [!success]- Answer (no question wording available)
> 6.1 Mortgagor — a property owner who agrees to bind their property as security for fulfilment of an obligation. 6.2 Mortgagee — a person who grants a loan or other benefit, in return for which the borrower gives property as security. 6.3 Section — that part of a building indicated on a sectional plan by a distinctive number. 6.4 Unit — the section together with the undivided share in the common property allocated to it, per the participation quota. 6.5 Notarial bond — a form of security registered over movable property. 6.6 Conduct rules — rules dealing with the conduct of occupiers of the sections, what is acceptable and unacceptable. 6.7 Common property — everything not labelled as a section on the sectional plan. 6.8 [Simultaneous transactions] — a number of transactions that must be simultaneously lodged and simultaneously registered at the Deeds Office. 6.9 Consolidation — the joining of two or more pieces of immovable property to make one larger piece. 6.10 Exclusive use area — an area forming part of the common property, reserved in terms of the law for the exclusive use of the owner of a particular section.

Related: [[Mortgage Bonds — Registration and Cancellation]], [[Sectional Title Schemes]], [[Exclusive Use Areas]]

## Q7 — Cooling-off clause requirements (7)

> [!success]- Answer (no question wording available)
> This clause aims to protect purchasers in lower income brackets against pressure-selling. The purchaser has the right to revoke/terminate the obligation within 5 days after signing an offer to purchase land or a deed of alienation. The purchaser must be a natural person. The property must have been sold for R250 000 or less. In calculating the 5 days, the first signing day is excluded, as is any Saturday, Sunday or public holiday. The purchaser's notice to the seller must be in writing, signed by the purchaser or agent. Consequence: the purchaser may cancel/terminate the contract without falling into breach.

Related: [[Cooling-Off Right for Purchasers]]
