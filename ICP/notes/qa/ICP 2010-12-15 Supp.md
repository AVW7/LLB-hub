## Q1 — Exclusive use areas (4)

> [!question] Question
> How would you reserve an Exclusive Use Area in terms of Act 95 of 1986? Can an Exclusive Use Area be mortgaged? If so how?

> [!success]- Answer

> b) Section 27A: reserved by amending the rules.

## Q2 — Life of a deed in the Deeds Office (7)

> [!question] Question
> Explain what happens to a deed once it is in the Deeds Office, from the day it leaves the conveyancer's office until he/she receives it back.

> [!success]- Answer
> Lodgement → Data-Prep → Distribution Room → Junior Examination → Senior Examination → Assistant Registrar of Deeds/Monitor → (if rejected: Delivery; if passed: Prep) → Execution → Numbering → Data-capture → Scanning/microfilming.

## Q5 — Transferring attorney's coordinating role (10)

> [!question] Question
> You obtained instructions to transfer a mortgaged property (mortgage to be cancelled). The transferee's mortgage bond instructions have gone to Pompei Attorneys; the cancellation instruction has gone to Caesar Attorneys. Explain the important role the transferring attorney plays, and the coordination involved.

> [!success]- Answer
> Make contact with clients and role players (estate agent, transferor). Contact the bond cancellation attorney for cancellation guarantee requirements and secure same. Contact the bond attorney with the guarantee requirements and obtain the guarantee. Give bond cancellation requirements to the bond cancellation attorney. Obtain signatures; apply for clearance figures; apply for transfer duty/exemption receipts; comply with FICA; draft the deed of transfer. Arrange lodgement once all attorneys are ready. Arrange registration once on prep. Pay the bond cancellation fee. Finalise and pay clients. (Plus any other relevant step necessary to complete the mandate.)

Related: [[Transfer of Land — Role Players and Process]], [[Mortgage Bonds — Registration and Cancellation]]

## Q7 — FICA responsibilities of accountable institutions (5)

> [!question] Question
> A law firm (attorney) is an "accountable institution" per Schedule 1 of FICA (Act 38 of 2001). What are the main responsibilities of accountable institutions?

> [!success]- Answer
> Establish and verify the identity of clients; keep records of clients and transactions; report suspicious and unusual transactions; report certain cash and electronic transfers; train staff and formulate/implement internal rules.

Related: [[FICA Compliance in Conveyancing]]

## Q8 — Rejection note (5)

> [!question] Question
> Will you expect a rejection note in your deed of transfer from this extract: "...the appearer declared that his principal truly and legally sold on 27 November 2008 ...and did cede and transfer to and on behalf of Zelpy Trust IT 786/09"? Why?

> [!success]- Answer
> Yes — the trust was only established after the date of the transaction (sale of the property), so it did not exist and could not be a party at the time of sale.
