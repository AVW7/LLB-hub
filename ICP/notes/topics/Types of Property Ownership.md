# Types of Property Ownership

Recurring "name/differentiate the types" question — usually a short list-and-explain or true/false.

## The main types

| Type | Nature |
|---|---|
| **Freehold** | Full ownership — owner is free to deal with the property as he pleases; owns both the land and the structure(s) on it. |
| **Leasehold** | Qualified ownership — occupier "owns" the land/buildings for a limited period only; on expiry the property reverts to the original owner (usually the authorities). A lease is a rental agreement between the land owner and a tenant. |
| **Sectional title** | Similar to freehold, but the owner owns a building or part of a building separate from the land, usually alongside other owners — see [[Sectional Title Schemes]]. |
| **Time-share** | A number of people acquire a right to occupy a particular property for a specified period of time each. |
| **Farm** | A property description type — rural/agricultural land. |
| **Agricultural holding** | A distinct property description type (listed alongside farm, sectional title, full title in some papers). |

## Freehold vs leasehold — the core distinction

Freehold property is where the land and buildings on it are privately owned by the registered owner; leasehold property is where the occupier "owns" the land and buildings for a limited duration only, and after expiry of that period the land reverts to the owner (usually the authorities).

Leasehold land in previously unsurveyed areas can take time to convert/register — those areas must first be surveyed and general plans drawn up and approved, which takes time and money.

## Unimproved vs improved land

- **Unimproved land** — vacant land, with no buildings on it.
- **Improved land** — land with buildings on it.

## Sectional title vs freehold ownership

Sectional title ownership involves ownership of a building or part of a building, separate from the land on which it is situated (e.g. a flat/section). Freehold property involves ownership of the whole building(s) **and** the land on which it is situated.

## "Types of property description" question pattern

A recurring list-3 question asks for types of **property description** (not full clauses) — answers seen: farm, sectional title, full title, agricultural holding.
