# FICA Compliance in Conveyancing

A law firm (attorney's practice) is an **"accountable institution"** in terms of Schedule 1 of the Financial Intelligence Centre Act 38 of 2001 (FICA).

## Main responsibilities of accountable institutions

- Establish and verify the identity of their clients.
- Keep record of clients and transactions.
- Report suspicious and unusual transactions.
- Report certain cash and electronic transfers.
- Formulate and implement internal rules.
- Train staff on FICA and monitor compliance.

## True/false traps

- Training staff and monitoring compliance **is** one of the duties of accountable institutions — True.
- A SARS document older than 4 months **is not** acceptable for FICA (proof of address/identity) purposes — the document must be recent.
