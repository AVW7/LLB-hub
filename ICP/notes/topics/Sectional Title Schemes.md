# Sectional Title Schemes

Core vocabulary and structural concepts of the Sectional Titles Act 95 of 1986 — tested in nearly every sitting, usually as definitions or true/false.

## Core vocabulary

- **Section** — that part of a building indicated on a sectional plan by way of a distinctive number.
- **Unit** — the section together with the **undivided share in the common property** allocated to it, in terms of the **participation quota**.
- **Common property** — everything in the scheme that has **not** been labelled as a section on the sectional plan.
- **Body corporate** — comprises all owners of units in the scheme; responsible for managing the scheme (see [[Body Corporate — Management and Conduct Rules]]).

## Share-block schemes vs sectional title

A **share-block scheme** is a scheme in terms of which ownership of a **share in a company** grants the shareholder a right to the **use** of property owned by that company — the shareholder does **not** own the immovable property itself (contrast with sectional title, where the unit owner has real ownership of the section).

## Consolidation and subdivision

- **Consolidation** — the joining of two or more pieces of immovable property to form one larger piece. Sectional title units **not** sharing a common boundary **can** still be consolidated (true/false trap).
- **Subdivision** — splitting one property into separate portions (the opposite of consolidation — a common true/false trap is describing subdivision as "combining" properties, which is wrong).

## Sectional title on farm property

Sectional title schemes **can** be opened and registered on farm property — not restricted to urban erven.

## Real right of extension

A developer may reserve a real right to extend the scheme when opening the sectional title register. If not disclosed to a purchaser, see [[Exclusive Use Areas]] for the purchaser's remedies. Disclosure of any real right of extension is one of the certifications required in the s15B(3) conveyancer's certificate — see [[Body Corporate — Management and Conduct Rules]].

## Clearance certificate

A **clearance certificate for the transfer of a sectional title unit must be lodged with every transfer** (true).

## Related property description note

A **sectional title** property description is one of the standard "types of property description" tested alongside farm and full title descriptions — see [[Types of Property Ownership]].
