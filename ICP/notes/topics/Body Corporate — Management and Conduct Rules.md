# Body Corporate — Management and Conduct Rules

## Management rules vs conduct rules

- **Management rules** deal with the **management of the scheme** — e.g. the appointment of trustees, holding of meetings, determination and collection of levies.
- **Conduct rules** deal with the **conduct of occupiers** of the sections — what is acceptable and unacceptable, e.g. rules on hanging laundry from windows, littering, noise.

Rules of the body corporate are divided into these two categories (true/false staple).

## Conveyancer's certificate — s15B(3) vs s10

Two different certificates are used depending on the situation, both relating to sectional title transfers:

| | s15B(3) certificate | s10 certificate/affidavit |
|---|---|---|
| Signed by | **Conveyancer** | **Developer** |
| Form | Certificate | Affidavit |
| When lodged | With **every** sectional title transfer | Only where **CRST** (Certificate of Real Sectional Title) is still held by the developer |
| Refers to | s25 real right (of extension) and levies paid | s10 — renting/letting of the unit |

### What the s15B(3) certificate certifies

1. Whether the body corporate has/has not been established.
2. That all levies have been paid, or provision made for payment, up to date of registration.
3. Whether a real right of extension exists and, if so, that it has been disclosed.
