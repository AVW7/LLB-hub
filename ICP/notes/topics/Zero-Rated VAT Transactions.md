# Zero-Rated VAT Transactions

Short, self-contained list question — the requirements for a transaction to qualify as zero-rated (sale of a business as a going concern).

## Requirements

1. The **seller** must be a VAT vendor.
2. The **purchaser** must be a VAT vendor.
3. The parties must agree **in writing** that the asset/property is sold as a **going concern**.
4. The asset must in fact be an **income-generating** activity/business at the date of sale/transfer.
