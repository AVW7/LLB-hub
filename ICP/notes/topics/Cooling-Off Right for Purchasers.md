# Cooling-Off Right for Purchasers

A statutory clause protecting purchasers in lower income brackets against pressure-selling.

## The rule

- The purchaser has the right to **revoke/terminate** the obligation within **5 days** after signing an offer to purchase land or a deed of alienation in respect of land.
- The purchaser must be a **natural person**.
- The property must have been sold for a purchase price of **R250 000 or less**.
- In calculating the 5 days: the first day on which the offer/deed was signed is **excluded**, as is any **Saturday, Sunday or public holiday**.
- The notice by the purchaser to the seller must be **in writing**, signed by the purchaser or his/her agent.
- Consequence: the purchaser may **cancel/terminate the contract without falling into breach**.
