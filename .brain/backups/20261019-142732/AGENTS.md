## MODULE DETAILS

### CCP — Criminal Court Practice (21 files)

**Primary Legislation:** Criminal Procedure Act 51 of 1977

**Core Topics:**

| CPA Section | Topic | Frequency |
|-------------|-------|-----------|
| s60 | Bail: grounds for refusal, factors | Every paper |
| s60(4) | Risks justifying bail refusal | 6+ papers |
| s60(11B) | Mandatory disclosures at bail | 6+ papers |
| s112(2) | Plea of guilty statement | 5+ papers |
| s115 | Plea of not guilty statement | 5+ papers |
| s113 | Change/correct plea | 4+ papers |
| s174 | Discharge at close of State's case | 6+ papers |
| s309/310/311 | Appeal procedure | Multiple |

**Key Case Law:** *S v Zinn* 1969 (sentencing triad), *Shabalala v Attorney-General, Transvaal* 1995 (docket access)

**Exam Pattern:** 50 marks, practical drafting (s112/s115 statements), scenario-based questions

---

### CLP — Constitutional Practice (17 files)

**Primary Legislation:** Constitution of South Africa, 1996 + Constitutional Court Rules

**Core Topics:** Bill of Rights, locus standi (s38), direct access (Rule 18), amicus curiae (Rule 10), limitation clause (s36), confirmation of invalidity (s172(2))

**Exam Pattern:** 25 marks, rights analysis + procedural application, draft court orders

---

### HCP — High Court Practice (24 files)

**Primary Legislation:** High Court Rules (Uniform Rules of Court)

**Core Topics:** Jurisdiction (s27), service of summons, pleadings (Particulars of Claim, Plea, Replication), default judgment, applications (notice of motion), interdicts, appeals, review

**Key Concepts:**
- Jurisdiction: personae (where defendant resides), causae (where cause arose), contractus (where contract made)
- Service: personal service, substituted service, edictal citation
- Pleadings: 4 essentialia for summons (parties, cause of action, relief, jurisdiction)

**Drafting Tasks:** Summons, notice of motion, heads of argument, notice of appeal

**Exam Pattern:** 25-50 marks, civil procedure drafting, jurisdiction questions

---

### MCP — Magistrate's Court Practice (14 files)

**Primary Legislation:** Magistrates' Courts Act 32 of 1944 + Rules

**Core Topics:** Jurisdiction (monetary limits), simplified procedure, default judgment, warrants of execution, Rule 18 admissions

**Key Concepts:**
- Jurisdiction thresholds: District Court (R120,000), Regional Court (R400,000)
- Sections 28-36 of the Act (jurisdiction bases)
- Small claims procedure (alternative)

**Exam Pattern:** 25 marks, lower court civil procedure

---

### COS — Legal Costs (13 files)

**Primary Legislation:** Attorneys Act + Court tariff scales

**Key Concepts:** Allocatur, taxation, party-and-party costs, attorney-and-client costs, de bonis propriis, drawing fee (6.25%/10.6%), attendance fee (graduated), contingency fee agreements

**Calculation Skills:** VAT 14% on fees (not disbursements), allocatur completion

**Exam Pattern:** 25 marks, numerical calculations + definitions

---

### FBE — Forms of Business Enterprise (14 files)

**Entities:** Close Corporations (max 10 members, CK1, limited liability), Partnerships (3 types, joint & several liability), Trusts (inter vivos vs mortis causa, 40% tax), Sole Proprietorships, Private Companies

**Tax Rates:** CC: 28%, Company: 28%, Business Trust: 40%, Individual: 18-40%

**Exam Pattern:** 25 marks, true/false + characteristics comparisons + advice letters

---

### LDR — Labour Dispute Resolution (16 files)

**Primary Legislation:** Labour Relations Act 66 of 1995

**Core Topics:** Unfair dismissal (s188), CCMA procedures (arbitration, conciliation), procedural fairness, substantive fairness, retrenchment, constructive dismissal

**Key Concepts:**
- Pre-dismissal procedures: notice, hearing, representation
- CCMA deadlines: 30 days to refer dispute
- Arbitration awards and review

**Exam Pattern:** Scenario-based, client advice on dismissal procedure

---

### ICP — Introduction to Conveyancing Practice (13 files)

**Primary Legislation:** Deeds Registries Act 47 of 1937, Sectional Titles Act 95 of 1986

**Core Topics:** Real rights vs personal rights, mortgage bonds, transfer of land, deeds registration, body corporates (management vs conduct rules)

**Key Concepts:**
- Mortgagor vs mortgagee
- Sectional title schemes
- Cancellation of bonds
- General plan and sectional plan

**Exam Pattern:** Definitions + practical conveyancing questions

---

### PLE — Professional Legal Ethics (18 files)

**Primary Legislation:** Legal Practice Act 28 of 2014, Rules of the Legal Practice Council

**Core Topics:** Conflicts of interest, client confidentiality, mandate limits, withdrawing from matters, fees and billing ethics, disciplinary proceedings

**Key Questions:**
- Can you represent a guilty client pleading not guilty?
- Ethical limits of client mandates
- Conflict between co-accused
- Proper withdrawal from representation

**Exam Pattern:** Ethical dilemmas + rule application

---

### PCT — Professional Conduct (16 files)

**Primary Legislation:** Attorneys Act 53 of 1979 (historical), Legal Practice Act

**Core Topics:** Contingency fee agreements, client care rules, trust account requirements, professional misconduct, practitioner's duties to court and client

**Key Concepts:**
- Contingency fees: double normal fee OR 25% of recovery (whichever lesser)
- Trust accounts: separate accounting requirements
- Client communication obligations

**Exam Pattern:** Application of conduct rules to practical scenarios

---

### IPM — Introduction to Practice Management (13 files)

**Core Topics:** Practice management, marketing (5 Ps), emotional intelligence (5 components), financial management, leadership, HR in law firms

**Key Concepts:**
- Emotional intelligence: self-awareness, self-regulation, motivation, empathy, social skills
- Marketing: Product, Price, Place, Promotion, People
- Practice management systems

**Exam Pattern:** 25 marks, knowledge questions + practical application

## FILE NAMING CONVENTIONS

Pattern: `{Subject}{Date}{Type}.{ext}`

| Component | Meaning |
|-----------|---------|
| **Subject** | CCP, CLP, COS, FBE, HCP, MCP, LDR, ICP, PLE, PCT, IPM |
| **Date** | `30Jan2010`, `25Jul2015`, `2012(1)`, `Dec2010` |
| **Type** | `Q` = Question paper, `A` = Marking memo/Answer |
| **Special** | `AssQ/AssA` = Assignment, `SuppA` = Supplementary |

Examples:
- `HCP6Mar2010Q.doc` — High Court Practice, 6 March 2010, Question paper
- `PLE30May2015A.doc` — Professional Legal Ethics, 30 May 2015, Answer memo
- `ICP16May2012Q.doc` — Conveyancing Practice, 16 May 2012, Question paper

## MAINTENANCE PRACTICES

### Adding New Files

1. Add files to the appropriate module folder
2. Follow naming convention: `{Subject}{DD}{Mon}{YYYY}{Q|A}.ext`
3. Run `python3 .reindex.py` to regenerate all indexes
4. Check the folder's `README.md` for updated year coverage and gaps

### Subdirectory README.md Files

Each module folder contains a `README.md` summarizing:
- File count and module name
- Years covered with **missing years highlighted**
- Year-by-year breakdown with dates and file types
- Undated/special files (assignments, templates, slides)
- Question/Memo pair status

These files are auto-regenerated by `.reindex.py` and help identify gaps in the collection.

### Known Output Files (Auto-Skipped)

The reindexer skips these output files:
- `index.md`, `index.json`, `AGENTS.md` (root level)
- `README.md` (in each subdirectory)
