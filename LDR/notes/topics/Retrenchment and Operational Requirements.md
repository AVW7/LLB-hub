# Retrenchment and Operational Requirements

Section 189 LRA consultation process — includes the richest single question in the archive (a full 15-mark first-notice letter).

## The s189 process

1. **First notice/letter** — the employer, once contemplating dismissals for operational requirements, must give written notice inviting **consultation**. It must be addressed to every party the employer is required to consult (affected employees and/or their **registered trade union**). Critically: **no final decision has been made yet**.
2. The notice must set out (and the parties must consult on) the matters in **s189(3)**:
   - the reasons for the proposed dismissals;
   - alternatives considered and the reasons for rejecting each;
   - the number of employees likely to be affected and the job categories in which they work;
   - the proposed method for selecting employees to be dismissed;
   - the time when dismissals are likely to take effect;
   - severance pay proposed;
   - assistance the employer proposes to offer affected employees;
   - the possibility of future re-employment;
   - the number of employees employed by the employer; and
   - the number of employees the employer has dismissed for operational requirements in the preceding 12 months.
3. Consultation itself (**s189(2)**) must engage with:
   - appropriate measures to **avoid** the dismissals;
   - appropriate measures to **minimise** the number of dismissals (e.g. eliminating overtime, moratorium on new appointments, voluntary retrenchment, early retirement, short time, temporary lay-off);
   - changing the **timing** of the dismissals;
   - **mitigating the adverse effects** of dismissal (e.g. agreements on re-employment of retrenched workers);
   - the method for **selecting** employees to be dismissed;
   - **severance pay**.

## Selection criteria — s189(7)

The employer must select employees according to criteria that are:
(a) **agreed** to by the consulting parties; or
(b) if no agreement is reached, **fair and objective** criteria — e.g. skills, experience, length of service, **LIFO** (last in, first out).

## Severance pay

- Minimum: **one week's remuneration for every completed year of service** (s41, Basic Conditions of Employment Act).
- Employees who **unreasonably refuse** an offer of reasonable alternative employment **forfeit** the right to severance pay.
- Employees who accept reasonable alternative employment elsewhere are not entitled to severance pay from the original employer.

## s189A

Applies to large-scale retrenchments (facilitation by the CCMA) — several papers explicitly instruct the student to **assume s189A does not apply**, so the plain s189 process governs.
