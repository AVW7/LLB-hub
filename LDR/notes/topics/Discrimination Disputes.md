# Discrimination Disputes

Two fact patterns recur: a **medical scheme / sexual orientation** dispute (near-verbatim in two papers) and a **trade union membership** discrimination-in-hiring dispute.

## Sexual orientation — medical scheme dependants

Facts: an employee wants to register his same-sex life partner as a dependant on the employer's medical scheme; the fund's rules don't provide for it and the employer refuses to change them.

- **Forum**: refer the dispute to the **CCMA**.
- **Merits**: the request can succeed — this is **unfair discrimination on the ground of sexual orientation**, a listed ground.

## Trade union membership — discrimination in recruitment

Facts: an unsuccessful job applicant believes she was turned down because of her trade union membership/views expressed at interview. She is not yet an "employee" as defined in **s213 LRA**, but the LRA still protects her.

- **s5(2)(c)(i) LRA**: no person may prejudice a person **seeking employment** because of trade union membership.
- **Referral route (s9 LRA)**: refer the dispute to a **council** if one exists for the sector; if not, to the **CCMA** for **conciliation**.
- If the dispute remains unresolved, refer it to the **Labour Court** for **adjudication** (see Flow Diagram 1, Schedule 4 of the LRA).
