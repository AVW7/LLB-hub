# Automatically Unfair Dismissal on Pregnancy

Tested once, but a clean example of the "automatically unfair" category — no substantive or procedural fairness defence is available once the ground is established.

## The rule

A dismissal on the ground of **pregnancy** (or any reason related to pregnancy) is **automatically unfair**. It is **neither substantively nor procedurally fair** — the usual fairness enquiry doesn't rescue the employer once the ground is proved, even where the employer raises an ostensibly different reason (e.g. alleged dishonesty in not disclosing the pregnancy).

## Remedies

- **Reinstatement** is one of the remedies available to the employee.
- **Compensation** must be **just and equitable**, but for automatically unfair dismissals it may **not exceed the equivalent of 24 months' remuneration**, calculated at the employee's rate of remuneration on the date of dismissal.
  - (Contrast with the general unfair dismissal cap of 12 months' remuneration — the doubled cap signals how seriously the LRA treats automatically unfair dismissals.)
