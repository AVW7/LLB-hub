# **Eyona mfundo iphantsi ifunekayo nokuqegeshelwa umsebenzi** 25

26. (1) Umntu uyakufanelekela ukuba amkelwe aze abhaliswe njengegqwetha ukuba—
(a) uzifezile zonke izinto ezifunekayo zesidanga seLLB asifumene kuyo nayiphi in iyunivesithi ebhaliswe kwiRiphablikhi, emva kokufundela eso sidanga—
(i) izifundo ezitsala ixesha elingekho ngaphantsi kweminyaka emine; okanye 30
(ii) izifundo ezitsala ezitsala ixesha elingekho ngaphantsi kweminyaka emihlanu ukuba isidanga seLLB usifundele emva kwesidanga seBachelor esingeyiyo iLLB, ngokwemigaqo yeyunivesithi leyo ethe yavunywa naliBhunga; okanye 35
(b) ngokulawulwa sisiqendu 24(2)(b), uzifezile zonke izinto ezifuna sisidanga somthetho asifumene kwelinye ilizwe, esilinganayo nesidanga seLLB, saza savunywa yiSouth African Qualifications Authority eyasekwa yiNational Qualifications Framework Act, 2008 (Umthetho 67 ka-2008); kananjalo
(c) ukufumene konke ukuqegeshelwa umsebenzi njengegqwetha elisengumkhwetha, okufunwa nguMphathiswa, kuquka—
(i) ukusebenzela uluntu, njengoko kuxelwe kwisiqendu 29; kananjalo 40
(ii) nesifundo sokuwuphatha umsebenzi wobugqwetha esilungiselelwe amagqwetha asengabakhwetha azimisele ukuba ngamagqwetha okanye ukuba ngamagqwetha eejaji ekuthethwe ngawo kwisiqendu 34(2)(b); kwaye 45
(d) uluphumelele uvavanyo lokukwazi ukusebenza olulungiselelwe amagqwetha asengabakhwetha, uvavanyo ekunokuthi kwenziwe isigqibo ngalo kwimigaqo.
(2) Igqwetha lifanelekela ukuba libhaliswe njengegqwetha lonaniselwano ngemizi ukuba liluphumelele uvavanyo lokukwazi ukuwenza umsebenzi wamagqwetha onaniselwano ngemizi ngendlela egqitywe kwimigaqo liBhunga. 50
(3) Igqwetha lifanelekela ukuba libhaliswe njengegqwetha lamaxwebhu afungelweyo ukuba liluphumelele uvavanyo lokukwazi ukuwenza umsebenzi wamagqwetha amaxwebhu afungelweyo ngendlela egqitywe kwimigaqo liBhunga. 55
