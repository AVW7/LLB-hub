# Iimali zokuhlawulela umsebenzi wobugqwetha

35. (1) De lube uphando oluxelwe kwisiqendwana (4) lugqityiwe zaza nezindululo eziqulethwe apho zasetyenziswa nguMphathiswa ngokuthi enze imimiselo, emva kokuba ebonisene neBhunga, iimali zokuhlawulela umsebenzi owenziwe ngamagqwetha, ziinkampani, ngamaziko-ncedo lomthetho okanye yiLegal Aid South Africa ekuthethwe ngazo kwisiqendu 34, ezo mali mazibe ngokwemiyinge equlunqwe yiRules Board for Courts of Law eyasekwa sisiqendu 2 seRules Board for Courts of Law Act, 1985 (Umthetho 107 ka-1985). 35

(2) iRules Board for Courts of Law mayithi, xa isenza isigqibo ngemiyinge njengoko kuxelwe kwisiqendwana (1), icingele—

(a) ukubaluleka, ukuntsonkotha, nobuchule obufunwa nguloo msebenzi;
(b) inqanaba elikulo igqwetha elo namava elinawo, ngendlela eqqitywe kulo Mthetho; 45
(c) ubungakanani bomsebenzi ekufuneka wenziwe, nexesha elithathwa nguloo msebenzi; kwaye
(d) nendlela loo msebenzi ozichaphazela ngayo iimali.

(3) Kungakhathaliseki ukuba uthini na omnye umthetho otsho okuchasene noku, akukho nto kwesi siqendu ethintela umntu owenzelwa umsebenzi ligqwetha ingumsebenzi ongenguwo owokumangalelana, ukungena kwisivumelwano esibhaliweyo negqwetha elo sokuba uza kulihlawula imali engaphezu okanye engezantsi kwaleyo iqingqiweyo kwimiyinge ngokwesi siqendu. 50

(a) indlela yokujongana neemeko ezibangela ukungakwazi kwabantu abaninzi ukuzifikelela iimali ezifunwa ngamagqwetha;
