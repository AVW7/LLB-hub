# Legal Practice Act Rules

Full text: [[Legal Practice Act Rules.pdf/markdown.md|Legal Practice Act Rules — full text]]

Final Rules made under s95(1), s95(3) and s109(2) of the Legal Practice Act 28 of 2014, published by the National Forum on the Legal Profession, Government Gazette No. 41781, 20 July 2018. These flesh out the mechanics of the Act — how fees are calculated, how the Council and Provincial Councils operate, how disciplinary hearings run, how the Fidelity Fund and trust accounts are administered.

## Frequently referenced provisions

> [!quote] r38 — Procedure to be followed by disciplinary bodies
> Sets out the detailed step-by-step disciplinary process that gives effect to s38 of the Act (see [[Legal Practice Act 28 of 2014]]), continuing through r39 (commencement of enquiry into alleged misconduct), r40 (investigation), r41 (disciplinary procedure itself), r42 (subpoena power), r43 (sanctions after a hearing) and r44 (appeal against the finding of an investigating or disciplinary committee).

Relevant to PLE topic notes on the professional misconduct/disciplinary process — read together with LPA s36–s44.

> [!quote] r54.14.14 — Withdrawals from trust banking account
> "Withdrawals from a firm's trust banking account shall be made only: ... to or for a trust creditor; or ... as transfers to the firm's business banking account, provided that such transfers shall be made only in respect of money due to the firm; and provided that no transfer ... is made in respect of any disbursement (including counsel's fees) or fees of the firm unless [the disbursement has actually been made and debited, a contractual obligation to pay has arisen, or the fees/disbursement have been correctly debited in the accounting records]."

> [!quote] r54.14.15 — Payments from trust banking account
> "Any cheque drawn on a firm's trust banking account shall be made payable to or to the order of a payee specifically designated. Payments from the trust banking account of a firm shall only be by cheque or electronic transfer. No withdrawals from the trust banking account of a firm may be made by way of cellular and telephone transacting."

> [!quote] r54.14.16 — Interest accrued on trust banking account
> Interest on money held under s86(5) of the Act must be paid over to the Fund (or its nominee) by set annual/monthly deadlines, unless the practitioner has been granted an exemption.

These trust-accounting mechanics (r54) are the practical companion to LPA s86 (trust accounts) — see [[Legal Practice Act 28 of 2014]] — and are relevant to PLE and PCT topics on handling client trust money and the consequences of mishandling it.

> [!quote] r47 / r48 — Fidelity Fund certificates
> Set out the application procedure and contributions payable by practitioners applying for a Fidelity Fund certificate — the certificate a practitioner must hold under LPA s84(1) before receiving or holding client funds.

Relevant to the same PLE/PCT trust-account and client-protection topics as LPA s84–s86.
