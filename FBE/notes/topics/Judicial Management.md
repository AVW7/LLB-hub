# Judicial Management

A rescue procedure for a company in financial distress, distinct from liquidation.

## Sequence of events

1. An interested party applies to court to have the company placed under judicial management.
2. The court grants a **provisional judicial management order**.
3. The **Master** appoints a **provisional judicial manager** and convenes separate meetings of **creditors, members, and debenture holders** (if any).
4. The provisional judicial manager **takes over management** of the company.
5. At those meetings, the provisional judicial manager **reports fully** on the company's financial situation and future prospects.
6. If a **final** judicial management order is granted, the court makes an order **vesting management in the final judicial manager**, subject to the supervision of the court.
7. The final judicial manager takes over management and, among other things, **investigates the company's affairs** in the same manner as a liquidator would.
8. When surplus funds become available, the manager **distributes these among creditors**.
9. In due course, the judicial manager may apply either for **cancellation** of the judicial management order, or for the **winding-up** of the company.

## Disadvantage

A judicial management order **affects the company's credit worthiness detrimentally — even if the order is later set aside.**
