# Pre-Incorporation Contracts

A contract concluded on behalf of a company that has not yet been incorporated / is still to be formed.

## Requirements for the contract to bind the company

1. The contract must be **in writing**.
2. It must be **concluded by a person who indicates that they are acting as an agent or trustee** for a company to be formed (entered into in the name of, or on behalf of, a company still to be formed).
3. The **Memorandum of Association** of the company must, at the time of registration, incorporate as one of its objects the **adoption or ratification** of the contract.
4. **Two copies** of the contract, one duly **certified by a notary**, must be lodged with the application to register the memorandum and articles of association.
5. The company must, **after incorporation, adopt and/or ratify** the contract.
6. The company must be **entitled to commence business**.

## Consequence of non-ratification

If the contract is not ratified, is only partially ratified by the company, or the company is never formed, the person who signed as agent/trustee is **fully personally liable** in terms of the contract.

## Protecting the other party

If acting for the party contracting *with* the not-yet-formed company (not for the company itself), include a clause along these lines:

> "Should the contract not be ratified, or be only partially ratified by the company, or should the company not be formed, then the signatory is fully liable in terms of the contract."
