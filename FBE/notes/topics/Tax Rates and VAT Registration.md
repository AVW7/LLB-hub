# Tax Rates and VAT Registration

True/false and short-answer staples across nearly every FBE paper.

## Income tax rates by entity (per FBE 2010-05-22 Q3)

| Entity | Tax rate |
|---|---|
| Close Corporation | **28%** |
| Private Individual (incl. sole proprietor) | **18–40%** (sliding scale) |
| Business Trust | **40%** |
| Company | **28%** |

- A **Partnership is not itself a taxpayer** for income tax — profits are taxed in the hands of the individual partners. See [[Partnerships]].
- A **sole proprietor is NOT taxed at a flat rate of 40%** — this exact false statement recurs in [[FBE 2012-05-16 Supp]] Q3.3 and [[FBE 2012-06-28 Special Supp]] Q6; sole proprietors are taxed as individuals on the sliding scale. See [[Sole Proprietorships]].
- True/false trap: "The rate of tax of a trust is 29% for the year ending 29 February 2008" → **False** — per [[FBE 2009-12-03 Special Supp]] Q5.4. The memo doesn't supply the correct historical figure, only that 29% is wrong; the rate to know is the current **40%**.

## VAT registration

- **Who:** any person (legal or juristic) operating a business.
- **When:** once the business's turnover exceeds the registration threshold.
- **Threshold changed over the period covered by the archive:**
  - **R300,000 per annum** — per the 2009 and 2010 papers ([[FBE 2009-12-03 Special Supp]] Q4, [[FBE 2010-11-16 Supp]] Q4.1).
  - **R1,000,000 per annum** — per the 2012 paper ([[FBE 2012-06-28 Special Supp]] Q4).
  - Know which figure applies to the sitting/year in front of you rather than assuming one number.

## Input tax vs output tax

- **Input tax** = the VAT a vendor **pays** on goods/services **acquired** for use in the enterprise.
- **Output tax** = the VAT a vendor **charges** on goods/services **supplied** (sold) by them.
- FBE 2010-05-22 Q4(b)–(c) tests the two definitions **swapped** — both statements as given are **false**, because each describes the other tax. Don't let the swap catch you out.

## Capital Gains Tax

- CGT has been levied on capital gains **from 1 April 2001** — true.
- On disposal of a **primary residence**, any gain or loss must be **disregarded** if the proceeds do not exceed **R2,000,000** — true (per [[FBE 2012-05-16 Supp]] Q3.2).
