## Q1 — Three characteristics each (6)

> [!question] Question
> Name 3 characteristics of: (a) Close Corporation (3) (b) Partnership (3) (c) Sole Proprietor (3)

> [!success]- Answer
> **(a) Close Corporation:** legal personality separate from its members; establishment/administration/operation subject to a minimum number of formalities; a single person can establish a CC and the object need not be to make a profit.
> **(b) Partnership:** the object of a partnership must be to make a profit; each partner can bind the partnership; each partner carries the full risk of the enterprise in the event of failure.
> **(c) Sole Proprietor:** can be terminated easily without formality; owner has the sole right to all the profits of the enterprise; credit worthiness of the sole proprietor is linked to that of the owner.

## Q2 — Pre-incorporation contract requirements (6)

> [!question] Question
> What are the requirements that need to be complied with in order for a pre-incorporation contract to be binding on the company?

> [!success]- Answer
> 1. The contract must be in writing.
> 2. It must have been concluded by a person who indicates that they are acting as an agent or trustee for a company to be formed.
> 3. The memorandum of association of the company must, at the time of registration, incorporate as one of its objects the adoption or ratification of the contract.
> 4. The company must, after incorporation, adopt and/or ratify the contract.
> 5. The company must be entitled to commence business.
> 6. Two copies of the contract, one certified by a notary, must be lodged with the application to register the memorandum and articles of association.

Related: [[Pre-Incorporation Contracts]]

## Q3 — Widely held company (3)

> [!question] Question
> In terms of the amendment Act a Company is a widely held company if….

> [!success]- Answer
> (i) Its articles provide for an unrestricted transfer of its shares; (ii) it is permitted by its articles to offer shares to the public; (iii) it decides by special resolution to be a widely held company.

Related: [[Private Companies]]

## Q4 — Yes/no (2)

> [!question] Question
> (a) A company loses its juristic personality and its incorporated status on deregistration?
> (b) A disadvantage of judicial management is that a judicial management order affects the credit worthiness of a company detrimentally, even if the judicial management order is set aside?

> [!success]- Answer
> (a) Yes. (b) Yes.

## Q5 — True/false (5)

> [!question] Question
> (a) A partnership can be formed for charitable purposes as a non-profit organization.
> (b) A sole proprietor cannot operate more than one business.
> (c) The members interest in a CC is expressed in percentages.
> (d) A writ can be used to attach the members interest and loan account of a CC.
> (e) A trust can be established as an inter vivos trust for the benefit of the beneficiaries.

> [!success]- Answer
> (a) False. (b) False. (c) True. (d) True. (e) True.
