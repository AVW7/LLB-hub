## Q1 — True/false (5)

> [!question] Question
> (a) Shareholders are entitled to conclude contracts on behalf of a Company. (1)
> (b) The owner carries the entire risk of a sole proprietorship. (1)
> (c) A Close Corporation cannot be a Member of a Company. (1)
> (d) A Special Resolution must be passed on a simple majority vote of the Shareholders who are present or represented at that meeting. (1)

## Q2 — Advice on business enterprise for a highly profitable, high-risk venture (6)

> [!question] Question
> A, B and C, who are good friends, wish to establish a business for the marketing of clothing products. They envisage that the business venture will be highly profitable, but that considerable financial risks will be entailed. Advise the parties which form of business enterprise should be used, and motivate your answer.

> [!success]- Answer
> **A Close Corporation** (2 marks), plus any four of:
> - A, B and C qualify as natural persons not exceeding 10 (ten) Members.
> - Cheap to incorporate.
> - Cheap to operate, as no audit required.
> - The complex management requirements of a company are not required, as only three Members are involved.
> - Limited liability of the Members.
> - Perpetual succession.
> - Favourable tax treatment whilst the Close Corporation qualifies as a Small Business Corporation.

## Q4 — VAT threshold, CC registration steps, s21 company membership (7)

> [!question] Question
> 4.1 When would a business enterprise be obliged to register as a VAT vendor? (2)
> 4.2 What steps would you follow to register a Close Corporation? (4)
> 4.3 Why may a Section 21 Company have more than 50 members? Motivate briefly. (1)

> [!success]- Answer
> 4.1 When it generates a turnover of more than **R300,000.00 per annum**.
> 4.2 The following documents must be lodged with the Registrar of Close Corporations:
> - **CK7** — name reservation of the entity to be registered, stamped with a R50.00 revenue stamp.
> - Once the name has been approved, a **CK1** containing the names of the members and all relevant details of the CC must be lodged, stamped with a R100.00 revenue stamp.
> - Together with the CK1, a letter in which the accounting officer accepts his position as such must be lodged.
> 4.3 Because a s21 company is, for the purposes of the Act, regarded as a **Public Company**.

## Q5 — Financial assistance for a share purchase (5)

> [!question] Question
> Your client wishes to purchase the Shares in Bikes (Pty) Limited, which operates a business for the sale and repair of bicycles. It is proposed that the purchase price of the Shares will be financed by way of a Bank loan to be obtained by Bikes (Pty) Limited against the security of a Notarial Bond which will be registered over its assets.
> (a) Is the above transaction permissible? (motivate your answer) (3)
> (b) Would your answer be different if the client had sufficient funds to pay the purchase price of the Shares, and that the purpose of the Bank loan was to repay the Seller's Loan Account to the Company? (2)

> [!success]- Answer
> (a) **No.** Section 38 of the Companies Act prohibits a company from giving direct or indirect financial assistance for the purpose of or in connection with the purchase of any Shares in the Company.
> (b) **Yes.** The repayment of the existing Shareholder's Loan Account does not constitute financial assistance relating to the sale of the Company's Shares.
