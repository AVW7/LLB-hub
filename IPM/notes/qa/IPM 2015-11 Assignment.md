## Q1 — Monthly start-up expenses (10)

> [!question] Question
> You are recently qualified and have decided to open a practice as a Sole Proprietorship. You approached the Bank for a loan and they granted you R200,000-00. You are aware it will be some time before you start earning an income, so you have decided to use R50,000-00 to set up your practice (furniture, equipment, etc.) and break down the balance over 12 months (R12,500-00 per month) to utilize towards your expenses. Set out (what you deem to be) your necessary and urgent expenses per month.

> [!success]- Answer
> Salaries (receptionist) — R3,500; Rental & Lights — R3,500; Telephone (landline & cellular) — R1,500; Stationery — R500; Petty cash (tea/coffee, postage stamps etc.) — R500; Travelling costs — R500; Insurance on equipment — R350; Accountant's fee (to write up books) — R1,000; Bank charges & interest on overdraft — R650; Advertising — R500. **Total: R12,500.**

## Q2 — SWOT analysis: importance, elements, self-application (15)

> [!question] Question
> 2.1 Explain the importance of a SWOT analysis and set out the elements of same (5). 2.2 Explain each element of the SWOT Analysis and, using your own attributes or characteristics, use two examples each to explain how this would apply to you (10).

> [!success]- Answer
> **2.1** SWOT = Strengths, Weaknesses, Opportunities, Threats (4). It assists in the preparation of your strategic plan, i.e. your long-term and short-term goal(s) (1).
> **2.2** **Strengths** — personal factors that give your practice the edge, e.g. your academic qualification; being single with no family commitments (2½). **Weaknesses** — factors inherent to you where your competition has the edge, e.g. your lack of experience; having specialised in a field difficult to break into (2½). **Opportunities** — outside-environment factors with a positive effect, e.g. being situated next to court; setting up in an area with few attorneys (2½). **Threats** — outside-environment factors with a negative effect, e.g. setting up in a saturated CBD (e.g. Durban); having specialised in Estate Law when banks now offer that service too (2½).
