## Q1 — Draft a 6-month start-up budget (25)

> [!question] Question
> You and your long-time university friend have decided to open up a practice in 2013. You have approached ABC bank for a start-up loan of R30,000.00 to cover some of the costs required, including office furniture, rental deposit, and stationery. To limit your capital expenditure you have decided that you will rent the fax machine, a photocopier and 1 computer. The bank has requested that, as part of the financial statements required to assess your application, you submit the budget for the first six months of your practice. The bank wants to see that you will be able to repay the loan at the rate of R3,000.00 per month. Prepare the budget for the first six months. You are required to provide the basis of how you have calculated the fee income.

> [!success]- Answer
> Marking scheme: 1 mark for each budget item, 2 marks for explanation of the calculation of budgeted income, 4 marks for the correct calculation, 2 marks for correct presentation/format.
>
> Budget line items expected: Fee income (fees charged per day × number of days); Office rent; Salaries; Telephone & fax; Electricity and water; Rental of fax machine; Rental of PC; Rental of photocopier; Insurance; Postage; Stationery (files, pens, printing paper, envelopes etc); Other office supplies (refreshments & cleaning material); Travelling/vehicle expenses; Advertising; Loan repayment (R3,000.00); Bank charges; and an excess/breakeven figure.
