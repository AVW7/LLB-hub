## Q1 — 5 components of emotional intelligence (5)

> [!question] Question
> List and briefly describe the 5 components of emotional intelligence.

> [!success]- Answer
> **Self awareness** — the ability to understand one's moods, emotions, abilities and drives, as well as how they impact on others.
> **Self regulation** — the ability to control or restrain yourself.
> **Motivation** — passion for one's work and eagerness to pursue goals with energy and persistence.
> **Empathy** — the ability to place yourself in the position of other people and treat people according to their emotional reactions.
> **Social skill** — the ability to build and manage relationships.

Related: [[Emotional Intelligence]]

## Q2 — 5 Ps of marketing (5)

> [!question] Question
> List and briefly describe the 5 P's of marketing.

> [!success]- Answer
> **Product** — the quality of the service offered by your firm.
> **Price** — your firm's fee structure being in line with the recommended or applicable tariffs, and whether it is in line with your firm's objectives.
> **Place** — the location of your practice: whether it is accessible and convenient.
> **Promotion** — ways and means of promoting your services.
> **People** — the importance of employing competent staff, as well as looking well after clients.

Related: [[Marketing Mix]]

## Q3 — Statutory requirements to open a practice (5)

> [!question] Question
> You and Mildred Baloyi have been friends since attending university and have just been admitted as attorneys, deciding to open your practice under the name "Baloyi and Partners Inc." Name five statutory requirements you will have to comply with before you can open a practice.

> [!success]- Answer
> Any five of: admission as an attorney and enrolment with the relevant Law Society; notify the Law Society of intention to practise (incl. trust bank account details, postal and physical address, fidelity fund certificate, auditor's certificate); register as a VAT vendor; register as an employer for income tax purposes; register with the Workmen's Compensation Commissioner; register for UIF purposes; register with a local Metropolitan/Regional Council for levies where applicable.

Related: [[Statutory Requirements to Open a Practice]]

## Q4 — Four fundamental management actions (4)

> [!question] Question
> What four actions would you regard as fundamental to the effective management of a legal practice?

> [!success]- Answer
> Planning; Organising; Guidance; Control.

Related: [[The Management Process]]

## Q5 — Defining management (5)

> [!question] Question
> How would one best define management?

> [!success]- Answer
> A process or range of activities; that provide direction; to achieve the goals of the firm; within the firm's environment; in the most productive way.

Related: [[The Management Process]]

## Q6 — Reporting a large cash payment (1)

> [!question] Question
> A client pays you a deposit of R30,000.00 in cash. To whom must you report the cash payment you received?

> [!success]- Answer
> The Financial Intelligence Centre.
