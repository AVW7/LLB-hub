## Q1 — Business plan: importance and elements (11)

> [!question] Question
> Mr Peter Pan and Miss Olive Oil have just been admitted to practice and intend to commence practice for their own account in Durban. They need to draw up a business plan. (a) Why is it important to draft a BUSINESS PLAN? (2) (b) Set out the main elements of the BUSINESS PLAN and briefly indicate what would be dealt with under each heading. (9)

> [!success]- Answer
> **(a)** Commits and focuses thinking; reminder of personal objectives; budget planning; assessing finance.
> **(b)** Heading and introduction; brief description of business; personal objectives; business structure; resources plan; financial plan; strategic plan; operational plan; client care plan (each with a brief explanation of what it deals with).

Related: [[The Business Plan]]

## Q2 — Client liaison and complaint handling (5)

> [!question] Question
> Promoting your practice is a public relations exercise. Briefly describe the following EXTERNAL PUBLIC RELATIONS TOOLS. (a) Client Liaison (mention 8 factors) (4). (b) Complaint handling (1).

> [!success]- Answer
> **(a)** Any 8 of: being aware of your client's needs and expectations as perceived by them; keeping your client informed of progress even if nothing is happening; tell them and tell them why; returning all phone calls promptly; ensuring your telephonist answers incoming calls promptly; ensuring your telephonist has a friendly and helpful manner; ensuring clients are received in a friendly and sympathetic manner; being nice to your clients — make them feel important; going the extra mile; punctuality is essential; discussing fees frankly with your client.
> **(b)** Deal with the complaint immediately and firmly; if you or your office were at fault, apologise politely but firmly.

Related: [[Client Liaison and Complaint Handling]]

## Q3 — Where an attorney's files should be kept (3)

> [!question] Question
> Prescription is a major risk facing practitioners. A good filing system is an essential component of avoiding prescription. An attorney's files should be in one of three places. Name these places and their functions.

> [!success]- Answer
> In the filing cabinet; on your desk — if working on the matter; or on your secretary's desk — for typing etc.

Related: [[Filing Systems and Preventing Prescription]]

## Q4 — Trust surplus report and mail instructions (4)

> [!question] Question
> (a) Briefly explain what is a TRUST SURPLUS REPORT (2). (b) After employing your secretary, you need to advise him/her on certain administrative procedures. What instructions would you give your secretary in respect of Incoming Mail? (2)

> [!success]- Answer
> **(a)** It indicates the legitimate amount available for transfer from the client's trust account to the client's business account. It ensures that monies do not accumulate in the trust account — trust surplus should be as small as possible.
> **(b)** Date-stamp all incoming mail; record monies receipted; sort and distribute mail; confidential mail to remain unopened.

Related: [[Financial Reports in Practice]], [[Office Administration and HR]]

## Q5 — Budget vs Cash Flow Projection (2)

> [!question] Question
> What is the difference between the Budget of a practice and its Cash Flow Projection?

> [!success]- Answer
> A Cash Flow Projection relates to the inward or outward movement of cash in a practice (or the business bank account). A Budget relates to profit or income and expense.
