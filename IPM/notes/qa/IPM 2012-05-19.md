## Q1 — Fundamental management actions (8)

> [!question] Question
> List and briefly explain four fundamental management actions which are part of the management process.

> [!success]- Answer
> **Planning** — identifying ways to achieve your goals and the resources required.
> **Organizing** — mobilizing the people, systems and infrastructure to do what they are supposed to be doing.
> **Leading** — providing direction and motivating your people to ensure their actions are focused on achieving the firm's goals.
> **Control** — making sure goals are achieved and making adjustments in case of any deviation; management must review goals on a continuous basis.

Related: [[The Management Process]]

## Q2 — SWOT analysis elements (8)

> [!question] Question
> List and briefly describe the elements of a SWOT analysis.

> [!success]- Answer
> **Strengths** — personal factors giving your practice an advantage, e.g. qualifications, experience, expertise.
> **Weaknesses** — personal factors working to your disadvantage, e.g. lack of experience, size of your firm.
> **Opportunities** — outside-environment factors with a positive effect, e.g. demand for your services, target market, growth opportunities.
> **Threats** — outside-environment factors with a negative effect, e.g. competition, the economic climate — factors you must plan to counter.

Related: [[SWOT Analysis]]

## Q3 — Financial reports and their significance (8)

> [!question] Question
> Briefly discuss the following reports and statements and their significance: 3.1 Income Statement; 3.2 Balance Sheet; 3.3 Cash Flow statement; 3.4 Debtor's age analysis report.

> [!success]- Answer
> **3.1 Income Statement** — sets out the performance of the practice for the specified accounting period/year, detailing income and expenses, showing a profit or loss for the period.
> **3.2 Balance Sheet** — sets out the financial position of the practice at a particular point, containing the assets, liabilities and practitioner's equity in the practice; determines the financial strength of the business.
> **3.3 Cash Flow statement** — shows the inflow and outflow of cash for a particular period, indicating a positive or negative bank balance; important to determine liquidity/ensure sufficient cash to pay day-to-day expenses.
> **3.4 Debtor's age analysis report** — a listing of each debtor's account balance aged according to the month the debt arose; shows total amounts outstanding per month; important for management of cash flow.

Related: [[Financial Reports in Practice]]

## Q4 — Whole life vs term insurance (1)

> [!question] Question
> What is the difference between whole life insurance and term insurance?

> [!success]- Answer
> Whole life insurance undertakes to pay the sum insured on death of the life insured, whereas term insurance undertakes to pay the sum insured only if the life insured dies within a specified period or term. Term insurance has no surrender value.
