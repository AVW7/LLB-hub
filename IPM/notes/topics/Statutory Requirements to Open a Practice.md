# Statutory Requirements to Open a Practice

A "name five statutory requirements" question, asked with different fact-pattern wrappers (friends admitted as attorneys deciding to open a firm together).

## The requirements (any five typically required)

1. Admission as an attorney and enrolment with the relevant Law Society
2. Notify the Law Society of your intention to practise — including trust bank account details, postal and physical address, fidelity fund certificate, and auditor's certificate
3. Register as a VAT vendor
4. Register as an employer for income tax purposes
5. Register with the Workmen's Compensation Commissioner
6. Register for Unemployment Insurance Fund (UIF) purposes
7. Register with the local Metropolitan/Regional Council for levies, where applicable

## Practice notes

- Only five are required for full marks (one mark each) even though seven are listed in the source memo — pick any five, but #1 and #2 are the two most fundamental and worth leading with.
- This overlaps with, but is distinct from, [[The Business Plan]] — statutory registration is a compliance checklist; the business plan is the broader strategic document.
