# FICA Reporting Obligations

A single but recurring-style fact pattern: a client pays a large cash deposit, and you must know who receives the report.

## The rule

Cash payments received by a practitioner above the reportable threshold must be reported to the **Financial Intelligence Centre** (under the Financial Intelligence Centre Act).

## Practice notes

- Only tested once in this archive, worth a single mark — but it's a "know it cold" fact, not something to reason out under time pressure.
- Distinguish from the practice's own [[Statutory Requirements to Open a Practice]] — FICA reporting is an ongoing compliance obligation once trading, not a one-off registration step.
