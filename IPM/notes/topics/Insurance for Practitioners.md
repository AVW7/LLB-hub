# Insurance for Practitioners

Two distinct angles tested: the mechanics of life insurance products, and the three underlying "needs" that insurance is bought to satisfy.

## Whole life vs term insurance

- **Whole life insurance** — undertakes to pay the sum insured on the death of the life insured (whenever that occurs).
- **Term insurance** — undertakes to pay the sum insured only if the life insured dies within a specified period/term. Term insurance has **no surrender value**.

## The three basic insurance needs

| Need | Description | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Permanent need** | To meet certain obligations in the event of death | Paying estate liabilities, making a bequest |
| **Temporary need** | An obligation that should be met at the expiry of a certain period of time, or at death prior to that period | Purchasing a partner's interest when they retire in 10 years' time — but also if the partner dies before then |
| **Future need** | An obligation to be met at some stage in the future | Making provision for a child's university studies |
