# Filing Systems and Preventing Prescription

Prescription is flagged in the source papers as a major risk facing practitioners, and a good filing system is presented as the essential control against it.

## The three places a file should be

1. In the filing cabinet
2. On your desk — if you are actively working on the matter
3. On your secretary's desk — for typing etc.

If a file isn't in one of these three places, it's effectively lost — which is how prescription risk creeps in.

## Ways to prevent prescription (4 required)

The archive's orphan 2019 paper asks for four ways to prevent prescription without supplying a memo — reason from the filing-system principle and general practice management diligence (e.g. a reliable diary/reminder system, regular file reviews, a robust filing system as above, prompt attention to new instructions).
