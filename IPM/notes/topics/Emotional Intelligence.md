# Emotional Intelligence

Tested both as a pure "list and describe" question and as an applied scenario (managing an employee with a conduct problem).

## The 5 components

| Component | Description |
|---|---|
| **Self-awareness** | The ability to recognise and understand your own moods, emotions, abilities and drives, and how they affect others. |
| **Self-regulation** | The ability to control or redirect disruptive impulses and moods — the propensity to suspend judgment and think before acting. |
| **Motivation** | A passion for your work and eagerness to pursue goals with energy and persistence, for reasons that go beyond money or status. |
| **Empathy** | The ability to place yourself in the position of other people and understand/treat them according to their emotional reactions. |
| **Social skill** | Proficiency in managing relationships and building rapport/networks; finding common ground with others. |

## Practice notes

- Marking is 1 mark for naming the component + 1 mark for the description (2 marks each in the fuller memo versions) — always give both the name **and** a one-line explanation, never just a list.
- In an applied scenario (e.g. "address unprofessional conduct with an employee"), work through all 5 components in turn rather than picking one — the marks are spread evenly across all five.
