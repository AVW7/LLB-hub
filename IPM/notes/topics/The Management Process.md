# The Management Process

The most frequently tested single topic in the module — some form of "define management" or "list the fundamental management actions" appears in more than half the papers in this archive.

## Definition of management

> A process or range of activities that provide the necessary direction to a firm's resources in order to achieve the goals of the firm within the firm's environment, in the most productive way.

Marks are awarded per element of this definition:
1. A process or range of activities
2. That provide direction
3. To achieve the goals of the firm
4. Within the firm's environment
5. In the most productive way

## The four fundamental management actions

Two memos in the archive use slightly different labels for the third action — know both:

| Action | Description |
|---|---|
| **Planning** | Identifying ways to achieve your goals and the resources required |
| **Organising** | Mobilising the people, systems and infrastructure to do what they are supposed to be doing |
| **Leading** (also called **Guidance**) | Providing direction and motivating your people to ensure their actions are focused on achieving the firm's goals |
| **Control** | Making sure goals are achieved and making adjustments in case of any deviation — management must review goals on a continuous basis |

## Practice notes

- Marking is generous: typically 1 mark for naming the action, 1 mark for a one-line explanation.
- If the question only asks you to "list" the actions (no marks for explanation), just name all four — don't spend time writing definitions.
