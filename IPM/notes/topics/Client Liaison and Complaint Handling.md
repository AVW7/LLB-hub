# Client Liaison and Complaint Handling

Framed in the source papers as the two **external public relations tools** for promoting a practice.

## Client liaison — factors (memo lists 11; questions typically ask for 6–8)

1. Being aware of your client's needs and expectations as perceived by them
2. Keeping your client informed of progress in their matter, even if nothing is happening
3. Tell them, and tell them why
4. Returning all phone calls promptly
5. Ensuring your telephonist answers incoming calls promptly
6. Ensuring your telephonist has a friendly and helpful manner
7. Ensuring clients are received in a friendly and sympathetic manner
8. Being nice to your clients — make them feel important
9. Going the extra mile — doing more than is basically necessary
10. Punctuality is essential
11. Discussing fees frankly with your client

## Complaint handling

1. Deal with the complaint immediately and firmly
2. If you or your office were at fault, apologise politely but firmly

## Practice notes

- Questions specify how many factors to mention (6 or 8) — read the mark allocation carefully; each factor is typically worth ½ mark.
- Complaint handling is consistently a small, separate sub-question (1 mark) tacked onto client liaison — don't skip it.
