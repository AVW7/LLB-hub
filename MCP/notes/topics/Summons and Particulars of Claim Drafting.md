# Summons and Particulars of Claim Drafting

The single most heavily drilled MCP skill — a full or partial particulars of claim (POC) appears in every past paper in this archive.

## Forms of proceedings

Three forms exist in the Magistrate's Court: **action** proceedings (by summons), **application** proceedings (by motion), and **provisional sentence** proceedings.

- **Simple summons** (Rule 5(2)(b), Form 2) — used where the claim is for a debt or a liquidated demand.
- **Combined summons** (Form 2B) — used in every other case; a statement of material facts (the particulars of claim) complying with Rule 6 must be annexed.
- Rule 5 (summons), Rule 6 (pleadings generally), Rule 7 (amendment of summons before service) govern content; non-compliance can render the summons an irregular proceeding, liable to be set aside (Rules 5(11), 6(13)).
- Summons is served by the **Sheriff** (Rule 9).

## Standard POC skeleton (drilled repeatedly)

1. Identify the **Plaintiff** — description, occupation, address.
2. Identify the **Defendant(s)** similarly; for a juristic person, plead incorporation/registration and registered/principal place of business.
3. Plead the **material terms** of any agreement, and annex a copy.
4. Plead the **breach**/facts giving rise to the claim.
5. Plead the **damages**/amount owing.
6. Plead any **suretyship** — bind the surety jointly and severally with the principal debtor, "the one paying the other to be absolved," and annex the deed.
7. Plead **demand** where it's a required element (see [[Demand Prior to Litigation]]).
8. Prayer: payment + interest (a tempore morae, or from date of service) + costs.

## Recurring fact patterns

- **Suretyship loan** — a CC borrows on an oral agreement; the sole member signs a written Deed of Suretyship as surety and co-principal debtor on the same date. Appears near-verbatim in [[MCP 2010-09-07 Supp]] and [[MCP 2012-08-30 Supp]] ("A. Brown / Red Leather CC").
- **Dishonoured cheque** — plead the cheque (holder, presentment, dishonour marked "Refer to drawer"), dispense with notice of dishonour under **s48(2)(c) Bills of Exchange Act 34 of 1964**, attach the cheque as an annexure. The memo expressly penalises mixing the cheque cause of action with the underlying causa (services rendered) in the same set of particulars — plead one or the other, not both. [[MCP 2010-03-06]] Q2.1
- **Building contract + surety** — breach of a phased-payment construction contract, cover the shortfall by completing with another contractor, claim the difference; again with a co-principal-debtor surety. [[MCP 2012 Special Supp]] Q5
- **Organ of state MVA claim** — precede the claim with a s3 Institution of Legal Proceedings against Certain Organs of State Act 40 of 2002 notice to the Minister. [[MCP 2010-03-06]] Q1
