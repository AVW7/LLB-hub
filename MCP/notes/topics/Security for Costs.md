# Security for Costs

A defendant may request security for costs from a plaintiff who is a **peregrinus** (outside the court's/country's jurisdiction, e.g. resident abroad).

## Timing and mechanics

- The request must be made **before close of pleadings**. [[MCP 2010-12-15 Special Supp]] Q3.1
- If the plaintiff is a **close corporation** and is met with a counterclaim, the CC (now a plaintiff in reconvention) generally cannot be made to give security merely because it's a CC — security under the Close Corporations Act can only be sought from a **plaintiff in convention**, and the party seeking it against a CC must satisfy the court there is good reason. [[MCP 2010-12-15 Special Supp]] Q3.2
- If security is requested and not furnished: apply to court under **Rule 62(2)** for an order dismissing the plaintiff's action with costs, alternatively staying proceedings until security is furnished, plus costs of the application.
- Where the plaintiff is a foreign peregrinus (e.g. resident in Zimbabwe), the defendant may seek security for costs to protect his ability to recover costs if successful. [[MCP 2010-09-07 Supp]] Q2.3, [[MCP 2012-08-30 Supp]] Q2.3
