# Notice of Bar

## When it applies

- A defendant fails to deliver a plea within the required time: deliver a **Notice of Bar** requiring the plea within **5 days**, failing which the defendant is *ipso facto* barred (alternatively, the notice can warn that the plaintiff will apply for default judgment without further notice to the defendant). If a conventional warning/further indulgence is first required, give that first. [[MCP 2010-12-15 Special Supp]] Q1c
- Same mechanism where a defendant, having entered an appearance to defend, then takes **no further step** for an extended period (two months, in the recurring fact pattern) — the plaintiff's remedy is again a Notice of Bar. [[MCP 2012 Special Supp]] Q2

## Standard notice content

Addressed to the Clerk of the Court and the defendant's attorneys; states the defendant must file the outstanding step within 5 days of receipt, failing which barred/default judgment will follow; dated and signed by the plaintiff's attorneys.
