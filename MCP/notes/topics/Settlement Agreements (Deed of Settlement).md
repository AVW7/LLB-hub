# Settlement Agreements (Deed of Settlement)

Drafted after pleadings have closed, once the parties reach agreement on a compromise figure.

## Standard clauses (consistent across both papers testing this)

1. Defendant agrees to pay a settlement sum plus costs and interest.
2. Defendant pays plaintiff's taxed party-and-party costs plus attorney-and-client costs, from date of the deed until final payment.
3. Interest at the legal rate from date of the deed (on the capital) and from date of taxation (on the costs).
4. Defendant pays in stipulated **monthly instalments** starting on/before a set date.
5. **Acceleration clause** — on default, the full balance becomes immediately due and the plaintiff may proceed with execution without a further application.
6. Payments made to the plaintiff's attorneys.
7. Payments applied first to attorney-and-client costs, then taxed costs, then interest, then capital.
8. Parties ask the court to make the agreement an **order of court**.
