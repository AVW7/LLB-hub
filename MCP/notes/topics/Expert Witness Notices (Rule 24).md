# Expert Witness Notices (Rule 24)

## Rule 24(9) — expert witness

- **Rule 24(9)(a)**: notice of intention to call an expert witness, not less than **15 days** before the hearing.
- **Rule 24(9)(b)**: a summary of the expert's opinion/evidence and the reasons for it, not less than **10 days** before the hearing.
- The notice must identify the expert, their qualifications/experience, and the substance of the evidence — e.g. what was inspected, the findings, and the reasonable/necessary cost conclusion in a repair-cost scenario.

## Rule 24(10) — other evidence (e.g. photographs)

- Separate notice mechanism: notify the other party of the intention to use specified evidence (e.g. photographs) at trial, at least **10 days** before trial.
- Give the opponent the right to **inspect** the evidence.
- If the opponent does not object to authenticity within **5 days** of delivery of the notice, the evidence is admitted without further proof.
- If the opponent objects, lead evidence at trial to prove authenticity.
