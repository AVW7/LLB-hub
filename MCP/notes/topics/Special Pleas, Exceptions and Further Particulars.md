# Special Pleas, Exceptions and Further Particulars

## Special pleas (dilatory/declinatory — don't attack the merits)

Grounds recognised across the papers: lack of **jurisdiction**; **prescription**; the matter should go to **arbitration**; **misjoinder**; **non-joinder**; **lis pendens**; **res judicata**. [[MCP 2010-03-18 Supp]] Q4.4, [[MCP 2010-12-15 Special Supp]] Q4.6 and Q1d

## Exception — vague and embarrassing pleading

Where the opponent's pleading (e.g. particulars of claim) is vague and embarrassing: notify the defect and require rectification **within 10 days**; if not remedied, apply to court to strike out/dismiss. [[MCP 2010-03-06]] Q3.4, [[MCP 2010-12-15 Special Supp]] Q1e

## Further particulars

If a request for further particulars (for trial) is wrongfully ignored, apply to court to **compel** their furnishing, with costs of the application. [[MCP 2010-12-15 Special Supp]] Q1g

## Rule 21 — close of pleadings

Closing pleadings does **not** itself prevent either party from later amending pleadings already filed — the memo confirms the "closed pleadings = no further amendment" proposition is **false**. [[MCP 2010-12-15 Special Supp]] Q4.4

## Recusal

If the presiding magistrate previously convicted your client in a related criminal matter, request the magistrate to **recuse** himself from the related civil trial. [[MCP 2010-12-15 Special Supp]] Q1i

## Summons lapses

If not served within **12 months** of issue by the clerk of the court, or if the plaintiff takes no further step within **2 months** after service by the sheriff. [[MCP 2010-12-15 Special Supp]] Q4.5

## Unanswered Rule 15(1)-type notice

Apply for **dismissal** of the claim with costs. [[MCP 2010-12-15 Special Supp]] Q1j
