# Rescission of Default Judgment

Brought by **Notice of Motion**.

## Typical relief sought

1. Condonation for late filing of the application (if applicable);
2. Rescission of the default judgment;
3. Leave to defend the action;
4. Costs in the event of opposition.

## Fact pattern (worth knowing)

Default judgment is granted after summons is served on the defendant's spouse, who ignores it. The defendant only learns of the judgment once discharged from hospital; a sheriff has already executed a warrant against his property in the interim (see [[Warrants of Execution]]). Settlement negotiations are attempted and break down, then the defendant applies for rescission. [[MCP 2010-03-18 Supp]] Q3
