# Warrants of Execution

## Types (memo asks for "any 3")

1. Warrant of execution against **movables**
2. Warrant of execution against **immovables**
3. Warrant of **ejectment**
4. Warrant of **delivery**
5. Warrant in terms of **s30** (of the Magistrates' Courts Act)

## Practice note

The sheriff executes a warrant by attaching the judgment debtor's property (e.g. furniture) to satisfy a default judgment. In the recurring rescission fact pattern, the sheriff attaches furniture roughly a month after default judgment is granted, before the judgment debtor becomes aware of it and applies to rescind — see [[Rescission of Default Judgment]].
