## Q1 — Loan + suretyship POC (15)

> [!question] Question
> Client A. Brown made an oral loan of R30,000 to Red Leather CC on 25 October 2011, repayable in full by 31 January 2012 — unpaid. The CC's sole member signed a written Deed of Suretyship the same date. The surety has also not paid. Draft full particulars of claim.

> [!success]- Answer
> Same structure as [[MCP 2010-09-07 Supp]] Q1, with dates rolled forward: Plaintiff Anton Brown; First Defendant Red Leather CC; Second Defendant James Smith (surety), full particulars otherwise unknown. First Defendant indebted for R30,000, loaned 25 October 2011 under an oral agreement, due 31 January 2012. Second Defendant's Deed of Suretyship annexed as "A." Prayer: judgment against both jointly and severally, one paying the other absolved, for R30,000 + interest at 15.5% p.a. from 1 February 2012 + costs.

Related: [[Summons and Particulars of Claim Drafting]]

## Q2 — MVA POC: jurisdiction, default judgment, costs, tender, summary judgment, expert notice (25)

> [!question] Question
> [Same POC facts as [[MCP 2010-09-07 Supp]] Q2, collision date rolled forward to 1 March 2012.]
> 2.1 Which court(s) have jurisdiction and why (6)? 2.2 Documents required for Default Judgment if the Defendant fails to enter an Appearance to Defend (4)? 2.3 What can the Defendant do to protect his costs recovery if successful (2)? 2.4 The Defendant believes both parties equally to blame, has R10,000 available — what should he do to prevent further costs (4)? 2.5 Can the Plaintiff apply for Summary Judgment after an Appearance to Defend? Give reasons (3). 2.6 What notice(s) must the Plaintiff's attorneys file before trial regarding the disputed vehicle damages, and what must they contain (6)?

> [!success]- Answer
> Identical reasoning to [[MCP 2010-09-07 Supp]] Q2: **2.1** Durban (residence), Johannesburg (employment), or Cape Town (whole cause of action arose) — any one. **2.2** Original and copy Request for Default Judgment; original Return of Service; original Summons; Damages Affidavit. **2.3** Ask for **security for costs** — Plaintiff is a peregrinus. **2.4** Make a **"without prejudice" offer** of R10,000 plus costs to date. **2.5** No — the damages are an **illiquid claim**. **2.6** An **Expert Notice** at least 15 days before trial (intention to use) and at least 10 days before trial (summary of opinion/reasons); expert should confirm examination of the vehicle, damage found, and fair/reasonable repair cost.

Related: [[Jurisdiction of the Magistrate's Court]], [[Default Judgment]], [[Security for Costs]], [[Rule 14 Judgment|Summary Judgment (Rule 14)]], [[Expert Witness Notices (Rule 24)]]

## Q3 — Deed of settlement (10)

> [!question] Question
> Action for damages from an unlawful assault; pleadings closed. Defendant's attorney conveys an offer of R8,000 plus interest at the legal rate and party-and-party costs to date; acceptable to your client. Defendant will pay in monthly instalments of R500. Draft the deed of settlement (headings may be omitted).

> [!success]- Answer
> Identical deed structure to [[MCP 2010-09-07 Supp]] Q3: R8,000 plus costs and interest; taxed party-and-party costs and attorney-and-client costs from date of the deed to final payment; interest at 15.5% p.a.; R500/month instalments; acceleration clause on default entitling immediate execution without further application; payments to the Plaintiff's attorneys, applied first to attorney-and-client costs, then taxed costs, then interest, then capital; request that the Court make the agreement an order of court.

Related: [[Settlement Agreements (Deed of Settlement)]]
