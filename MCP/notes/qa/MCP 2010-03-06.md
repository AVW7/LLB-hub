## Q1 — SAPS collision, letter of demand to the Minister (12)

> [!question] Question
> Client Jacob van Heerden was stationary at a red light when a marked SAPS vehicle, driven by Sgt Jones, collided with the rear of his vehicle, causing R35,000 damage. He wants to claim the repair cost from the South African Police Services. DRAFT the necessary document(s) to complete your cause of action.

> [!success]- Answer
> A formal letter of demand to the **Minister of Safety and Security**, headed as a Notice in terms of the **Institution of Legal Proceedings against certain organs of State Act, No 40 of 2002**. It must state that the writer acts for the client, set out the date/place/vehicles/facts of the collision, allege sole negligence of Sgt Jones (acting in the course and scope of his employment with SAPS), state the police reference number, quantify the damages (R35,000 — fair and reasonable cost of repair), and record that this letter serves as the formal s3 notice of intention to recover the client's damages.

Related: [[Demand Prior to Litigation]], [[Summons and Particulars of Claim Drafting]]

## Q2 — Dishonoured cheque: POC and summary judgment (22)

> [!question] Question
> Client Mandlakazi Mpofu received a cheque from I O U (Pty) Ltd for R27,500 for services rendered. The cheque was dishonoured, marked "Refer to drawer." She wants to sue by way of Ordinary Summons.
> 2.1 Draft FULL particulars of claim (10). 2.2 The Defendant enters an appearance to defend and you believe it has no defence. 2.2.1 What step(s) would procedurally be correct (1)? 2.2.2 Draft the necessary application (11).

> [!success]- Answer
> **2.1** Particulars of claim on the cheque cause of action: Plaintiff is Mandlakazi Mpofu, adult female physiotherapist; Defendant is I O U (Pty) Ltd, a duly incorporated company; Plaintiff holds a cheque dated 10 January 2010 for R27,500 drawn by the Defendant in her favour on Standard Bank Cape Town; presented for payment 11 January 2010 and dishonoured marked "Refer to drawer"; notice of dishonour dispensed with under **s48(2)(c) Bills of Exchange Act 34 of 1964**; copy of the cheque annexed as "A." Prayer: payment of R27,500, interest at 15.5% p.a. from 11 January 2010, and costs. (Marks also awarded for pleading services-rendered instead — but note: marks are deducted for mixing the cheque cause of action with the underlying causa in the same particulars.)
>
> **2.2.1** Apply for **Summary Judgment**.
>
> **2.2.2** A drafted Application for Summary Judgment: notice that application will be made on a stated Monday at 09h00 or so soon thereafter as the matter may be heard, for an order upholding the application with costs, and judgment for the capital (R27,500), interest at 15.5% p.a. from 11 January 2010, and costs of suit — noting that the original cheque will be used in support of the application (or, if pleaded on services rendered, that the Plaintiff's supporting affidavit is attached). Addressed to the Clerk of the Court and the Respondent/Defendant's attorneys.

Related: [[Summons and Particulars of Claim Drafting]], [[Rule 14 Judgment|Summary Judgment (Rule 14)]]

## Q3 — Procedural "what would you do" drill (10)

> [!question] Question
> 3.1 You wish to call an expert witness at a trial (2). 3.2 You requested security for costs from a peregrinus Plaintiff but received no reply (2). 3.3 After litis contestatio, the Plaintiff fails to obtain a trial date timeously (2). 3.4 The Plaintiff's particulars of claim are, in your opinion, vague and embarrassing (4).

> [!success]- Answer
> **3.1** Give a **Rule 24(9)(a)** notice at least 15 days before the hearing of your intention to call the expert, and a **Rule 24(9)(b)** summary of the expert's evidence at least 10 days before the hearing.
> **3.2** Apply to **dismiss the Plaintiff's claim with costs** (no reference to compelling first is permitted — no marks if a candidate mentions compelling then dismissal).
> **3.3** The Defendant may: apply for a hearing date; apply for dismissal of the Plaintiff's case; or do nothing (any two).
> **3.4** Give notice that the particulars are to be clarified, within 10 days; if not remedied, apply to dismiss the Plaintiff's claim.

Related: [[Expert Witness Notices (Rule 24)]], [[Security for Costs]], [[Special Pleas, Exceptions and Further Particulars]]

## Q4 — Types of warrants of execution (3)

> [!question] Question
> List 3 types of warrants of execution.

> [!success]- Answer
> Any 3 of: warrant of execution against movables; warrant of execution against immovables; warrant of ejectment; warrant of delivery; warrant in terms of section 30.
