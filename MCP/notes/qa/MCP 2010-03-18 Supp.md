## Q1 — Credit sale + suretyship: demand, POC, summary judgment (22)

> [!question] Question
> Farm Supply (Pty) Ltd supplied cattle feed to Cattle CC on credit, payment due 15 days after statement, interest at 18% p.a., parties consenting to MC jurisdiction. James Lambert (sole member) signed a suretyship. R142,000 of feed was sold and delivered in December 2009; a statement sent 31 December 2009 met no response. Not covered by the National Credit Act.
> 1.1 Is a letter of demand necessary before summons? Motivate (3). 1.2 Draw the Particulars of Claim (15). 1.3 An Appearance to Defend is entered — may Farm Supply apply for Summary Judgment? Motivate (4).

> [!success]- Answer
> **1.1** No. There's no need to place the Defendant in mora — the time period for payment (15 days after receipt of statement) was already fixed by agreement.
> **1.2** POC pleads: Plaintiff (Farm Supply (Pty) Ltd, incorporated company); First Defendant (Cattle CC, close corporation); Second Defendant (James Lambert); the agreement's material terms (credit sale, MC jurisdiction consent, 15-day payment term, 18% p.a. interest) with the agreement annexed; delivery of feed to the value of R142,000; the statement sent 31 December 2009; First Defendant's failure to pay; Second Defendant's suretyship as surety and co-principal debtor (deed annexed). Prayer: judgment against First and Second Defendants jointly and severally, one paying the other absolved, for R142,000 + interest at 18% p.a. from 16 January 2010 + costs.
> **1.3** Yes — the claim is for a **liquidated amount**, one of the causes of action for which summary judgment is available (liquid document; liquidated amount; ejectment; delivery of specified movables).

## Q3 — Rescission of default judgment (10)

> [!question] Question
> James Smith buys a used Toyota Tazz from Eric Ngcobo for R60,000 with a warranty re a new gearbox. It breaks down on the way home. James refuses to pay pending repair; Eric denies the gearbox is the issue and sues. James is hospitalised; summons is served on his wife, who ignores it. Default judgment for R60,000 + interest + costs is granted on 7 August 2009; the Sheriff attaches his furniture on 10 September 2009 under a warrant of execution. Settlement negotiations later break down. On 7 February 2010, James launches an application for rescission of the default judgment. Draft the Notice of Motion (headings and affidavits may be omitted).

> [!success]- Answer
> A Notice of Motion addressed to the Clerk of the Court and the Plaintiff/Respondent's attorneys, giving notice that application will be made for an order: (1) granting condonation for the late filing of the application; (2) rescinding the default judgment granted on 7 August 2009; (3) granting the Applicant leave to defend the action; (4) costs in the event of the application being opposed.

Related: [[Rescission of Default Judgment]], [[Warrants of Execution]]

## Q4 — Subpoenas, experts, default judgment documents, special pleas (12)

> [!question] Question
> 4.1 What is a Subpoena duces tecum (2)? 4.2 What must a Plaintiff do if intending to call an expert witness at trial (3)? 4.3 The Plaintiff claims MVA damages; the Defendant has not entered an Appearance to Defend — what documents must the Plaintiff file to obtain Default Judgment (4)? 4.4 Give 3 instances where a Defendant can file a Special Plea (3).

> [!success]- Answer
> **4.1** A subpoena requiring a witness to attend court and bring specified documents.
> **4.2** Send a notice 15 days before the hearing (Rule 24(9)(a)) of the intention to call the expert, then a summary of the expert's opinion and reasons 10 days before the hearing (Rule 24(9)(b)).
> **4.3** Original Summons; original Return of Service; original and one copy of the Request for Default Judgment; Damages Affidavit.
> **4.4** Any 3 of: jurisdiction; prescription; the matter should go to arbitration; misjoinder (also: non-joinder, lis pendens, res judicata per later papers).

Related: [[Expert Witness Notices (Rule 24)]], [[Default Judgment]], [[Special Pleas, Exceptions and Further Particulars]]
