# Equality and the Harksen Test

Only one paper in this archive tests the equality clause via *Harksen v Lane* — and critically, it is an **orphan question with no surviving marking memo**.

## Core framework

The single source for this topic — [[CLP 2010-12-15 Special Supp]] — instructs candidates to "apply the criteria laid down by the Constitutional Court in *Harksen v Lane NO* 1998 (1) SA 300 (CC)" to a single-sex school admission scenario, alongside identifying which of the applicant's constitutional rights are at issue.

> [!warning] No memo in the archive for this topic
> No marking memo survives for this sitting, so the archive does not confirm which specific *Harksen* stages/elements the assessors expected, or how they were meant to be applied to these facts. Do not treat any particular formulation of the *Harksen* test as "the answer" unless you have verified it against your prescribed materials — this note deliberately does not reproduce a test that was never sourced from the hub's documents.

What the fact pattern establishes (from the question itself, not a memo):
- Ms Anna Smith seeks admission to a state boys' school; the school's stated justification is single-sex policy and facility constraints (bathrooms/change rooms).
- The applicable right in issue is most obviously **equality (s9)**, alongside access to education-type considerations — verify the exact rights against your own materials since no memo confirms the expected list.
- An NGO advocating women's rights wants to make submissions — this arm of the question is really about [[Amicus Curiae]], not equality doctrine itself.
