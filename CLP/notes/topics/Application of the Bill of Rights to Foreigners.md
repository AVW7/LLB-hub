# Application of the Bill of Rights to Foreigners

Foreign nationality is not, on its own, a bar to Bill of Rights protection — a discrete point worth remembering when a fact pattern involves a non-citizen.

## Core framework

**s7(1) Constitution:** the Bill of Rights "enshrines the rights of all people in our country" — this includes foreigners, not only citizens. Foreigners are entitled to all rights in the Bill of Rights **except those expressly reserved for citizens** (e.g. s19(3) — the right to vote).

Foreigners also retain **locus standi under s38** to approach a competent court alleging infringement — e.g. as someone acting in their own interest under s38(a). See [[Locus Standi]].
