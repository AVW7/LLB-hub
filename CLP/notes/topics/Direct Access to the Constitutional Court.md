# Direct Access to the Constitutional Court

Rule 18 direct access lets a litigant skip the ordinary court hierarchy and approach the CC as a court of first (and last) instance — but it is the exception, not the rule.

## Core framework

**s167(6)(a) of the Constitution**, read with **Rule 18** of the Constitutional Court Rules, governs when direct access may be granted. It is **subject to the leave of the Constitutional Court**, granted only when it is **in the interests of justice** to do so.

The CC's general approach: parties should ordinarily follow the usual route through the courts first — it is not ordinarily in the interests of justice for a court to sit as both a court of first and final instance. This is particularly so in common-law matters.

**Rule 18 requirements:**
1. Brought on notice of motion, supported by an affidavit setting out the facts relied on.
2. Lodged with the Registrar and served on all parties with a direct or substantial interest, setting out: (a) grounds for why direct access is in the interests of justice; (b) the nature of the relief sought and its grounds; (c) whether the matter can be decided without oral evidence and, if not, (d) how such evidence should be adduced and conflicts resolved.
3. Any party wishing to oppose must notify the applicant and Registrar within 10 days.
4. Once opposition is received (or the time for it has lapsed), the Chief Justice gives directions — which may include calling for written submissions, or indicating none are needed.
5. May be dealt with summarily without oral/written argument beyond the application itself — unless opposed, in which case it proceeds only after the subrule (4)(a) directions process.

**Rule 12 — urgent applications:** where urgency justifies it, the Chief Justice may dispense with the ordinary forms and service and give bespoke directions (as far as practicable in line with the Rules). The application must still be on notice of motion, accompanied by an affidavit explicitly setting out the circumstances justifying departure from the ordinary procedure. Rule 12 is used together with Rule 18 where direct access is sought urgently.
